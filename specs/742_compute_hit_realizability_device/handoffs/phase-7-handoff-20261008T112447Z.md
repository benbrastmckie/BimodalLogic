- PLAN DEFECT, corrected at the consumption site: the planned `hitOf` conjuncts
  `fwdLive`/`bwdLive` chain `τ`-paths that SHARE an endpoint (`G_τ := {(x, y) | τ ∈ pathTypes
  x y}`), while a `SegmentFactorization`'s segments are disjoint and joined by one `step`; the
  type of a path minus its last state is not the path's type, so `mem_hitOf_iff` over the
  planned graph is false (counterexample `#eval`-witnessed above; issue log
  `plan-defect`). `fwdLiveSep`/`bwdLiveSep` are `live` (Phase 6's generic greatest fixpoint) in
  the step-then-path graph `fwdSepGraph` and its mirror; `fwdLive`/`bwdLive` are untouched.
- `hitOf` is an image over `Q × Q`, never a filter over `Finset.univ` of the triple product of
  `KType sig k` (which has `2 ^ (3 · |NormalForm sig k 0|)` elements); candidates for
  `τneg`/`τpos` are `allPathTypes`, and `mem_hitOf_iff_live` recovers the planned filter shape.
- The splice is a generic noncomputable concatenation (`exists_concat`, by `Classical.choose`
  on `cutAt_cover`): the run is only needed existentially. Its specification is index-based
  (`ρ (cutAt z + i) = (L z)[i]`); the run hypotheses are stated with `head?`/`getLast?`/
  `getElem?` (no dependent bound proofs) so that the `if`-defined family can be rewritten by
  `hLpos`/`hL0`/`hLneg` without motive failures.
- `hk : 1 ≤ k` enters only in the ⇐ direction, twice, through `doets_lemma_1_1`: nonemptiness
  of every positive/negative segment (the plan did not name this use) and the `now` state's
  segment being the middle one (the plan's use).
- The module is new (`HitOf.lean`) rather than an extension of `Present.lean` (684 + ~800 lines
  would pass the plan's own ~900-line split trigger).
