## Key Decisions

- One closure serves both read-outs: `PathEntry` carries `hasNow : Bool` as the `now` budget, so
  `pathTypes` and `pathTypesNow` filter the same `pathClosure` on the flag; `canExtend` refuses a
  second `now`.
- `closureIter` stops at the first stationary round and is proved equal to the `Nat.iterate`
  (`closureIter_eq_iterate`); `closureBound` is arithmetic
  (`card Q * card Q * 2 * 2 ^ card (NormalForm sig k 0)`, `closureBound_eq`) because
  `Fintype.card (KType sig k)` would enumerate `2^256` functions at depth 1 — without both, no
  `#eval` would return.
- Completeness is by induction on the inductive `StepPath` (`exists_mem_iterate_of_stepPath`),
  applying `kTypeOf_listSeg_append_congr` with the stored representative; soundness is by
  induction on the iterate (`realizes_of_mem_iterate`). Stationarity is the generic
  `iterate_stabilizes` (a bounded, strictly growing measure forces a fixpoint within the bound),
  instantiated with the key-image cardinality.
- Liveness is the greatest fixpoint of a pruning step (`liveSet := pruneStep^[card Q] univ`,
  generic `live_iff` on a Boolean graph over a finite type): ⇒ chooses successors inside the
  fixpoint (`hasChain_of_mem_fixed`, `choose` + `Nat.rec`), ⇐ shows a chain survives every round;
  same stabilization lemma. `fwdLive_iff`/`bwdLive_iff` are the plan's statements verbatim; the
  plan's reachability-plus-cycle criterion was not used (recorded as an altered deviation).
- The closure's filter predicate is a conjunction under a binder that instance resolution cannot
  synthesize as a `DecidablePred` (though `fun _ => instDecidableAnd` typechecks); it is named
  (`Extendable`) with its own instance.
- Mathlib at this pin deprecates `List.Chain'` for `List.IsChain`; the list-shaped membership
  theorems use `List.IsChain` and `List.isChain_append` / `List.isChain_singleton`.
