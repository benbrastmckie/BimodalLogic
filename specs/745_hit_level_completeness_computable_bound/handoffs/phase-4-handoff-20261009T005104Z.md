## Key Decisions

1. **Two choice sites named, not one.** The plan's task item asked for a comment naming where
   the non-constructivity enters (`Set.Finite.bddAbove`). The proof has TWO: `choose!` picks a
   witnessing ray pair — hence, through `splice_classify`, a jump time — for each member of
   `HitCl`, and `Set.Finite.bddAbove` then asserts only that SOME upper bound of those offsets
   exists. Both are named in the section header; neither exhibits anything, which is why the
   `∃ L` shape is the recorded result rather than a presentational choice.

2. **`Set.Finite.image` + `BddAbove`'s `upperBounds` witness replaced the `toFinset`/`Finset.sup`
   route.** The plan listed `Set.Finite.toFinset` and `Finset.sup` among the names to re-resolve
   and predicted `Finset.sup` bookkeeping as the dominant cost. Both were re-resolved by name
   and both turned out unnecessary: `((hitCl_finite …).image off).bddAbove` yields
   `⟨L, hL : L ∈ upperBounds (off '' HitCl)⟩` directly. This is NOT a route substitution — the
   plan's own stated route is "`Set.Finite.bddAbove` on the image of the offsets to get `L`",
   which is exactly what was done; only the Scope Hypothesis's cost prediction was wrong.

3. **One declaration-scoped linter suppression, documented in the docstring.** The pinned
   signature binds `(hk : 1 ≤ k)` and the truncation argument genuinely does not use it
   (`HitCl` is defined at every depth), so `unusedVariables` warned. Renaming the binder to
   `_hk` would have altered the signature this phase's verification requires verbatim, so the
   suppression is `set_option linter.unusedVariables false in` on that one declaration, with a
   docstring paragraph saying why `hk` is carried (the pinned signature, and `HitPresented`'s
   own `hk` downstream).

4. **The `show`-then-`rw` form of `exists_splice_eq` had to be abandoned for a term proof.**
   Writing the `symm_apply_apply` goal out with `show` forced elaboration at `implicit`
   transparency and produced an application type mismatch between
   `{σ // σ.state t = stayA.state t}` and
   `Presheaf.StabFibre FA.toTaskFrame.toFibre.toTaskFrame t (stayA.state t)`, after which the
   `rw` found no pattern. `congrArg Subtype.val ((seamOmegaEquiv t (stayA.state t)).symm_apply_apply ⟨σ, h⟩)`
   compiles. Worth remembering for Phase 6, which will touch `IsRun` and may hit the same
   `toFibre.toTaskFrame` coercion wall.
