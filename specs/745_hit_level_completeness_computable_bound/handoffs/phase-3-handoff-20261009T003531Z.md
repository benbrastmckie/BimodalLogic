- **No transport lemma was needed for `splice_classify`.** `WorldHistory.path` is
  `WorldHistory.state` by definition and `seamOmegaEquiv`'s `.symm` carries the seam condition
  `σ.state t = stayA.state t` as its `.2` field, so `path_classify` applies to
  `(spliced stayA t bf).path` directly and `WorldHistory.ext_state` lifts the result. The plan's
  route (`pathFibreEquiv`/`seamOmegaEquiv` → `path_classify` → `ext_state`) was followed; no
  explicit `pathFibreEquiv` rewrite appears because the equivalence's definitional transparency
  made one unnecessary. 29 lines, green on the first attempt.
- **The closure-size figure is now re-derived, not carried.** `#eval (plusSubformulaClosure Fp).card`
  = 4 was run BEFORE the case split was written, and `PlusFormula.subformulas Fp` printed as
  `[Fp, atom p, ⊤, ⊥, ⊥]`, so the four members are exactly `untl ⊤ p`, `atom p`, `⊤`, `⊥`. With the
  marker `now` that is five letters, and `letter_cases` is the exhaustion proving the case analysis
  complete. The `#eval` is left live at the foot of that section so a reader can re-run it.
- **The assembled read-out forms avoid the distinctness lemmas entirely.** `clTrace_stayA_apply`
  and `clTrace_jump_apply` case on `letter_cases` in the forward direction and substitute via
  `rintro ... rfl` in the backward direction, so neither needs to know the letters are pairwise
  distinct. The ten distinctness lemmas (`now_ne_*`, `Fp_ne_*`, `p_ne_*`, `top_ne_bot`) are landed
  anyway and are UNUSED in this phase — they exist for phases 5-6, where `labOf`'s flag table must
  be case-split on the letter. Stated here so a later reader does not mistake them for live
  dependencies of phase 3.
- **The function-level forms are the handoff surface.** `clTrace_stayA_eq` and `clTrace_jump_eq`
  give `clTrace MA Fp t stayA` and `clTrace MA Fp t (jump w)` as explicit lambdas, which is what
  makes "trace equality is a rewrite" literal rather than aspirational. Phase 6's
  `runTrace (labOf L) ρ = clTrace MA Fp t …` obligations should discharge against these, not
  against the per-letter lemmas.
- **The only `w`-dependence in a jump trace is the `p` letter's threshold.** `clTrace_jump_apply`
  makes this visible: `now` is at `t`, `⊤` is everywhere, `Fp` is everywhere (`b` absorbs, so a
  later `b` always exists), `⊥` is nowhere, and `p` holds exactly after `w`. That single threshold
  is what phase 5's presentation has to reproduce, and it is why distinct `w` give distinct traces.
- **Nothing in this phase evaluates a `kTypeOf` or runs `decide` over `KType`.** The read-outs go
  through `clTrace_letter`/`clTrace_now` and `PlusTruthAt` only. The one `KType` occurrence in the
  file is this sentence's counterpart in a docstring, not an evaluation.
