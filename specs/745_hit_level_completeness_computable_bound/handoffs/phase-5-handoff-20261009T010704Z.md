## Key Decisions

- **`labOf` as an `if`-chain over the five letters, not a match on `PlusFormula` structure.**
  Matching the letter's underlying formula shape (`.untl`, `.impl .bot .bot`, …) would have bound
  the table to `PlusFormula.top`'s current definitional unfolding; the `if ℓ = clNow Fp / …`
  chain depends only on `clNow`, `clLetter` and the closure-membership proofs Phase 3 landed.
- **Three state-side flag `def`s (`isNowState`, `isJumpState`, `isPostState`) factored out of
  `labOf`.** This is what lets the five letter-branch lemmas (`labOf_now`, `labOf_top`,
  `labOf_Fp`, `labOf_p`, `labOf_bot`) be `@[simp]` with a state-side right-hand side, so Phase 6
  never has to unfold `labOf` itself.
- **Nine symmetric letter-disequality aliases** (`top_ne_now`, `Fp_ne_now`, `p_ne_now`,
  `bot_ne_now`, `top_ne_Fp`, `p_ne_Fp`, `bot_ne_Fp`, `bot_ne_top`, `bot_ne_p`). Phase 3 proved
  these in the `now_ne_*` / `*_ne_*` direction; `simp` given `h : a ≠ b` rewrites `a = b` to
  `False` but NOT `b = a`, and `labOf`'s `if`-chain presents the conditions in the reverse
  direction. Each alias is a one-line `.symm`.
- **`stepOf` edges stated on `.val`, not on `Fin` literals.** `(1 : Fin (L + 3))` depends on
  `L + 3 > 1` reducing, and the mid-chain conditions (`1 ≤ i.val ≤ L + 1`, `j.val = i.val + 1`)
  are `ℕ` arithmetic that `decide`/`omega` handle directly. `decide (...)` bodies keep both
  `stepOf` and `labOf` computable `def`s, never `noncomputable def`.
- **The bridge landed at THREE levels, deliberately.** `labOf_inl`/`labOf_inr` are the four-case
  split over `letter_cases` (flags only); `labOf_inl_iff_clTrace_stayA` /
  `labOf_inr_iff_clTrace_jump` take the two position correspondences as hypotheses and conclude
  letterwise agreement with `clTrace`; `runTrace_inl_eq` / `runTrace_inr_eq` are the
  `funext`-ed function-level forms. Only the third level makes Phase 6 a rewrite, which is the
  phase's stated goal, so stopping at the first two would have left the goal unmet.
