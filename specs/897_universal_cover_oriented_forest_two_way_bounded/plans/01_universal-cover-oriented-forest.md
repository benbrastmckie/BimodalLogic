## Overview

Build the universal cover of a doubly serial one-step relation as a dependently-indexed inductive
reduced walk, prove it is an oriented forest and doubly serial, and prove the endpoint projection
is surjective and `TwoWayBounded`, so that the landed `saturation_of_orientedForest` makes the
cover a regular ZTime frame and `TwoWayBounded.plus_invariance_of_isZTime` transfers L-plus truth
from base to cover. Scope is one new module (`FormalSystem/Semantics/UniversalCover.lean`), one
new `ForMathlib` acyclicity criterion, and one additive generalization of `FrameOver.ofStep` whose
`[Finite W]` binder the infinite cover carrier cannot satisfy. Done means: the three pinned
challenge identifiers compile sorry-free and axiom-clean, the full Lean gate set is green against
its pre-task baseline, and the generated surfaces (library root, module inventory, Typst status
counts) are byte-current.

### Research Integration

Eight findings from `reports/01_universal-cover-oriented-forest.md` shape this plan, and five of
them change what gets built rather than only how. Every file:line anchor cited below was
re-derived independently at plan-authoring time rather than carried on the report's word.

1. **Mathlib's `Quiver/Covering.lean` is rejected as the starting point** (F5). It defines only
   the covering *property* (`Prefunctor.IsCovering`, star/costar bijectivity) and constructs
   nothing; the whole Mathlib tree has one consumer of it (`Quiver/Schreier.lean`). There is no
   reduced-path predicate, no universal cover of a quiver or `SimpleGraph`, and no `Quiver`
   instance on `Digraph` at this pin. Routing through it would still leave
   `(SimpleGraph.fromRel ·).IsAcyclic` -- the actual `acyclic` field of `IsOrientedForest` -- owed
   in full. The task description names that file as "the recorded starting point"; this plan
   deliberately departs from it, and `## Decisions` records that departure.
2. **The carrier is a dependently-indexed inductive reduced walk** (Decision 3), indexed by the
   last move's direction and pre-vertex as `Option (Bool × W)`. Reducedness becomes a constructor
   side condition on data already in the type, and the multigraph convention (an opposite-edge
   pair lifting to two distinct edges, a loop lifting to a ray) falls out of the constructors
   instead of being asserted.
3. **Base points are taken over all of `W`**, not one per connected component (Decision 2). The
   cover is a redundant disjoint union of the components' universal covers, which eliminates every
   connectivity, component-quotient, base-point-choice and `Classical.choice` obligation from the
   construction at zero cost to the consumers.
4. **No generation hypothesis and no doubly-serial-base hypothesis are needed** (F3).
   `FrameOver.taskRel_eq_iter` (`IntNormalForm.lean:224`) already proves every `[F.IsRegular]`
   frame over `intOrder` is generated by its one-step relation, and `F.serial w 1 zero_le_one` is
   exactly the forward and backward seriality witnesses for `F.step`. The headline theorem
   therefore quantifies over an arbitrary regular `FrameOver intOrder` with no side condition and
   no carrier identification.
5. **`FrameOver.ofStep` cannot present the cover frame** (F2). Reading its body
   (`IntNormalForm.lean:456-488`) confirms the `[Finite W]` binder is consumed at exactly one
   place -- the final `TaskFrame.saturation_of_finite (ofStepRel R₁)` argument -- and the cover
   carrier is infinite whenever the base has any edge. A `Saturation`-parameterised sibling
   constructor is therefore additive new API, not a convenience.
6. **Unique path lifting is not needed** (F4). `TwoWayBounded` has exactly two fields (`forth`
   and `step`), and `plus_invariance_of_isZTime` (`HistoryMorphism.lean:589`) consumes those plus
   `Function.Surjective f` and the four instances `[F'.IsRegular]`, `[F.IsRegular]`,
   `[SuccOrder ↑D]` and `[IsSuccArchimedean ↑D]`. The source's "with unique lifts" is over-strong
   for this consumer.
7. **One structural lemma discharges three obligations** (Decision 5). A single
   `cases`-on-the-step-relation lemma yields the length/tag/parent disjunction from which
   `loopFree`, `oppFree` and the adjacency hypothesis of the acyclicity criterion all follow. It
   is designed to be the only place `cases` on the step relation is needed.
8. **The acyclicity obligation has a constructive route that never mentions `Walk.IsCycle`**
   (F7, R1). The criterion is proved through `isAcyclic_iff_forall_adj_isBridge` and
   `isBridge_iff` (which is `.rfl`) plus an ancestor-reachability invariant. A second, independent
   route (length-maximal cycle vertex with two down-edges) exists as fallback, so the obligation
   has two routes and neither needs a `sorry`.

The report's own layering recommendation (R5: the `ForMathlib` criterion and the frame-constructor
generalization first, then carrier, structural lemma, lifts, two-way boundedness, assembly) is
adopted as the phase order, refined by one plan-time finding the report did not enumerate: a new
`ForMathlib/` module costs four registration edits, which are scheduled as named Phase 1 work.

## Goals & Non-Goals

**Goals**:

- `isAcyclic_of_rank_parent` -- a reusable rank/parent criterion for `SimpleGraph.IsAcyclic`,
  stated in Mathlib's own namespace with no dependency on this library.
- `ofStepSat` -- the `Saturation`-parameterised sibling of `FrameOver.ofStep`, which presents a
  frame over `intOrder` from a doubly serial one-step relation on a carrier that need not be
  finite.
- `exists_orientedForest_cover` -- the assembly: every regular frame over `intOrder` has a regular
  cover frame whose endpoint projection is surjective and two-way bounded, with the L-plus truth
  transfer along it.

**Non-Goals**:

- Countability of the cover. The consumer's countable-model step runs *inside* the cover and is a
  separate step, and the archived decidability-repair finding establishes the cover must be taken
  *before* it.
- Unique path lifting as a stated theorem, and the `Prefunctor.IsCovering` machinery generally. Not
  consumed by `plus_invariance_of_isZTime`; addable later if a consumer needs it.
- Any change to an existing signature. `FrameOver.ofStep` keeps its name, its type, its
  `[Finite W]` binder, and all three of its bridge lemmas; its three downstream consumers
  (`IntPresentation.lean`, `Nomic/ZTime.lean`, and `IntNormalForm.lean`'s own worked instance) are
  untouched.
- The induced-subdigraph inheritance lemma, and any `Decidable` content.
- New rows in `docs/theorem-index.md`, and any new `Paper:` doc-comment anchor. Adding either
  invites a C15 paper-anchor failure for no benefit to this task's consumers; the module docstring
  carries the mathematical record instead.
- `FormalSystem/Metalogic/`, and the `check-metalogic-cycles.sh` allowlist. No new module imports
  any `FormalSystem.Metalogic` subdirectory, which is what keeps assertion B unperturbed.
- The Mathlib graph-inventory context note the report recommends. That is a source-store
  deliverable in a different territory, not Lean work, and belongs to its own task.

### Phase 4: The Structural Lemma and the Oriented-Forest Fields [COMPLETED]

**Goal**: Prove the single `cases`-on-the-step-relation lemma and, from it, the three fields of
`IsOrientedForest` for the cover's one-step relation, discharging the `acyclic` field through

**Tasks**:
- [x] Prove the structural lemma: a step from one cover point to another either lengthens by one
      with the target's last-move tag recording a forward move out of the source's endpoint and
      the target's parent equal to the source, or shortens by one with the source's last-move tag
      recording a backward move out of the target's endpoint and the source's parent equal to the

      on that relation.

## Lean Challenge Statements

One fenced block, not two, and every declaration sits at the module root rather than inside a
`namespace` block. This is a constraint of the snapshot assembler, not a statement about where the
implementation's declarations live: the assembler keeps only the text preceding the first
declaration and then rewrites each declaration from its assignment onward, so any `import`,
`namespace`, `open` or `end` line sitting between two declarations is discarded. The phases above
state the real namespaces -- `SimpleGraph.isAcyclic_of_rank_parent`,
`FormalSystem.Semantics.FrameOver.ofStepSat` and
`FormalSystem.Semantics.exists_orientedForest_cover` -- and the block below pins the three
signatures, with every type written so that it resolves at the root under the `open` lines shown.

```lean
import FormalSystem
import Mathlib.Combinatorics.SimpleGraph.Acyclic

open FormalSystem.Semantics FormalSystem.PlusLanguage

theorem isAcyclic_of_rank_parent {V : Type*} {G : SimpleGraph V}
    (rk : V → ℕ) (par : V → V) (hle : ∀ v, rk (par v) ≤ rk v)
    (hadj : ∀ ⦃u v : V⦄, G.Adj u v →
      (par v = u ∧ rk u < rk v) ∨ (par u = v ∧ rk v < rk u)) :
    G.IsAcyclic := sorry

def ofStepSat {W : Type} [Nonempty W] (R₁ : W → W → Prop)
    (fwd : ∀ w, ∃ u, R₁ w u) (bwd : ∀ w, ∃ v, R₁ v w)
    (hsat : TaskFrame.Saturation (ofStepRel R₁)) : FrameOver intOrder := sorry

theorem exists_orientedForest_cover (F : FrameOver intOrder) [F.IsRegular]
    (M : TaskModel F.toTaskFrame) :
    ∃ (F' : FrameOver intOrder) (_ : F'.IsRegular) (f : F'.WorldState → F.WorldState),
      TwoWayBounded f ∧ Function.Surjective f ∧
      ∃ g : HistMorphism F' F, g.toFun = f ∧
        ∀ (φ : PlusFormula) (τ' : WorldHistory F'.toTaskFrame) (t : ↑intOrder),
          PlusTruthAt (g.pullM M) τ' t φ ↔ PlusTruthAt M (g.mapH τ') t φ := sorry
```

The cover's own internal layer -- the walk inductive, the cover-point structure, the one-step
relation, the length and parent maps, the structural case lemma, the three oriented-forest field
lemmas, the two one-step lifts, the three iterated lifts, the cover frame, the surjectivity proof
and the two-way-bounded proof -- is deliberately absent from this section, for two independent
reasons. First, two of those declarations are inductive types, which have no body a snapshot tool
could force to `sorry`, so they cannot be pinned by this mechanism at all; and every remaining
declaration mentions them, so none can be stated without them. Second, they are implementation

see. The three identifiers above are statable against existing compiled API alone and are exactly
what the two named consumers -- the decidability paper proof's cover step and the completeness
programme's final-frame step -- read.

## Decisions

1. **The Quiver route is rejected.** The task description names
   `Mathlib/Combinatorics/Quiver/Covering.lean` as the recorded starting point; research
   establishes it defines only the covering property, constructs nothing, and would leave the
   `acyclic` field of `IsOrientedForest` entirely unpaid, since `Quiver.arborescenceMk` yields
   `Quiver.Arborescence` rather than acyclicity of the `fromRel` graph. This is a deliberate
   departure from the task text, recorded rather than taken silently. Its one genuine contribution
   is kept: the positive and negative orientation tags of `Quiver.Symmetrify` confirm the
   direction-tag encoding is the standard one.
2. **Base points are taken over all of the carrier**, not one per connected component. The cover
   is a redundant disjoint union of the components' universal covers; a disjoint union of forests
   is a forest, double seriality is per-point, and the projection is surjective more easily. This
   is a presentational deviation from the archived source's "disjoint union of the universal
   covers of its components", flagged here rather than taken silently, and it removes every
   connectivity, component-quotient and base-point-choice obligation.
3. **Reducedness is structural**, carried by an `Option (Bool × W)` index recording the last
   move's direction and pre-vertex. The alternative -- a flat list of tagged vertices with
   validity and reducedness side predicates -- was set aside because its reducedness predicate is
   a three-element sliding window over the vertex sequence that depends on the origin at the first
   step, which is materially more awkward than the index.
4. **The cover's one-step relation is an inductive**, not an equation between cover points, to
   keep `HEq` out of every subsequent proof.
5. **`FrameOver.ofStep` is generalized in place** to a `Saturation`-parameterised sibling rather
   than having its body duplicated inside the new module, which would let the composition,
   seriality and limit proofs drift. This widens the task's territory to
   `FormalSystem/Semantics/IntNormalForm.lean`, and the edit is kept minimal: no existing
   signature changes and no existing consumer is adapted.
6. **No generation hypothesis and no doubly-serial-base hypothesis are taken.** Both are
   projections of `[F.IsRegular]` over `intOrder`, so the headline theorem quantifies over an
   arbitrary regular frame.
7. **Unique path lifting is not stated.** `plus_invariance_of_isZTime` does not consume it, and
   stating it would cost the covering-property machinery for nothing.
8. **The acyclicity criterion is placed in `FormalSystem/ForMathlib/`**, not inside the new
   semantics module, because it mentions nothing from this library and Mathlib has no equivalent
   at this pin. The placement costs four registration edits, which are scheduled as named Phase 1
   work rather than discovered mid-phase.
9. **No `Paper:` doc-comment anchor and no theorem-index row is added.** C15 resolves every
   paper-anchor citation against a pinned record, so a new anchor for a declaration whose paper
   counterpart is not in that record would fail the gate for no benefit. The module docstring
   carries the mathematical record instead.
