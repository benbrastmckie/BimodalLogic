## Overview

The task closed under **outcome (b)**, as the plan recorded and the task description names as a
complete and valid result: the single-family hypothesis bundle `Presents` cannot be discharged
on this route in its landed shape, and the obstruction is now a theorem in the tree, not prose.
For every signature and depth, `∃ T S, Presents M τ t φ sig k T S` holds iff `φ` is `⊡`-decided
at the seam (`exists_presents_iff`), and a regular `ℤ`-frame seam with no inhabitant for any
parameters is held in place by a wired evidence probe (`no_presents_on_fixture`). Beside it the
corrected shape landed: over a two-letter trace, `⊡φ` at a seam is a hypothesis-free finite
conjunction over the realized family triples `Hit` (`plusStab_iff_forall_hit`), field (3) is
discharged for every depth-`≤ k` sentence target (`determinedByKType_eval`), and the four-field
`PresentsMulti` is inhabited outright — with the reduction's entire remaining content isolated in
`Hit`, which nothing in the tree computes.

| (4) `verdict` | Does not follow from (1)-(3); it pins the one value all pairs share. | A finite conjunction over `Hit` (`plusStab_iff_forall_hit`); the `Bool` is `decide (⊡φ)` under `open Classical`. |

Coverage answer, as the task asked for early: the landed `KTypeSummary/` cluster supplies
Büchi's **complementation half in full** — the hypothesis-free bridge needs nothing beyond
`family_cover`, `family_saturation`, `finite_family_triples` and the new Doets corollary — and
**nothing of the realizability half**: computing `Hit` is the residue (B1-B3 below).
