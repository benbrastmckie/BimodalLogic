## Literature Proof Structure

**Source**: Caleiro, Vigano, Volpe, "On the Mosaic Method for Many-Dimensional Modal Logics: A Set-Theoretic Approach to Mosaics" (2013), sections 3.1 (Defs 3.1-3.7), 3.2 (Defs 3.8-3.9, Lemma 3.10, Thm 3.13), 4.3 (Thm 4.11 and the paragraph following it). In corpus, `verified_conversion`. This is the stand-in for the absent Handbook section 5.10; it treats exactly "linear tense logic plus an orthogonal S5-like modality", the closest published shape to L⁺'s `⊡`.
**Strategy**: existence of a saturated set of mosaics (finite, syntactic) iff satisfiability, by a defect-curing ω-construction; decidability by enumerating all mosaic sets on the finite closure.

### Step Map
1. **Points** (Def 3.2): subsets `Γ ⊆ Λ` of a finite closure satisfying local conditions L1 (negation-complete), L2 (conjunction), L3 (`∀B ∈ Γ → B ∈ Γ`, the T-axiom for the horizontal box). -- [Caleiro] Def 3.2
2. **Mosaics** (Def 3.3): pairs `(Γ, Δ)` of points. VERTICAL coherence V1-V3 (`G`/`H` propagation); HORIZONTAL coherence H1: `Γ ∼_Λ Δ` (state-equivalent: same state formulas). -- [Caleiro] Def 3.3
3. **Vertical saturation** (Def 3.5): SV1/SV2 (`FA ∈ Ω` needs a vertical successor mosaic containing `A`; dually `PA`), SV3 (insertion), plus class-specific conditions. -- [Caleiro] Def 3.5
4. **Horizontal saturation** (Def 3.6): SH1: if `∃A ∈ Ω` and `A ∉ Ω` then there is `(Ω, Γ) ∈ S_H` with `A ∈ Γ`. -- [Caleiro] Def 3.6
5. **Structure of mosaics** (Def 3.7): `(S_V, S_H)` with shared `Points`; combined interaction conditions SWdc, STrn, SCon, SSdc, SMb- for the branching classes. -- [Caleiro] Def 3.7
6. **Chronicles and defects** (Defs 3.8-3.9): a labelled frame based on `S`; a defect is an unwitnessed `FA`, `PA`, or `∃A`. -- [Caleiro] Defs 3.8-3.9
7. **Curing lemma** (Lemma 3.10): every defect on a finite chronicled frame based on `S` can be cured by adding points, using SV1/SV3/SH1 (and SWdc/SSdc for interacting classes). -- [Caleiro] Lemma 3.10
8. **Equivalence** (Thm 3.13): `Γ` satisfiable iff a structure of mosaics for `Γ` exists; `⇐` by the ω-construction, `⇒` by labelling a model with fresh atoms per world. -- [Caleiro] Thm 3.13
9. **Decidability** (Thm 4.11): for the BASIC class `D = ()` only, enumerate the finitely many mosaic sets on `Λ` (`O(2^{2^n})`) and check coherence+saturation in polynomial time. -- [Caleiro] Thm 4.11

### Dependencies
- Step 7 depends on Steps 3-5; Step 8 depends on Steps 6-7; Step 9 depends on Step 8 and on `Λ` being finite WITHOUT combined conditions.
- Caleiro section 4.3, immediately after Thm 4.11: "the argument ... does not extend to the logics `L(C, D)` for `D ≠ ()`. Namely, when there is some interaction between the vertical and the horizontal components, the simple translation of a model into sets of mosaics ... produces a structure that does not necessarily satisfy all the saturation conditions required"; considering mosaics on the full language would restore saturation "but then the result of decidability would not follow since the number of possible mosaics ... is infinite". Decidability of the interacting Ockhamist class is credited to Burgess via Rabin's theorem, not to mosaics.

### Potential Formalization Challenges
- **Step 2 (H1) and Step 4 (SH1) vs. the repository's `⊡`.** The repository's horizontal relation is "same `WorldState` at the same time" (`stab_iff`), and `stab_congr_state` makes every `⊡χ` and `□χ` a state formula. H1 is therefore exactly `Mosaic.Agree`. SH1 is the witness direction, which the landed `StabSaturated` does NOT encode (it encodes L3/propagation, automatic on semantic labels).
- **Step 5: L⁺ needs a combined condition Caleiro does not list.** Two histories agreeing at state `s` at `t` have the SAME set of admissible continuations (the task relation from `s`). In a label calculus this is "the vertical successors available from `Γ` are invariant under `Γ ∼ Δ`" — an interaction between the dimensions. Note the repository's structure is NOT Ockhamist: same-state histories need not share a past, so SWdc fails and the class is not one of Caleiro's `D`; the condition is bespoke.
- **Step 9 does not transfer** by the source's own statement once Step 5's bespoke condition is present. Any L⁺ mosaic decidability argument must either (a) prove the bespoke condition is reflected by finite-closure labels (an open theorem, not in any corpus source), or (b) use a richer mosaic (the source's own suggestion, section 5: "pairs of horizontal segments", citing Reynolds [19] for rectangular frames).
- **`amalgamate` is semantic, not syntactic.** The landed `paste`-based amalgamation glues HISTORIES; the mosaic calculus glues LABELS. The "free" amalgamation precondition does not carry to the syntactic calculus, where gluing is a coherence condition on pairs of points (V1-V3, H1), not a theorem.
- **Relation to `Probe710.not_finite_width_fmp`.** A saturated set of mosaic-TYPES presents finitely many types, not finite fibres; the ω-construction (Step 8, `⇐`) produces an infinite model in which types repeat. R2 is therefore not refuted by 710, but 710 does require that the unfolding generate unboundedly many distinct histories through a state from finitely many types, which SH1 alone does not guarantee.
