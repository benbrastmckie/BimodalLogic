## Why the Sub-Interval Problem Is Fundamental

Proved by analysis that the sub-interval types of (x, u)/(x', u') are NOT determined by:
- Interval types of (x, t) at depth k
- Depth-k 1-var NFs of x, t, u
- Orderings

Counterexample: Two different arrangements of types within an interval can produce different sub-interval type sets while having the same overall interval types and the same split-point type.
