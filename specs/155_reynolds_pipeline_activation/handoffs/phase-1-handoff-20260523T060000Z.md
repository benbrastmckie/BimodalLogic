### Analysis: c in S_C^M from Existing Properties

**Result**: NOT POSSIBLE.

The suffices c has: hc_interval, hcd_form (formula agreement with d at rank r), hcd_gp (gap/point agreement), hcd_boundary (boundary correspondence).

To show c in S_C_M, we need: for all mu u > c in M, cont_holds_cross at u. This means: for all A with depth <= r, if A holds at all mu v in (a_bwd(n), y') in N, then A(u in M).

This is a cross-structure property: N-truth implies M-truth at arbitrary u above c. The game transfers truth between specific challenge-response pairs (c <-> d), not at arbitrary points above c. There is no mechanism in the suffices hypotheses to derive this property.

**Why c_inf works but suffices c doesn't**: c_inf = inf(S_C_M) satisfies c_inf in S_C_M by the standard infimum argument (if u > c_inf, then u is not a lower bound, so exists s in S_C_M with s < u, and s's tail condition gives cont_holds_cross at u). This is a purely set-theoretic argument that requires c_inf to BE the infimum, not just have formula agreement with d.
