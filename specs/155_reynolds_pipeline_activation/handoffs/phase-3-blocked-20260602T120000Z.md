## Blocker Analysis

### The Sub-Interval Problem (Confirmed Fundamental)

The 3 root sorries (StaviCompleteness.lean lines 2347, 2429, 2787) trace to `nf_2var_existential_transfer`, which needs to prove: for a 2-point config (x,t)/(x',t') with matching depth-k NFs and interval types, 3-var existential transfer holds at depth j < k.

At depth j=j'+1, after zone-matching u to u', the quantifier part needs 4-var transfer at depth j' for the configuration (u,x,t)/(u',x',t'). This requires interval_nf_types agreement for SUB-INTERVALS (x,u)/(x',u') and (u,t)/(u',t'), which is NOT derivable from the enclosing interval types.

Specifically: interval_nf_types M k x t = interval_nf_types M' k x' t' does NOT imply interval_nf_types M k x u = interval_nf_types M' k x' u'. The counterexample: M has {tau_a, tau_b} in (x,u) and {} in (u,t); M' has {tau_b} in (x',u') and {tau_a} in (u',t'). Both full intervals have {tau_a, tau_b}.

### Approaches Analyzed

1. **Direct NF induction on j (inner depth)**: Fails because sub-interval types are needed at every depth, not just the base case.

2. **Using nf_2var_from_interval_data at lower depth (mutual recursion)**: Creates circularity -- nf_2var_from_interval_data at depth k calls nf_2var_existential_transfer at depth k, which would need nf_3var_from_interval_data at depth j < k, which needs nf_3var_existential_transfer...

3. **Splitting Zone Match (finding u' that preserves sub-interval decomposition)**: Not provable from the hypotheses. Different points with the same NF type can have different sub-interval type decompositions.

4. **EF Game Bridge (plan's approach)**: Correct approach per GHR93 literature, but requires 300-500 lines of new infrastructure connecting two different type systems:
   - NF world: M.carrier with sig, NormalForm sig k n
   - Game world: ExtendedCarrier with muSig sig, rank_type, decomposition_agreement
   - Bridge needs: nf_characteristic M k 1 -> nf_profile (extendPoint x) -> rank_type
   - Also needs: interval_nf_types -> interval_types on ExtendedCarrier
   - Then compose with ghr93_decomposition_implies_game (sorry-free) and extract NF agreement back

### Why the Bridge Is Hard

The main difficulty is connecting two different monadic signatures:
- M operates with `sig.preds` (the original predicates)
- ExtendedCarrier operates with `muSig sig = sig.preds + Unit` (adding a mu predicate)
- NormalForm types are indexed by signature, so NormalForm sig k 1 != NormalForm (muSig sig) (2*r) 1

The connection goes through:
1. `stavi_truth_mu_at_point`: for extendPoint x, mu-truth = standard truth
2. `nf_profile_determines_rank_type`: nf_profile determines rank_type
3. But nf_profile is NF on muSig at depth 2*r, while we have NF on sig at depth k

Proving that depth-k NF on sig determines nf_profile on muSig requires showing that the extended structure's NF at actual points is functionally determined by the original structure's NF.

### Additional Finding: chronicle_gap_contradiction Approach

The Phase 5 approach (rewiring limitDomSubtype_isSuccArchimedean) has an additional complication: the old proof in ChronicleToCountermodel.lean (commented out, lines 488-762) tried to show not-contemp_equiv for points with different MCS. But `one_class` (from NoGapsDiscreteProof.lean) proves ALL points ARE contemp_equiv in Prior structures. So the old approach of deriving contradiction from not-contemp_equiv is fundamentally wrong.

The correct approach for chronicle_gap_contradiction needs a different path:
- Either use `one_class` to show the structure is isomorphic to Z (hence SuccArchimedean)
- Or find a direct proof that bounded orbits are impossible in Prior structures
