## Why rank-r agreement is insufficient

Specifically:
- If t' < d: t' ∉ S_C because some mu-point u between t' and d has ¬ cont_holds. But rank-r formula agreement between t' and d doesn't constrain what happens between them.
- If d < t': t' ∈ S_C (upward-closedness), and d ≤ t' (hd_glb). But this is consistent with d < t'; no rank-r formula contradiction arises.
