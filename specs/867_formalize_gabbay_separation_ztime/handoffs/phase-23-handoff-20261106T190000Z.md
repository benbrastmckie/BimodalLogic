## Key Decisions, and the One That Phase 24 Must Know About

**The base case is `sepMulti`, not the identity.** Phase 23's recursion bullet says `sep7Body`
returns "`φ` unchanged when `untlDepth φ ≤ 1`"; its own base-case bullet, three items earlier,
says `hasPureUTypes_of_untlDepth_le_one` "is what makes the budget's base case `sepMulti`,
exactly as GHR's `n = 1` case is 10.2.6". The two are inconsistent and only the second reading is
sound: a formula of `untlDepth` 1 satisfies 10.2.6's hypothesis but is in general **not**
separated, because its maximal `untl`s may sit under `snce` nodes. Returning it unchanged would
be unsound at every depth-1 input and would additionally break the recursive call at every
greater depth, since the reindexed constituents land at depth at most one. Implemented as
`sepMulti φ` at the guard. `sep7Fuel 0 φ = φ` is retained and is sound for a different reason:
budget 0 is reached only at `untlDepth φ ≤ 0`, where `isSOnly_of_untlDepth_eq_zero` makes `φ`
`untl`-free and hence already separated. Recorded in the module docstring, annotated inline on
the plan item, and in `issues.jsonl`.

**`sep7Fuel_spec` carries two conjuncts, not three.** The plan directs that the third conjunct be
"whatever invariant the recursion needs re-established at the recursive call — state it from the
proof obligation, not from a guess". Read off the obligation: `mapS_substLeaf_spec7`'s induction
runs on `IsSeparated S` and needs from the recursive call only separatedness and the truth
equivalence. `NoSnceUnderUntl` of the reindexed constituents **is** needed, but as a hypothesis
discharged locally at the call site by `noSnceUnderUntl_substLeaf_of_isSOnly` and
`noSnceUnderUntl_unabstrFn`, not as a conclusion the recursion re-establishes. Phase 24's part
(b) anticipates this: if "sep7 does not raise `junctionDepth`" turns out to need a third
conjunct, that is an additive change to `sep7Fuel_spec` with **no** signature change to
`isSeparated_sep7` or `usTruth_sep7`.

**The landed device was reused, not rebuilt**, and the two blocking greps confirm it
mechanically. One addition was necessary: `substLeaf_mapU_abstrArgs`, the two-level syntactic
round trip, whose `untl` case is exactly the landed `substLeaf_mapU_abstr` applied to the node's
two arguments.

**For Phase 24's duality bullet**: `IsSeparated φ` implies `NoSnceUnderUntl φ` constructor for
constructor (`IsSeparated.untl` gives `IsUOnly` of both arguments, matching
`NoSnceUnderUntl.untl`, and `IsSeparated.snce`'s `IsSOnly` arguments satisfy the `snce` clause
through `noSnceUnderUntl_of_isSOnly`). That bridge is **not** landed here — it was dropped to
avoid scope creep — but it is six lines if Phase 24's conjugation step wants it.
