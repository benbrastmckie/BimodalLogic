## Key Decisions, and the Three Plan Deviations (all annotated inline on the plan items)

1. **The abstraction's inner pass is `mapSDeep`, not the landed `mapS`.** The plan's sketch
   `abstrSnceArgs j L (untl ψ φ) = untl (mapS (abstrFn j L) ψ) (mapS (abstrFn j L) φ)` cannot be
   what the bullet describes: `mapS` stops at `untl` without descending, so a maximal `snce`
   lying under a nested `untl` is never reached. Counterexample
   `snce ⊥ (untl ⊥ (untl ⊥ (snce ⊥ p)))`: junction depth 3, the inner `snce ⊥ p` is in
   `innerSTypes`, the sketch leaves it in place, `NoSnceUnderUntl` fails, `sep7` is inapplicable.
   The bullet's primary instruction — rewrite each maximal `snce` inside each maximal `untl` — is
   executed with `mapSDeep k φ := (mapU (fun u => (k u.swap).swap) φ.swap).swap`, used only via
   its five `rfl`/`simp` equations. Both blocking greps are clean (no second
   `mapU`/`mapS`/`substLeaf`/`abstrFn`/`unabstrFn`; the Syntax-level update lemma is cited
   nowhere).
2. **`sep8Body` guards at each maximal temporal node, not at the whole formula.** Exactly the
   shape the Phase 23 handoff's sizing addendum predicted, for the reason it gave: part (c)'s
   bound is `≤ max (jdU φ) 1` and is strict against `junctionDepth φ` only where the head kind
   forces `jdU φ < jdS φ` (or its mirror). For `jdU φ = jdS φ` — reachable at every depth `≥ 2`
   — it is not strict. GHR's "dual nature of the results so far" reduction is therefore a
   precondition of part (c), not a convenience. The `untl` case is `k (sep8StepDual _)`, proved
   through `junctionDepth_swap`, `usTruth_swap`, `usTruth_swap_reflect`.
3. **Part (a) is a `≤ 2` bound, strict only above depth 2.** At depth 2 (e.g. `snce ⊥ (untl ⊥ p)`)
   there is nothing to abstract and no drop; the recursion closes regardless because part (c)
   licenses the call. Landed as `junctionDepth_mapU_abstrSnceArgs_le_two` plus
   `junctionDepth_mapU_abstrSnceArgs_lt (h : 2 < junctionDepth φ)`.
