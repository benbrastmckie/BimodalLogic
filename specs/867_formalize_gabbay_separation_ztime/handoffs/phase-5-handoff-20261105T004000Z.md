## The pinned Case 5 candidate

```
   P ∧ (U(A,B) ∨ A ∨ ¬B)
 ∨ (¬U(A,B) ∧ ¬A ∧ B ∧ S(q ∧ ¬A ∧ B, (A ∨ ¬B) ∧ q ∧ P))
```
with `K := ¬A ∧ ¬B`, `N := K ∧ S(¬q, ¬A ∧ B)`,
`Inner := S(¬K, A ∧ S(¬K, a))`, `Ev := K ∧ ¬S(¬q,¬A∧B) ∧ Inner`, and
`P := (¬K ∧ S(a,¬K)) ∨ Ev ∨ (¬N ∧ S(¬N, Ev))`.

Derivation: for `r < t`, `U(A,B)@r ↔ Q(r) ∨ (Bint(r) ∧ U(A,B)@t)`; splitting on the pure-future
`U(A,B)@t` gives `U(A,B)@r ↔ ¬bad*(r)` resp. `¬bad*(r) ∧ ¬C(r)`, where
`bad*(r) := ∃x∈(r,t]: K(x) ∧ (¬A∧B) on (r,x)` and `C(r) := (¬A∧B) on (r,t]`. Re-anchoring the
guard-failure condition at its rightmost witness collapses the `U(A,B)@t` branch to
`U(A,B)@t ∧ P`, because `q` on `(s,x_g)` forces `S(¬q,¬A∧B)@x_g`'s witness at or below `s`,
handing `bad*(s)` the witness `x_g` and contradicting the event conjunct.

## Precisely located obstruction (Cases 6, 7, 8)

Not a tactic failure. Cases 7 and 8 have `¬U(A,B)` in the **guard**, so their guard-failure
condition is `∃r: ¬q(r) ∧ ¬bad*(r)` with `¬bad*` a universal over `(r,t]`; re-anchoring that
`∃∀` needs two nested witnesses and retains an `s`-bounded **forward** conjunct, which is not a
formula at `r` and so cannot be an `S`-guard. Cases 6 and 8 have `¬U(A,B)` in the **event**, so
their event conjunct is the un-negated `bad*(s)`, and Case 5's collapse — which depends on
negating that existential — is unavailable. The negation route is circular: `¬Case8` has
Case 5's shape and `¬Case5` has Case 8's.

What would unblock: a published correct form of Cases 6-8 over the integer flow (the source
gives only reductions with acknowledged gaps), or a measure-carrying device bounding the
re-anchoring chain. Over `ℤ` each step crosses one `¬B` point and `(s,t]` is finite, so a
bounded chain exists per model; what is missing is one finite formula serving every model.
