## Overview

`Axiom.minFrameClass` had a machine-checked *upper* bound only (`axiom_validIn_min`); its
*minimality* for the two `.ZTime` axioms was carried solely by literature citations in docstrings.
This implementation lands `FormalSystem/Metalogic/Independence/ZTimeSharpness.lean`, whose nine
named theorems prove that `Axiom.prior_UZ` and `Axiom.z1` are not valid at `FrameClass.Base` and —
via the order fact that `.Base` is the unique class strictly below `.ZTime` — not valid at any class
strictly below `.ZTime`. The `.ZTime` row of `Axiom.minFrameClass` is therefore now proved sharp in
both directions rather than cited, and the three docstrings plus two `Independence/` ledgers that
previously presented the claim as literature-only now cite the theorems.

## Decisions

- **The route is not the one the dispatch anticipated, and the dispatch's trap was sidestepped.**
  Neither `Z1Countermodel.lean` nor `MinusLanguageSoundness.lean` nor `Semantics/LexCarrier.lean`
  was used. Both countermodels live on `translationFrame D` with the
  `translationHist`/`translationModel`/`translation_realizes` layer from
  `Semantics/Correspondence/DurationFrames.lean`. Because nothing is transferred out of the L-minus
  language, the `tr_ne_untl` mismatch the dispatch warned about (`Formula.someFuture` is a top-level
  `untl`; nothing in the range of `tr` is) never arises.
- **Non-discreteness, not non-Archimedean-ness, is what refutes both axioms.** Each fails over any
  *densely ordered* duration group, so one generic lemma per axiom at an arbitrary
  `(D : TemporalOrder) [DenselyOrdered ↑D]` yields the `.Base` result at `D = ℚ`. Per the dispatch's
  explicit instruction the claim was **not** silently upgraded: the theorems say `.Base` (and "any
  densely ordered `D`" in the generic lemmas), and the module docstring says in as many words that
  no claim is made about ℚ specifically or about non-Archimedean orders.
- **The `CoNotPriorU` obstruction was checked before choosing the statement form, and does not
  bite.** That obstruction arises only because `CoNotPriorU.lean`'s theorem must simultaneously
  *validate* `CO` while refuting `prior_U_gap`; a bare non-validity claim validates nothing. The
  proofs landed here are therefore **frame-level** (`¬ F.ValidOn φ`), strictly stronger than the
  model-fixed form the dispatch allowed for. The statement form and its justification are recorded
  in the module docstring.
- **The atomic instance is forced, not a convenience.** A schematic `∀ φ` non-validity form is
  false: `Axiom.prior_UZ ⊥` has an unsatisfiable antecedent and so *is* `.Base`-valid. Both
  `not_validIn_base_*` docstrings record this.
- **Phase order was chosen around a single full rebuild.** `Axioms.lean` is transitively imported by
  essentially the whole tree and Lean invalidates on whole-file hash, so Phases 1-2 verified the new
  module and its wiring with scoped builds *before* any prose edit, and Phase 4 spent exactly one
  full rebuild. No separate documentation rebuild was taken.
- **No `file.lean:NNN` citations were added.** `scripts/reanchor-lean-citations.py` rewrites those,
  so every cross-reference in the new module and the amended docstrings is by declaration name and
  file path. `def:BX-z` is the only paper anchor cited, per
  `docs/reference/paper-definitions-of-record.md`.
- **`ztimeSharpOrder`, not `qD`.** `Metalogic/Conservativity/DenseObstructionTransfer.lean` already
  declares `qD` in the enclosing `FormalSystem.Metalogic` namespace.

## Impacts

- The second half of the dual-verification adequacy argument about a permanent frame-class gap is
  now machine-checked rather than cited. The first half — that no bimodal certificate can ever
  exist for `prior_UZ` or `z1`, since a certificate would exhibit a ℤ-time countermodel
  contradicting their ℤ-time validity — already followed from `axiom_validIn_min`. The missing half
  was that these inferences are nonetheless refutable at a non-discrete temporal order, i.e. that
  the search's silence is a genuine frame-class gap rather than an artefact. That is exactly
  `not_validIn_base_prior_UZ` and `not_validIn_base_z1`.
- `not_derivable_base_prior_UZ` and `not_derivable_base_z1` are available to any consumer needing
  underivability at `.Base` for these two schemas, and the two generic
  `not_validOn_*_dense` lemmas are reusable at any densely ordered duration group, not only ℚ.
- `eq_base_of_lt_ztime` is a reusable frame-class order fact: any future `.ZTime`-minimality
  argument needs only a single `.Base` refutation.
- No existing result depends on the new module, so nothing downstream changed behaviourally; the
  only tree-wide effect was the `Axioms.lean` docstring invalidation, now rebuilt green.

## Follow-ups

Recorded here rather than acted on, per the dispatch's SCOPE clause. The `.Dense` and `.RTime` rows
of `Axiom.minFrameClass` are unproved-sharp on exactly the same grounds as the `.ZTime` row this
work closes, and the research found all four **cheaper** than the row closed here:

- `density` (`GGφ → Gφ`, `.Dense`): `validOn_dn_iff_denselyOrdered`
  (`Semantics/Correspondence/DurationFrames.lean`) is an *iff*; at `D = intOrder` (not densely
  ordered) its forward direction yields the `.Base` refutation nearly free.
- `dense_indicator` (`¬U(⊤,⊥)`, `.Dense`): `Semantics/Correspondence/Indicator.lean` proves
  `F ⊨ ¬X⊤ ↔ DenselyOrdered F.Duration` — the same one-step route.
- `prior_U_gap` and `sep` (`.RTime`): minimality here needs refutations at both `.Base` and
  `.Dense`, since both are strictly below `.RTime`. For `prior_U_gap`,
  `Independence/CoNotPriorU.lean`'s `priorUGapFormula_false` already refutes the formula in a model
  over the clock frame at ℚ — which is dense — so a single `¬ ValidOn` extraction plausibly
  discharges both classes at once. `sep` appears to have no existing witness.

Two further observations, neither in scope here and neither acted on:

- `FormalSystem/Metalogic/Independence.lean`'s module docstring and `Independence/README.md` already
  disagree about how many results the directory carries, and the README additionally uses the
  superseded class names `.Dedekind`/`.Discrete` where the code now says `.RTime`/`.ZTime`. One entry
  was added to each ledger in that ledger's own numbering convention; the pre-existing discrepancy
  was deliberately not reconciled. (The new entries do use the current `.ZTime` spelling, since
  writing `.Discrete` for a result about `FrameClass.ZTime` would be wrong rather than merely
  old-fashioned.)
- `Axioms.lean`'s `prior_UZ` docstring renders the axiom as `F(φ) → U(φ, ¬φ)` while the constructor
  is `φ.someFuture.imp (Formula.untl φ.neg φ)` — the `untl` arguments read in the opposite order.
  The shape-pin `example` protects this work's theorems from the discrepancy; correcting the
  docstring prose is a separate, tiny fix. The new module's shape-pin section notes the conflict and
  records that the pin resolves it in favour of the constructor.

Two context-extension recommendations from the research are also worth a follow-up: a note on the
`translationFrame`/`translationHist`/`translationModel`/`translation_realizes` countermodel kit
(including the two frames that silently *validate* the targets — `clockFrame`, periodic; the static
frame, time-invariant), and a paragraph recording that frame-level refutation is obstructed only
when a statement must simultaneously *validate* something on a valuation-rich flow.

Optional strengthening the plan isolated and this round did not take: `.Dense` and `.RTime`
corollaries reusing the same `ztimeSharpOrder` frame (Phase 5), and pinning the four headline
results on `FormalSystem/MainResults.lean`'s build-time axiom-audit page (Phase 6, which couples
`scripts/debug-artifact-allowlist.txt` and `C14_BASELINE` in
