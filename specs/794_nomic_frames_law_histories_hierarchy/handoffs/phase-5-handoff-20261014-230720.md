- **The risk the plan flagged did not materialise.** `F2_lawful` — the one genuinely hard proof,
  isolated in this phase precisely because it might stall — compiled on the first attempt, as did
  `F2_not_static` and `nomic_hierarchy`. No `[BLOCKED]` escalation, no `sorry`.
- `F2_not_static` avoids rewriting the *Static* equation entirely: `sigA 0` and `sigA 1` reduce
  definitionally to `e` and `a`, so `h sigA hmem 0 1` *is* the equation `H_F^e = H_F^a` up to
  defeq, and `Set.ext_iff` transports `sigB` across it. That dodges the carrier-boundary `rw`
  failure recorded in the Phase 4 handoff before it can arise.
- **Three docstring choice claims were wrong and were corrected against measurement**, which is
  the honest-recording discipline the plan asks for rather than a late polish:
  `stepPath_forward_invariant` is *not* choice-free (Mathlib's `Int.leInduction` is
  choice-dependent on this pin, while `Int.induction_on` is not), and
  `mem_lawHist_iff_isStepPath`, `mem_lawHist_ofStepFamily_iff` and `lawHist_mono_of_le` inherit
  choice from the regularity instance they consume. Only `stepPath_invariant` measures
  `[propext, Quot.sound]`. All corrections were made *before* the final verification build, not
  after it.
