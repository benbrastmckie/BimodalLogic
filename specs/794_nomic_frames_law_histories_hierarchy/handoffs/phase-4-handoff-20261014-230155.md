## Decisions / deviations

- **Scope Hypothesis confirmed**: witness 1 transcribed from the research report's R9 block
  without change beyond the recorded 100-column reflow, and built clean on the first attempt
  before witness 3 was started.
- Witness 3's `R_b`/`R_c` is written as "either both ends are `a` or neither is", which is the
  same five-pair relation as `{(a,a)} ∪ ({b,c} × {b,c})` and makes the two-sided `R_b`-invariance
  of `u = a` immediate. `R3_refl` carries both seriality obligations and the
  constant-path-is-everywhere-a-law-history fact in one lemma.
- The witnesses live in `namespace NomicHierarchy` rather than at the bare `Semantics` level,
  following `FrameConstraintIndependence.lean`'s house style; the plan left these names to be
  fixed in this phase. `nomic_hierarchy` itself will sit at the `Semantics` level, as its pinned
  Challenge statement requires.
- One build failure, fixed in one edit: `rw [h W3.a]` inside `F3_not_uniform` is refused because
  the membership binder is `↑intOrder → F3.World` while `altBC` is a `ℤ → W3`, so the rewrite is
  not type-correct at `implicit` transparency. Routed through `Set.ext_iff` instead — the same
  carrier-boundary avoidance the plan records for `mem_lawHist_ofStepFamily_iff`.
