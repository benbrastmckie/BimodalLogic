## The verdict, in one paragraph

Finding (d) is **refuted** and the obstruction is **elsewhere**. A step-counting path record IS
compositional over discrete time — the durations are exactly the multiples of one step, so the
step count is a function of the duration and `k = k₁ + k₂` subdivides — and composing single
steps into the task the relation must carry at an arbitrary pair of times is machine-checked, for
exactly this shape of construction, by Phase 3's `chain_rel`. What blocks the port is
`TaskFrame.Saturation`, a required argument of `FrameOver.ofReflectiveRegular`: on the unravelled
carrier `W ⊕ (W × List W)` none of the tree's three discharges applies — `saturation_of_finite`
binds `[Finite W]` (and `example (W : Type) [Nonempty W] : Infinite (W ⊕ (W × List W))`
machine-checks, with `FrameOver.worldNonempty` making every frame's carrier nonempty),
`saturation_of_fib_finite` wants finite fibres that an arbitrary `FrameOver D` does not supply,
and `saturation_of_deterministic` wants determinism that the unravelling contradicts by design.
