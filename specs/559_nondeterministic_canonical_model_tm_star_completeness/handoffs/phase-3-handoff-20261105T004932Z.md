## How the construction actually works (differs from the plan's sketch)

`intIso : ↑D ≃+o ℤ` identifies the carrier with `ℤ` as an **ordered group**, so the step duration
is `δ := intIso.symm 1` and `m • δ = intIso.symm m` for every `m : ℤ`. The history to be lifted
becomes a `ℤ`-indexed chain of base states; `fwdSeq` walks up it by `TwoWayBounded.step`,
`bwdSeq` down it by `exists_preimage_step_neg`, `chain` glues them, and `chain_rel` composes the
unit tasks via `FrameOver.forward_comp` (plus `FrameOver.reflection` for the backward direction)
into the task `WorldHistory.ofTotal` demands at *every* pair of times. `FrameOver.nullity`
discharges the zero-step base case. No `Order.succ`/`Order.pred` recursion and no appeal to
*Saturation* in `F'` — the Scope Hypothesis is confirmed, with `intIso` in place of the
Archimedean iterate.

## Phase 7 pre-analysis (read before starting it)

Finding (d) is **refuted** as stated and the real obstruction is elsewhere. Compositionality of a
step-counting path record IS satisfiable over ℤ-time (durations are the multiples of one step, so
`k = k₁ + k₂` subdivides). *Saturation* is the blocker: the cone carrier `W ⊕ (W × List W)` is
infinite even for finite `W`, so `TaskFrame.saturation_of_finite` does not apply;
`TaskFrame.saturation_of_fib_finite` needs finite fibres, which `aa_expand`'s arbitrary
`F : FrameOver D` does not give; and `TaskFrame.saturation_of_deterministic` is unavailable
because the cone relation branches by construction — branching is the point. The one sanctioned
reformulation (a `PartialHistory` path record) replaces the carrier by one of initial segments,
which is larger still, and leaves that obligation unchanged.
