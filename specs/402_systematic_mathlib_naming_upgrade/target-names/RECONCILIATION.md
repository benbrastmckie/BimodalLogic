## The 18 `tactic*` syntax declarations — excluded from the rewrite set

Listed separately in `tactic-declarations.txt`. These are auto-generated by `syntax`/`macro`
commands, with names derived from the user-facing tactic token
(`FormalSystem.Automation.Normalization.tacticModal_norm` from the `modal_norm` tactic).

`tools/refcount.py` reports **NO SUCH DECLARATION** for every one of them: they carry no
`.ilean` reference entry at all, definition or usage. The resolved-reference rewriter therefore
structurally cannot touch them, which is why they are a separate Phase 8 decision rather than
part of Phase 6.

Research projected 19; the measured set is **18**. A search for any other flagged name whose
final component begins with `tactic`, and for any flagged name containing the substring
`tactic`, returns nothing further (the only other hit,
`Separation.is_syntactically_separated`, is an ordinary predicate). 18 is the complete set.
