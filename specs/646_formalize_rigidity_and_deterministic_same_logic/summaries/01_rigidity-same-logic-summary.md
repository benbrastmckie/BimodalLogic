## Overview

Both MF-correspondence results landed as real, sorry-free theorems in three new library modules.
R2 (rigidity) is true as stated in the source report and needed no stronger hypothesis: over a
dense Archimedean duration group a task frame is static iff it has a uniform dwell time, so every
finite-carrier frame over such an order is static. R1 (the deterministic frames determine the
same L-logic as all task frames) is stated against the tree's `ValidDetIn` vocabulary at every
frame class, with the manuscript's unbundled `.Base` shape as a corollary; the missing third fact
the originating report overlooked, `ShiftSet.frame_deterministic`, is a one-line helper declared
in the new module. Both hypotheses of R2 are machine-checked sharp.

## Decisions

- The core chop `eq_of_rel_of_step` is stated at `[Archimedean D]` with interpolation and a
  closed step bound, with no density, *Limit*, *Seriality* or `Nontrivial`; density enters once,
  in `eq_of_rel_of_uniform_radius`. This is what "state the theorem at the hypothesis the proof
  actually uses" asks; the paper-shaped biconditional is derived from it.
- `Static` and `UniformDwell` are `def`s on a bare relation (so they apply to either frame
  bundling); `UniformDwell` uses the paper's `(w)_{x₀} = {w}` cone-equality form.
- R1 is stated uniformly in the frame class against `ValidDetIn`, with the general
  `validOnFrames_iff_deterministic` underneath; `sat_ofModel_frame`'s two-token body is inlined
  rather than importing `Compactness.lean`'s ultraproduct closure.
- The `ℤ` sharpness instance is compiled (`intPermissiveFrame_not_static`) rather than left as a
  docstring remark, which required `import Mathlib.Data.Int.SuccPred` for `SuccOrder ℤ` — the
  same import `Metalogic/Soundness.lean` already uses for the same instance.
- The theorem-index rows are pinned by C14 (the heredoc mechanism took exactly one appended line
  per declaration in each of the two blocks), so no ledger row is prose-only.

## Impacts

- `TaskFrame.Static` / `TaskFrame.UniformDwell` are now predicates available to any consumer;
  `FrameOver.static_of_finite` says finite-carrier frames over dense Archimedean orders carry no
  dynamics, which bears on finite-model constructions over `ℚ` and `ℝ` time.
- `validIn_iff_validDetIn` gives every consumer of `ValidDetIn` a free transfer to and from
  `ValidIn` at any class without a completeness detour, and `validOnFrames_iff_deterministic`
  extends it to `IsComplete`/`IsQTime`-style predicates that have no engine.
- `ShiftSet.frame_deterministic` is the fact any future edit of `ShiftSet.lean` (the
  reconciliation held by the sibling task) can relocate beside `reverse_repr` without renaming.
