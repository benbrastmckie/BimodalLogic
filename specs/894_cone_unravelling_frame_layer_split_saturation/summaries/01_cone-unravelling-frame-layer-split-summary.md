## Follow-ups

- **BLOCKER, needs a user decision.** Every frame-assembly signature the plan declares omits a
  base-*Saturation* hypothesis that the cone construction provably consumes.
  `FrameOver.ofStepSat` demands `TaskFrame.Saturation (ofStepRel (coneR F.step))`; the only route
  to it is `saturation_coneR`, which demands `TaskFrame.Saturation (ofStepRel F.step)`. All three
  escape routes are closed, and each closure is machine-checked:
  1. A bare `FrameOver` carries no frame axiom at all — `FormalSystem/Semantics/TaskFrame.lean`
     line 1072 (`structure FrameOver`) versus line 1189 (`class IsRegular`).
  2. *Compositionality* + *Seriality* + *Limit* does not imply *Saturation* —
     `FormalSystem/Semantics/FrameConstraintIndependence.lean`'s
     `constraints_pairwise_independent`, third conjunct:
     `⟨ℤ, rayRel, rayRel_compositional, rayRel_serial, rayRel_limit, rayRel_not_saturation⟩`.
  3. The cone's *Saturation* cannot be obtained structurally the way the universal cover's is:
     `IsOrientedForest (coneR E)` is false, since the `inl` layer copies `E`. Only the `inr` layer
     is a forest — which is precisely why *Saturation* had to be split in two.

  Base *Saturation* is moreover **necessary**, not merely convenient. At
  `E := fun w u => (w ≤ u : Prop)` on `ℤ`, the family
  `{TaskFrame.Fib (ofStepRel (coneR E)) (.inl w) 1 | w : ℤ}` has `w`-th member
  `{.inl u | w ≤ u} ∪ {.inr q | w ≤ q.1}`; it is `⊇`-directed (the member at `max w₁ w₂` sits
  inside the intersection, both parts being monotone in `w`), every member is a nonempty
  `IsFiber`, and `⋂₀` of it is empty. So the cone over a C+S+L base can fail *Saturation*
  outright.

  The pinned `exists_coneUnravelling_of_constraints` is therefore satisfiable **only** by
  delegating to `exists_orientedForest_cover_of_constraints`, whose existential witness is the
  universal cover and not the cone — a proof that would pass every name, sorry and axiom check
  while asserting nothing about this module. Escalated rather than silently weakened, per
  `rules/plan-compliance.md`'s *Statement Fidelity* section.

  Two mechanical resolutions, either of which unblocks the remaining Phase 5 and all of Phase 6:
  - **(a) thread the hypothesis** — `coneFrameOfSerialSat (F) (hcomp) (hser) (hlim) (hsat)`,
    `coneF_twoWayBounded_of_constraints (F) (hcomp) (hser) (hlim) (hsat)`,
    `exists_coneUnravelling_of_constraints (F) (hcomp) (hser) (hlim) (hsat) (M)`, with
    `hsat : TaskFrame.Saturation F.TaskRel` bridged into `ofStepRel` form once against
    `FrameOver.taskRel_eq_iter_of_constraints` — exactly as Phase 5's own second task directs.
  - **(b) instance-projected form only** — `coneFrame (F) [F.IsRegular]` and
    `exists_coneUnravelling (F) [F.IsRegular] (M)`, dropping the `_of_constraints` layer.
- Whichever is chosen, the plan and the module docstring should state that the cone's *Saturation*
  is **inherited** rather than manufactured. The contrast with `UniversalCover.lean` — whose base
  need not be saturated — is the substantive difference between the two constructions.
