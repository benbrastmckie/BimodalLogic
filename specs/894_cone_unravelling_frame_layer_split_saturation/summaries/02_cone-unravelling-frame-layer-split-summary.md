## Decisions

- **`coneF_surjective` needs `(E := F.step)` supplied explicitly at the headline.** Its `{E}`
  binder is genuinely unread (the theorem carries `@[nolint unusedArguments]` for exactly that
  reason), so inference has nothing to solve from and the term fails to elaborate without it.
- **`x.natAbs = n + 1` from `x < 0` cannot go through `omega`.** `x : ↑intOrder` is not
  syntactically `Int`, so omega reports "no usable constraints found". Routed through
  `Nat.exists_eq_succ_of_ne_zero (Int.natAbs_ne_zero.mpr (ne_of_lt hxlt))` instead.
- **The `Constraints consumed:` markers on `base_taskRel_eq_ofStepRel` and
  `coneF_twoWayBounded` list all four constraints, not the three their proofs read.** C34a is
  right to insist: the marker states what the declaration *demands*, and `[F.IsRegular]` demands
  four. `UniversalCover.lean` can mark its instance-projected declarations at three because each
  has a binder-free twin carrying that claim; this module has no twin by the recorded decision,
  so a three-constraint marker here would advertise a hypothesis set no declaration offers. The
  reason is stated inline at both declarations.
- **Literal `_of_constraints` and `sorry` tokens were kept out of the new doc comments.** Phase 5's
  and Phase 6's own verification criteria are bare greps over the module, so explanatory prose
  mentioning either token trips them. Three occurrences were rephrased with no change in
  substance; the substantive claims (why there is no constraint-parameterised layer, why no
  mechanical check can detect delegation to the cover) are intact.

## Impacts

- `exists_coneUnravelling` is available to the formula-level Auxiliary-Atoms rule as the
  frame-side prerequisite: a regular frame over `intOrder` can be replaced by a regular
  unravelling in which the base survives verbatim in the `inl` layer alongside a path-recording
  `inr` layer, with L⁺ truth transferred along a surjective two-way bounded projection.
- `NamingRules.lean` no longer asserts the obligation is open, so a reader of that module is
  pointed at the discharge rather than at a gap.
- `base_taskRel_eq_ofStepRel` is a relation-level equality the tree previously lacked. It is a
  promotion candidate for `IntNormalForm.lean`, beside `taskRel_eq_iter`; the rationale for
  keeping it local for now is recorded at the declaration so a later reader can move it
  deliberately rather than duplicate it.
- `ProofSystem.FrameClass.Sat` members can now be manufactured by either route, and the two
  modules cross-reference each other on which to pick: the cover when the base is not known
  *Saturated*, the cone when a verbatim copy of the base must survive.
