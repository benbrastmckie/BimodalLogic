## Executive Summary

- **TM/L+ over regular Z-frames EVADES every published negative result in the corpus, and it does so for a boring reason: not one of them has a hypothesis TM meets.** Every undecidability or non-r.e. theorem for products with an S5 factor needs either a *second* linear/transitive/K factor commuting with time (GKWZ Thms 5.38-5.40, 7.5, 7.17-7.20, 7.24-7.25), *three* commuting factors (GKWZ Thm 8.22; Thm 11.1 read through Thm 3.29), or a *difference/counting* operator (HKKM 2019 Thm 1). TM has exactly one linear factor, one S5 factor, and no difference modality. The S5-plus-linear products themselves sit on the *decidable* side of the book (Thms 6.60, 6.61; HWZ Cor 69 for `<Z,<>` with Until and Since).
- **The frame condition the brief posited is the wrong one, and in the wrong direction.** TM's task frames *do* satisfy the full product frame condition: with total-domain histories (`PartialHistory.lean:467`) and the unrestricted `box` clause (`PlusTruth.lean:88`), the carrier `H_F x D` with `box` and `<` is literally GKWZ's product frame (section 3.3, p.125) of the universal frame on `H_F` and `(D,<)`; commutativity and Church-Rosser (p.220-221) hold by construction. What separates TM from the product literature is that TM is a **proper extension** of `Log_SU(Z) x S5`: `modal_future` (`Axioms.lean:297`, `box phi -> box G phi`) and `box_time_invariant` (`PlusTruth.lean:349`) are TM-valid and product-refutable. The conditions responsible are three closure properties of the history set -- shift closure, path closure, limit closure (`blc_plusValid`, `PlusLimitClosure.lean:258`) -- together with state-locality of valuations (`TaskModel.lean:53-60`).
- **The stability modal is not a product coordinate.** `stab` (`PlusTruth.lean:93`) is the same-state-at-the-same-time equivalence; Church-Rosser between `stab` and the temporal relation fails on any frame with a non-deterministic step. So the three-dimensional negatives do not apply even as a reduction target, and in GKWZ section 3.4 / Halpern-van der Meyden-Vardi terms `stab` is the knowledge modality of a *synchronous* agent whose local state is the global state, with neither perfect recall (Thomason section 4 Def. 6 condition (2); Caleiro Def. 2.5 Wdc) nor no-learning -- the one class whose published logic is just the fusion (GKWZ Thm 13.3; HvdMV Thm 3.1), and again TM strictly extends it (`stab p <-> p` is L+-valid, fusion-refutable).
- **Neither direction transfers, so the literature does not settle L+.** Published positives (6.60, 6.61, HWZ Cor 69, Caleiro Thm 4.11, GKWZ 13.6) decide *sublogics* of TM/L+; extensions of decidable logics need not be decidable. Published negatives have unmet hypotheses. The brief's "free choice of runs" diagnosis is also corrected: HWZ Remark 13 (PDF p.11) shows a quasimodel's run set may be enlarged freely, so the obstruction is not run choice but that TM's state function and run set must be *induced by one time-invariant frame whose states carry the atoms and `stab`* -- which is exactly the (W) soundness half the programme is pursuing.
- **Consequence for Stage 1.** Nothing in the corpus shows the ranking or either creation of rounds 1-2 to be aimed at an undecidable target; nothing shows the opposite. The one literature-grounded risk worth naming on the record: if a formula can force the relevant part of a frame to be backward-deterministic, `stab` acquires perfect recall and the T x W / "know-time-and-do-not-forget" family starts to apply. ## Findings

### Codebase Patterns

**F1. TM/L+ is a product-frame logic with a restricted model class, not a relativised product. [VERIFIED]**

The structure the question turns on, read at source:

- A world history has a *total* domain: `WorldHistory F := {tau : PartialHistory F // tau.IsTotal}` (`PartialHistory.lean:467-468`), the paper's `H_F`. So the evaluation carrier is the full Cartesian product `H_F x D`.
- The `box` clause is unrestricted over histories at the same time: `| .box phi => forall sigma : WorldHistory F, PlusTruthAt M sigma t phi` (`PlusTruth.lean:88`). The temporal clauses move along the same history (`:89-92`). Atoms are state-local: `| .atom p => M.valuation (tau.state t) p` (`:85`), with `TaskModel.valuation : F.WorldState -> Atom -> Prop` (`TaskModel.lean:53-60`).
- Hence the 2-frame `(H_F x D, R_box, <)` with `(tau,t) R_box (sigma,s) iff t = s` and `(tau,t) < (sigma,s) iff tau = sigma and t < s` is, verbatim, GKWZ's product frame `F1 x F2` of the universal frame on `H_F` and the flow `(D,<)` (GKWZ section 3.3, p.125: "(u1,v1) R_h (u2,v2) iff u1 R u2 and v1 = v2; (u1,v1) R_v (u2,v2) iff u1 = u2 and v1 R v2"). Left and right commutativity and the Church-Rosser property (GKWZ section 5.1, pp.220-221, Fig. 5.1) hold by construction: for a universal horizontal relation each of the three diagrams closes with `u := (sigma, s)`.
- Consequently, for the base language, **every validity of the product `Log_SU(D) x S5` is TM-valid on every task frame** -- TM-models are product models with a state-local valuation.
- **The inclusion is proper.** `modal_future : Axiom ((box phi).imp (box (allFuture phi)))` (`Axioms.lean:297`) is a TM axiom, and `box_time_invariant` (`PlusTruth.lean:349-358`) proves `box psi` has one truth value per model, its docstring stating the reason: "the history set of a task frame is shift-closed by construction (`WorldHistory.timeShift`)". A product countermodel to `box p -> box G p`: `H = {h1, h2}`, `D = Z`, `p` true at `(h1,0)`, `(h2,0)`, false at `(h2,1)`; `box p` holds at `(h1,0)`, `box G p` fails there. Shift closure of `H_F` is therefore a frame condition TM imposes that product frames do not -- the first of the conditions that make TM an extension.
- The regular class adds four further constraints (`TaskFrame.lean:1172-1211`: `comp`, `serial`, `limit` at `:828`, `saturation` at `:683`), none of which is a product-frame property and none of which is determinism (the tree keeps deterministic frames as a separate, independent notion under `Metalogic/Independence/`).
- The base language's Z-time validity is already decided and finitely axiomatised in-tree: `Compression.decidableValidZTime (phi : Formula) : Decidable (ValidZTime phi)` (`Assembly.lean:147`); `completeness_ztime : WeakCompleteness FrameClass.ZTime` (`StrongCompleteness.lean:1119`), where `WeakCompleteness fc := forall psi, ValidIn fc psi -> Derivable fc [] psi` (`SetConsequence.lean:226`) and `FrameClass.Sat .ZTime F = F.IsRegular /\ F.IsZTime` (`FrameClassValidity.lean:151-155`); the axiom system `Axiom` (`Axioms.lean:152-466`) has finitely many schemas. These are statements about TM over regular Z-frames, base language, empty premise set, hypothesis `[F.IsRegular]`.

**F2. `stab` is not a product coordinate, and is not the T x W / Ockhamist undividedness relation. [VERIFIED]**

- `| .stab phi => forall sigma, tau.state t = sigma.state t -> PlusTruthAt M sigma t phi` (`PlusTruth.lean:93`). As a relation on `H_F x D`: `(tau,t) R_stab (sigma,s) iff t = s and tau.state t = sigma.state t`. It is an equivalence on each time slice, refining `R_box`.
- Church-Rosser with the temporal relation fails: from `(tau,t) R_stab (sigma,t)` and `(tau,t) < (tau,s)` the product condition demands `u` with `(sigma,t) < u` and `(tau,s) R_stab u`, forcing `u = (sigma,s)` and `tau.state s = sigma.state s`; this fails whenever two histories agree at `t` and diverge at `s`, which is exactly a non-deterministic step. Regularity does not exclude it (F1, last bullet). So `(H_F x D, <, R_box, R_stab)` is **not** a three-dimensional product frame, and `stab_state_only` (`PlusTruth.lean:332`) -- `stab phi` depends on the world state alone -- is the formal face of the same fact.
- `stab` also fails the backward-closure that defines the historical-necessity tradition. Thomason section 4, Definition 6, condition (2): "if `w1 ~_t w2` and `t' < t` then `w1 ~_{t'} w2`" (with the valuation constraint of Definition 7 to match). Caleiro Definition 2.5 (Wdc): "if `x < y ~ y'` then there exists `x' ~ x` with `x' < y'`". Both say undividedness persists into the past; `R_stab` does not (two histories may share a state at `t` and differ at `t-1`). In GKWZ section 3.4 terms, `stab` is the accessibility of an agent that *knows the time* (pp.137: "`(t,f) R_i (t',f')` implies `t = t'`") but neither *does not forget* nor *does not learn* (p.137-138). `box` is the degenerate agent that knows the time and nothing else -- synchronous, no forgetting, no learning, hence (GKWZ p.138: such a structure "is isomorphic to the product of frames `(T,<)` and `(R, S_1, ..., S_n)`") the product coordinate of F1.
- L+ strictly extends the synchronous-fusion logic as well: `stab p <-> p` is valid on every task frame (state-local atoms, `PlusTruth.lean:85, 93`) and is refutable in any C-basic structure (Caleiro Def. 2.5-2.6) or synchronous temporal epistemic structure (GKWZ p.135-136) with a point-wise valuation.

**F4. The published positive results, and why none transfers upward. [VERIFIED at source]**

- **Thm 6.60, p.324**: `Log{(N,<)} x S5` and `PTL x S5` decidable, via the one-variable fragment of `QLog_SU(N)` (Thm 11.31, p.487) and the translation of Thm 3.29 (p.162). **Thm 6.61, p.324**: `K4.3 x S5`, `Log{(Q,<)} x S5`, `Lin x S5`, `Log_FP(Q) x S5` decidable by quasimodels; footnote at p.572 (chunk 594): the proof "is not hard to generalize ... for the case of S and U as well". **Thm 11.76, p.529**: `PTL x S5 = [PTL, S5]`, i.e. the product with `N`-time is finitely axiomatised by the fusion plus commutativity.

Namely, when there is some interaction between the vertical and the horizontal components, the simple translation of a model into sets of mosaics ... produces a structure that does not necessarily satisfy all the saturation conditions required." The dispatch's citation of this passage is confirmed. Note the direction: Caleiro's interaction classes are `Wdc`, `Sdc`, `Mb-` (Def. 2.5), the Ockhamist backward-closure conditions; TM's interactions are *different* (synchrony, shift closure, full-path closure, state-locality), so Caleiro's negative remark is about his hierarchy, not TM's.
- **GKWZ Thm 13.3, p.564-565** (synchronous systems over a homogeneous flow: logic = fusion, decidable for the standard flows) and **HvdMV Thm 3.1 (PDF p.8)** (synchrony alone adds no axiom: `C_m^sync` is axiomatised by `S5U_m`): the class `stab` belongs to as an agent. **GKWZ Thm 13.5, p.567-568** and **Thm 13.6, p.568**: the know-time-do-not-forget-do-not-learn logics are the product logics and are decidable by an MSO reduction (note 13.6's flow list is `N`, `Q`, finite orders and first-order-definable classes; `Z` alone is covered by HWZ Thm 15 instead).
- **Why none transfers**: each decides a logic TM/L+ *contains* (F1, F2 give the witnesses `box p -> box G p` and `stab p <-> p`). Decidability is not inherited by extensions; what is needed is a representation theorem for the extension's model class, which is the (W) soundness half the programme already names. The corpus contains no such theorem for a shift-closed, full-path, state-local history set.
- **The "free choice of runs" diagnosis is corrected.** HWZ Def. 11 (PDF p.10-11) defines a run as any function through the state function satisfying the Until/Since coherence; Def. 12 (PDF p.11) makes a quasimodel a state function plus *a* set of runs covering every type; **Remark 13 (PDF p.11)**: "for any two sets of runs `R1` and `R2` in `f`, if `R1 ⊆ R2` and `<f, R1>` is a quasimodel for phi then `<f, R2>` is a quasimodel for phi as well." Taking *all* runs is therefore always available in HWZ. What TM cannot do is choose the state function and the run set independently of a frame: in TM the quasistate at every time is the type set realised by `H_F` at that time -- the *same* set at every time by shift closure -- and the runs are the task-respecting paths of one time-invariant relation whose states carry both the atoms and every `stab` formula (`stab_state_only`, `PlusTruth.lean:332`). That is the condition under which GKWZ/HWZ quasimodel realisation stops applying, and it is the condition the tree's (W) obligation is about.

**F5. The literature's names for TM's three closure conditions. [VERIFIED]**

- Shift closure of `H_F` = GKWZ's agents who "know the time and neither forget nor learn" (section 3.4, pp.137-138; section 13.2, p.566): `box` is that agent with trivial observation, which is why the `box` structure is a product (F1).
- Path closure plus limit closure = the "complete bundle" of the Ockhamist tradition: Reynolds 2003, section 1 (p.356): "Prior's Ockhamist logic is the logic of complete bundled structures", as opposed to "bundled" logics that "restrict evaluation and modal quantification to within a certain specified and fixed set or 'bundle' of histories". In-tree, `blc` (`PlusLimitClosure.lean:243-249`) is documented as "the Burgess/Thomason branch-extension pattern transposed to the stability modal", and `blc_plusValid` (`:258`) is its validity on every regular frame. Reynolds 2003 (p.356) also records that decidability of many branching-time logics "follows from the decidability of the second order monadic logic of trees" -- a tree-based route; TM's `stab` is state-based over a non-tree frame with bi-infinite time, so that remark is lineage, not a result that applies.
- State-locality of valuations = GKWZ's own temporal-epistemic convention (p.135: "A valuation in S is a function from the set of propositional variables into the set `2^S` of all subsets of S", "`(M,(t,f)) |= p` iff `f(t) ∈ V(p)`"). The published decidability results of chapter 13 are nonetheless stated over *all* structures of a class (arbitrary run sets `R`), not over the full-path structures of a frame; TM's run set is fixed by the frame.
