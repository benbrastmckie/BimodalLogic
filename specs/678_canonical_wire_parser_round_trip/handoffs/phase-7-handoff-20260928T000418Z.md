- **Key decisions / deviations**
  - `decodeFormula` requires a formula object's **exact** canonical shape (the tag's own keys, in
    order, and no others) and is therefore structurally recursive — no fuel parameter and no
    monotonicity lemma. That one rule is what closes D2, D8 and a wire-level fresh index together.
    Unknown keys stay tolerated at the envelope, target and lasso levels, as the contract requires.
  - `parse_base_only` is proved from an explicit guard at the end of `decodeCert` rather than from a
    six-level induction chain over the collection decoders. The guard is the contract's
    atom-identity clause made executable at the level where the contract states it, and it is the
    repository's own established idiom (`mkFamily`'s `atomNotBase` fault is likewise vacuous on
    parsed input, as `CertificateImport.lean`'s "Atom shape" docstring already says). Its
    unreachability on decoded input is itself recorded as a theorem, `decodeFormula_base_only`.
  - Formula key order mirrors `DataExport.Formula.toJson` exactly, `"event"` carrying an
    `untl`/`snce`'s second Lean argument and `"guard"` its first.
