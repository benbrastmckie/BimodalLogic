## Overview

Three of the four remaining non-`.Base` rows of `Axiom.minFrameClass` are now sharp, and two of
them come out stronger than sharp. A new module,
`FormalSystem/Metalogic/Independence/DenseRTimeSharpness.lean` (400 lines, 29 declarations,
sorry-free), proves `density` and `dense_indicator` valid at a frame class exactly when that class
is at least `.Dense`, and proves `prior_U_gap` not valid at either class strictly below `.RTime`.
The fourth row, `sep`, ships a *proved* obstruction rather than silence: it is vacuously valid on
every frame with a least positive duration, so no discrete witness for it can exist. The same pass
corrected the two documentation defects the dispatch named, in its load-bearing order — proof work
first, ledger reconciliation last — inside the single budgeted full-tree rebuild.

## Decisions

- **The dispatch's `density` route was not used.** `validOn_dn_iff_denselyOrdered` quantifies
  `∀ F ∀ φ` on its left side, whereas `ValidIn .Base ψ` fixes one `ψ`, so the iff does not
  compose. The route taken is a direct nine-line refutation on `translationFrame` with the
  valuation `A = {x | x ≠ p}`, reusing the `ZTimeSharpness.lean` kit. The `dense_indicator` route
  *is* the one-step iff the dispatch named, because `validOn_neg_nextTop_iff` is stated per-frame.
- **Documentation defect 1 was misdiagnosed in the dispatch, and executing it literally would have
  introduced an error.** `Axioms.lean:16-29` declares three renderings of `untl` and is explicit
  that they disagree on argument order: constructor and infix are guard-first, the prefix `U(e,g)`
  is deliberately event-first, keyed to `Formula.prettyPrint`. Under that convention `prior_UZ`'s
  prose `F(φ) → U(φ, ¬φ)` is *correct*, and so is every prefix rendering on the four axioms this
  task touches — rewriting them would have desynchronised the docstrings from the printer and from
  the machine appendix's `schema_string`. What was genuinely defective was three pieces of
  infix/English prose that reverse guard and event. All four touched axioms' prefix renderings
  were checked against their constructors; no fourth reversed reading exists.
- **Every statement is at `Formula.atom a`, never schematic.** A `∀ φ` non-validity form can be
  outright false (`Axiom.prior_UZ ⊥` has an unsatisfiable antecedent and *is* `.Base`-valid).
- **The frame-versus-model criterion was applied before writing, not discovered mid-proof.**
  `CoNotPriorU.lean`'s obstruction bites only where a statement must *simultaneously validate*
  something on a valuation-rich flow. Every result here is a bare non-validity claim, which
  validates nothing, so all of them are stated frame-level (`¬ F.ValidOn φ`). This is recorded in
  the new module's docstring.
- **No claim was silently upgraded.** The `prior_U_gap` row gets minimality only; nothing is
  claimed about `.ZTime` (incomparable with `.RTime`, not below it), and nothing is claimed about
  `ℚ` specifically even though the clock frame's duration group is `ℚ`.
- **`sep` was excluded up front with a proved boundary**, not attempted and abandoned. The
  `Lex (ℚ →₀ ℚ)` witness is costed at 350-450 lines and is recorded as a follow-up.
