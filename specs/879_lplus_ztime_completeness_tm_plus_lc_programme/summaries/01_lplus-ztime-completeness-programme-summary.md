architecture, the 21-row ingredient table, and the go/no-go verdict — were discharged by the

the report's dependency edges were set, and the two `specs/ROADMAP.md` sentences the report
corrects were amended. Nothing under `FormalSystem/` or `Tests/` changed and no completeness or
decidability claim lands.

### The verdict of record (report finding 4)

GO on T3 — TM⁺ with the until-form schema `LCU` and its mirror, the clock rule, and the two
Auxiliary-Atoms rules — as a staged paper programme whose cheap Lean ingredients land now. NO-GO
on T1 as stated (TM⁺ + `LC_n` + mirror under `RuleSet.none`): it is reduced, not refuted, to two
open questions (Q1: is `lcU α β` derivable from TM⁺ + `LC_n` + mirror at ZTime for state-local
`β`; Q2: are `clock`, `aaFwd`, `aaBwd` admissible), both owned by 901. NO-GO on a Lean proof of
any of T1–T3 today: the engine needs a deterministic ω-automaton for the violation language and
Mathlib at this pin has no ω-automata and no determinization, the same obstruction the standing
MSO-route refusal records.

**Where separation is consumed (finding 2.4)**: exactly once, in the legitimacy of initialising
the two product automata at the cut from the cut colour's past-type and future-type sets
`PT(w₀) × FT(w₀)` — which is sound only because every closure formula's truth at the cut is a
function of the pair `(pt, ft)`, the property `separationOver_ztime` supplies and an unseparated
hue such as `(a U c) S b` breaks.
