## Overview

The paper's Gluing lemma `app:gluing` is now proved in the library in full generality, at the
convex-history layer rather than at any one special case: for a task frame `F` and convex
histories `τ₁, τ₂` whose domains share a point at which they agree, the union is the **unique**
convex history on the union of the domains restricting to each. It lives in the new module
`FormalSystem/Semantics/Gluing.lean` (465 lines, 21 declarations) in namespace
`FormalSystem.Semantics.PartialHistory`, built on `PartialHistory` alone so its import closure
provably cannot reach `Extension/`, where Zorn's lemma enters. The choice-freeness `rmk:choice`
claims for the lemma is asserted mechanically by the C14 gate on every build rather than left as
docstring prose, and the ten existing `app:gluing` citation sites that named only a special case
now also name the general declaration.

## Decisions

- **The three landed special cases are left independent**, with the decision and its reason
  recorded in-source at each. `Presheaf.sheaf_clause` (closed-interval seam) and
  `Presheaf.Ray.seamGlue` (ray seam) are not re-derived from the general construction, because
  `Sheaf.lean`'s Implementation Notes record that `glue_states_le` / `glue_states_not_le` being
  *literally* `dif_pos` / `dif_neg` is load-bearing downstream; the instance claim is discharged by
  witness (`convexGlue_rays_isTotal`, `convexGlue_rays_states_past`) instead.
  `Presheaf.directed_gluing_clause` is independent as a matter of mathematics, not of filing: the
  paper's own rational counterexample shows the directed case genuinely needs *Saturation*, and it
  measures `Classical.choice`
- **`DecidablePred τ₁.domain`** is accepted as an instance-implicit hypothesis — what Lean charges
  for defining a function by cases on a union, in the manner of Mathlib's `Set.piecewise` — rather
  than any of the three escapes, each of which is recorded as rejected in the module docstring.
  `Classical.propDecidable` in particular would reinstate `Classical.choice` and destroy the very
  pin this task exists to land
- **The off-zero reflection law is load-bearing**: `convexGlue`'s two backwards-orientation
  `respects_task` branches close through `TaskFrame.reflection_of_ne`, never the unrestricted
  `FrameOver.reflection`, which closes the same goals and measures `Classical.choice`. This is the
  concrete hazard the C14 rows exist to make fail loudly instead of silently converting a ZF
  result into a ZFC one behind a green build
- `app:gluing` is cited **by name only, never block-quoted**, so its record row stays
  `LIVE-UNPINNED` and no promotion to the pinned manifest is owed
- **Non-goal held**: the limit presentation of `H_F` is a different statement. The docstring notes
  how it relates — a compatible family of sections is a gluing — and nothing is folded in
