## Impacts

- A plan validator and a human reader now reach the same verdict on a plan containing a
  pre-decided `[BLOCKED]` decision gate: it is a documented, reconciled shape rather than an
  unresolved conflict with the final-phase transitive-dependency rule.
- A planner authoring a plan expected to come to rest `[BLOCKED]` now has a documented, sanctioned
  way to express that (mark the phase heading; let the implementer's return carry the outcome for
  postflight to stamp) instead of being tempted to instruct a phase checklist item to hand-set the
  plan-level `Status` field, which an implementation agent is contractually required to refuse.
- No change to the closed six-value phase-heading enum, the `### Phase N: {name} [STATUS]` heading
  contract, the plan-level six-value `Status` vocabulary, or any of their mechanical consumers
  (`update-phase-status.sh`, `update-plan-status.sh`, `update-task-status.sh --phase-check`).
