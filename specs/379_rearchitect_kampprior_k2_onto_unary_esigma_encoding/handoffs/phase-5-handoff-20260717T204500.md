## The genuine remaining Phase-5 work — the Until/Since chain (precise next steps)

**Key structural finding (why this is real work, not mechanical reuse):** the reuse anchors
`VVecEA2.translateRight`/`translateLeft` (and their engines `bracketBuildRight`/`bracketBuildLeft`
+ `chainHolds`, in `NfToVecEA.lean` / `VecEATranslation.lean`) build **bounded, 2-endpoint** nested
Until/Since chains: `VecEA2.holdsRight` (`NfToVecEA.lean:41`) fixes the free var at the RIGHT
endpoint `t`, existentially quantifies the LEFT endpoint `z0 < t`, and asserts a bounded bracket
between — there is **no unbounded before/after**. The Phase-3 `efSat`
(`ExistsForallFormula.lean:100`) differs structurally:
- the single free var (r = 1) is pinned at an **arbitrary interior point** `x (ψ.pin 0)`, not an
  endpoint;
- `intervalType 0` is the **unbounded** past segment (`∀ y < x 0`), and
  `intervalType (Fin.last …)` is the **unbounded** future segment (`∀ y > x (Fin.last n)`).

So Prop 3.5 on the Phase-3 object must build, evaluated at `env 0 = x (ψ.pin 0)`:
1. **pointType at the pin** — `unaryToFormula (ψ.pointType (ψ.pin 0))` — DONE (atomic layer).
2. **rightward future chain** — a nested `Formula.untl` through `x_{pin0+1} < … < x_n`, each step
   asserting the next point's `pointType` and the intervening `intervalType` on the open segment,
   reusing the `bracketBuildRight`/`chainHolds` PATTERN (which is already proven for the bounded
   interior); then a **new unbounded-future terminal cap** asserting `∀ y > x_n, intervalType_last`
   ("henceforth `β_{n+1}`"). The cap is the new content — express it in TL as a `¬untl(⊤, ¬β)`-style
   "always in the strict future" and prove its correctness against the `efSat` after-clause.
3. **leftward past chain** — the `Formula.sinc` mirror of (2) through `x_{pin0-1} > … > x_0`, then
   the **new unbounded-past terminal cap** for `∀ y < x_0, intervalType 0`.

Then `translateProp35 ψ := Formula.and (pin pointType) (Formula.and (rightChain) (leftChain))`, and
the correctness theorem is
`efSat N env ψ ↔ temporal_truth N atomMap (env 0) (translateProp35 ψ)`
(under a surjective `atomMap` onto `(sigE sig F).preds`, the standard hypothesis threaded through
`atom_literal`/`nf_depth0_char_formula`). Lift to the ∨∃∀ layer (`VeeExistsForall`) by mapping the
disjuncts and `Or`-folding (mirror `VVecEA2.translateRight`'s `translateVEF1` wrapper).
