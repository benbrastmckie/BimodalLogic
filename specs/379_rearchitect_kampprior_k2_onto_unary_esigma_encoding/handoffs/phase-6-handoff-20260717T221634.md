## The load-bearing scope decision (READ before Phase 7 consumes this)

`efSat` (Def 3.1) carries **unbounded** universal caps β₀ on `(−∞, x₀)` and β_{n+1} on
`(x_{ψ.n}, +∞)`; `VecEA2.holds` (a purely *bounded* interval predicate) does not. A clean
`efSat ↔ VecEA2.holds` biconditional is therefore **false in general** — the backward direction
cannot reconstruct the caps. Rabinovich's Prop 4.2 canonical 2-endpoint form
`ψ₀(z₀) ∧ ψ₁(z₁) ∧ [bracket](z₀,z₁)` has these caps **vacuously true**. Since no fixed `UnaryType`
is realized at every point of an arbitrary `N`, "trivial cap" cannot be a syntactic constraint; it
is carried as the two explicit **semantic** hypotheses in `EndpointPinnedCapTrivial`. This is the
faithful, in-scope target (HARD PROHIBITION 9 respected: NO generalized/extended `VecEA2` was
built). Phase 7's Negation case must supply `EndpointPinnedCapTrivial` for each disjunct it feeds
here — i.e. every r=2 object reaching negation must be endpoint-pinned with trivially-true caps by
construction. If Phase-3's `Atomic`/`Disjunction`/`Exists` combinators ever produce an r=2 object
with a *non-trivial* cap that must be negated, this bridge does not cover it and that is a new,
separate concern (escalate — do not extend `VecEA2`).

## Proof-engineering notes (transfer to Phase 7 Fin-bridging)

- `Fin.castSucc`/`Fin.succ` compose with `Fin.mk` by `rfl`; `i.succ.castSucc` is defeq
  `⟨i.val+1, _⟩` — used a `show`/defeq to convert the `efSat` between-clause index.
- `by omega` inside a **type** (a `have`-statement conclusion or a `set`-lambda) does NOT see the
  arrow-hypothesis binders reliably. Fix used: a **total** witness accessor
  `w' : Nat → carrier := fun m => w ⟨min m k, by omega⟩` (unconditional bound) plus a tactic-level
  `hw'val : ∀ m (hm : m < k+1), w' m = w ⟨m, hm⟩` (Fin proof passed by name, no type-level omega).
  This is the clean pattern for gluing a `Fin (ψ.n+1)` chain out of two endpoints + a
  `Fin (k+1)` interior. Non-dependent `ite` else-branches also lose the guard hypothesis; the
  clamp side-steps that too.
- Segment-index arithmetic (`bracket.segmentTypes ⟨j⟩ = efIntervalTP (intervalType ⟨j+1⟩)`) is
  handled by `(efIntervalTP_eval …).mp hseg` (defeq through the beta-reduced `segmentTypes` field)
  then a `congr 1; apply Fin.ext; simp only; omega` index-equality `rwa`.
