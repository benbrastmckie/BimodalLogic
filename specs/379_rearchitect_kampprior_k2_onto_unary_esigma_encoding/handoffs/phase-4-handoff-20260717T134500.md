## What remains for Phase 4
1. **Lemma 3.2(2) backward** — `conjSat N env (target) → efSat N env ψ`. THE crux. Two obligations
   (both documented in the module docstring §"backward direction"):
   - **Existence content.** `pairwiseProjections` alone is NOT a sound backward target: over `r=0`
     free vars it is the empty conjunction (`conjSat … [] = True` vacuously) yet an ∃∀-*sentence*
     need not be satisfiable. The backward target must additionally carry the chain-existence claim
     — in Rabinovich these are the **singleton (1-free-var) ∃∀-formulas** pinning one point and
     asserting the surrounding chain. Add a singleton-projection constructor and fold it into the
     target (a richer `ConjExistsForall` that also holds arity-≤1 conjuncts, or a product of
     `pairwiseProjections` with a list of singleton projections).
   - **Piecewise chain gluing.** Given the pair chains, partition the `n+1` ordered points into the
     maximal gaps between consecutive *pinned* positions; the free-var pair spanning each gap gives
     a chain segment with correct unary point/interval types on that gap; glue along shared pinned
     endpoints using `LinearOrder N.carrier` (`N.carrier_order`). StrictMono across a shared pinned
     point `p`: left chain gives `a_{p-1} < a_p = env`, right chain gives `env = b_p < b_{p+1}`.
     Interval types transfer verbatim: each glued open interval coincides with an interval of the
     spanning pair's chain (point/interval type data is shared across all projections). Expect a
     substantial `Fin`-indexed piecewise construction (likely 300–800 lines); consider proving it
     first for `pin` injective+monotone (WLOG normal form) to cut cases.
2. **Lemma 3.2(1)** — conjunction ≡ disjunction of ∃∀ via order-preserving interleavings (merge two
   ordered chains; disjoin over all interleavings of the two point sets; finite). Combinatorial.
3. **Lemma 3.4 ∧-closure** — follows from 3.2(1) (distribute-then-merge). Blocked on 3.2(1).
