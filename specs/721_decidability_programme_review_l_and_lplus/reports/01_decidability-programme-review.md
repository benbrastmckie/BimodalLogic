The existing `FMP/` directory is not a semantic FMP at all (permissive relation, zero `TruthAt`, truth lemma false), and the open item it recorded ("build a genuine filtered task relation and re-discharge the four axioms") is unachievable on a finite carrier over Z.
- It does **not** threaten Z-time decidability, because the landed route never needed FMP: `exists_witnessFamily_of_not_validZTime` compresses an *infinite* Z-model into a finitely presented one (a `ShiftSet` on carrier Z read off labelled lassos), exactly GKWZ's "infinite models with regular structure built from repeating finite pieces" (`ztime-no-finite-carrier-fmp.md` §8-9; Krommes 2020 for K4 × S5 as the published example that FMP failure and undecidability are independent, with the caveat that TM is not a product logic).
