## Impacts

- `FrameOver.static_of_countable` is now citable library-side, and with it the reading the
  research phase recorded for the manuscript: over `ℤ` finite frames have rich dynamics; over `ℚ`
  finite frames are static and the countable non-static ones are clocks; over `ℝ` even countable
  frames are static, and the only non-static frames have uncountably many states that behave like
  clock readings. The axiom that does the work over `ℝ` is *Limit* (closed level sets) together
  with *Saturation* (the total history through `thm:extension`).
- `ForMathlib/Topology/` is a new upstreamable subtree. `Sierpinski.const_of_countable_range` is
  stated in Mathlib's shape and is the natural upstream candidate; it is independent of
  everything else in this repository.
- `Correspondence/` now has import edges to `FormalSystem.Semantics.Extension` and
  `FormalSystem.ForMathlib.Topology`, both confined to `RigidityReal.lean` and both recorded in
  the directory README's `## Dependencies`.
