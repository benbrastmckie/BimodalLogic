## Overview

`FormalSystem/Semantics/Presheaf/Path.lean` (new, 55 declarations, 1,554 lines) builds the
instance of Johnstone's sheaf/Conduché correspondence at `X = Beh F`: the path category `Path(F)`
with the world states as objects and the sections of `Beh F` with their endpoints as morphisms
(`PathHom`, `PathCat`), its length functor `PathCat.len : Path(F) ⥤ BD⁺`, the proof that `len` is
a discrete Conduché fibration (`PathCat.len_isDiscreteConduche`), the proof that the associated
presheaf of `len` is `Beh F` **as an equality** (`PathHom.assocRestrict_sec`, by `rfl`), and the
`D = ℤ` clause in two layers — sections over `[0, n]` are the adjacency-respecting windows
(`beh_int_iff_adjacent_window`), and `Path(F)` is the free category on the one-step graph
`(W, ⇒₁)` (`PathHom.pathEquiv` with `toPath_id`/`toPath_comp`). The HARD SCOPE LIMIT holds:
`fact:conduche-equivalence` is cited and external, the general "associated category"/"associated
presheaf" notions are instantiated and not defined, and the module docstring says so. Every
declaration measures `[propext]`, `[propext, Quot.sound]` or axiom-free; none reaches
`Classical.choice`.

  every comparison of sections made at the `.val` level, where `Beh.restrict`'s order proofs are
  irrelevant; `glue`'s seam `dite` never unfolded outside `Sheaf.lean`.
- `Mathlib.CategoryTheory.Functor.Basic` imported minimally; all three instances on synonyms
  (`BObj`, `PathCat`, `StepGraph`), none on a cluster type; `assert_not_exists` lock untouched.
- `IsDiscreteConduche` stated in general so `len` can be shown to satisfy it; no general
  construction from a fibration or a sheaf (the HARD SCOPE LIMIT). No phase found the general proof
  cheap: extracting data from a general `∃!` needs choice.
- The `D = ℤ` layer routed through `taskRel_natCast_iff_iter` and the choice-free `reflection_int`;
  `ofStep` built from `Beh.ofAdjacentWindow`; endpoint arithmetic by `decide`/`omega`/core
