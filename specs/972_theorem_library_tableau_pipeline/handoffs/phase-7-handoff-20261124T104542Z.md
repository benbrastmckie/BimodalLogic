## Three defects this phase found and fixed

1. **ADMIT under-implemented the ADMISSION rule.** The plan specified self-subsumption among
   candidates only. A single fold is order-dependent (a later, more general entry does not
   displace an earlier one), and nothing compared against the hand-curated registry at all. The
   invariant caught 4 of 10 entries on its first real run. Fixed with a two-pass `admitStage`
   plus `--registry-statements`, fed by
   `specs/evidence/theorem-library-pipeline/registry-statements.lean`.
2. **`simpa` closes an arity-2 entry through `Classical.choice`**, which the plan's own validation
   criteria forbid on a registry row. Replaced by an arity-aware
   `simp only [substF, <subst>, reduceIte] at h` + `exact h`.
3. **The generated module exceeds the 1,500-line limit** (2,053 lines for six entries, because a
   fully explicit concrete derivation is hundreds of lines). The emitter now pins a
   `set_option linter.style.longFile` to the file's own length, after the module docstring, which
   the style linter requires to be the first command after the imports.

## One open limitation, recorded not fixed

CERTIFY's tableau rebuilds are untimed: `--timeout-ms` bounds `decideOne` only, and
`--deadline-s` is checked at the top of each loop iteration, so one formula can hold the whole
budget. That is what truncated ModalTemporalBoolean. Records are also written only after the
loop, so killing a stalled run loses every record. Both are follow-ups for Phase 8 to name:
give CERTIFY its own `IO.asTask` budget, and flush records incrementally.
