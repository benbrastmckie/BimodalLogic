## Three decisions a successor must not undo

1. **Emitted lines wrap at 98 columns.** `linter.style.longLine` is enforced at 100 characters
   package-wide. Measured: zero lines over 100 anywhere under `BimodalTools/` or `BimodalLib/`,
   then confirmed from a live build warning ("exceeds the 100 character limit"). Fully explicit
   formula source passes 100 on any formula of interest, so unwrapped output would make Phase 7's
   generated module red under `--wfail`.
2. **Emitted declaration names are camelCase** (`fooConcrete`, `fooSubst`), never underscored:
   `C26` flags a non-trailing underscore in a live `def`/`abbrev` name and the `env_linter` sweep
   over the non-`FormalSystem` lakefile roots carries a pinned finding ceiling.
3. **In-module assertions are `example ... := by rfl`, not `#guard`.** `lean_lib BimodalTools`
   carries no `linter.hashCommand` opt-out — only `BimodalTest`, `BimodalToolsTest` and
   `BimodalLibTest` do — so a `#`-command in a `BimodalTools` module is a warning and CI builds
   `--wfail`. `#guard` remains correct inside the test module.
