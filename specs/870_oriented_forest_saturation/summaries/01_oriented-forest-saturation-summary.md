## Overview

`FormalSystem.Semantics.saturation_of_orientedForest` is landed: every relation whose underlying
undirected **multigraph** is a forest satisfies the literal `TaskFrame.Saturation` predicate at
`ofStepRel` over `ℤ`, from `IsOrientedForest E` as its only hypothesis — no finiteness, no
fibre-shape restriction, and no forest data structure. The `hgen`-parameterised variant
`saturation_of_orientedForest'` serves a consumer holding a relation only *equal* to `ofStepRel E`.
All eight phases are complete, every declaration is sorry-free, and the measured axiom profile of
both headline theorems is `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` — exactly
`TaskFrame.saturation_of_finite`'s own, so the lemma adds no new axiom cost at either consumer.

## Decisions

- **Merge-point uniqueness is proved target-free.** `mergePoint_unique` concludes `c = c'` from two
  divergent merges of the same pair with **no** common target, by transferring `c'`'s support
  membership across the walk `walk_unique` identifies (`Walk.mem_support_append_iff`) and then
  applying `meet_approach_disjoint`. The pinned `merge_unique` (which does carry a common `z`) is
  derived from it, with its three length identities falling out of `iter_length_unique`. The reason
  is recorded under Plan Deviations: the plan's phase-7 independence step needs the target-free
  form.
- **The duration minimum ranges over all representations of all members** —
  `Nat.find` over `{n | ∃ a, {u | iter E n a u} ∈ S}` — and the selected member is proved
  `⊆`-**least**, not merely minimal, so
  `TaskFrame.sInter_nonempty_of_directed_of_minimal`'s obligation is discharged ignoring its
  `T ⊆ Sstar` premise.
- **The reductions and the bridge are stated over an `hgen` hypothesis** relating a task relation
  on `ℤ` to the one-step relation, with `ofStepRel_gen` discharging it from
  `ofStepRel_of_nonneg`/`ofStepRel_of_nonpos` and `seg_subsingleton` landed as the `ofStepRel E`
  corollary the plan names. The primed headline theorem is what needs that generality.
- **Reduction C absorbs the all-backward case.** Once members of both signs occur the family is
  closed outright, so only "all forward" and "all backward" remain, and `isOrientedForest_swap`
  turns the second into the first. The selection core is therefore stated in `iter` vocabulary
  rather than against a task relation.
- **`FrameOver.ofStep`'s `saturation` field is left unchanged**, still routing through
  `TaskFrame.saturation_of_finite`. Rewiring it is a separate decision with its own blast radius.

## Plan Deviations

  index arithmetic. **This repairs a real gap in the plan's phase-7 route**: that step compares the
  merge of `(a_m, a')` at target `x` with the merge at target `y`, which have no common target, so
