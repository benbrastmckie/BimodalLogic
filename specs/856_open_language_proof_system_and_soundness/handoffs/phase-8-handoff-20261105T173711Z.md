## Key decisions

- The two seriality schemata are proved **directly** from `exists_gt` / `exists_lt` rather than
  transported through `openValid_ofPlus_iff` as `StarAxiomValidity.lean` does. Both are closed
  formulas, so transport would work, but the direct proof is two lines and keeps the whole block
  discharged against `OpenTruthAt` uniformly, avoiding a higher-order `ofPlus ?φ =?= ...`
  unification at each use.
- Block aggregates are stated in `OpenAxiom` **constructor** order, not in the order the plan's
  task bullet lists the names: `serial_future`/`serial_past` come first in the inductive. This
  matches the convention Phases 6-7 established, which Phase 11's `obtain` will rely on. Each
  aggregate's docstring enumerates its covered names.
