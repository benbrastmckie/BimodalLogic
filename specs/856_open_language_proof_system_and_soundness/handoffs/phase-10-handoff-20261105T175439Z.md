## Key decisions

- **The five discrete-uniformity schemata transport** through `openValid_ofPlus_iff` from
  `plusAxiom_validIn_min`, not proved directly. Each records a *frame-level* uniformity fact
  (a regular task frame's discreteness is symmetric, propagates, is modally necessary), proved
  once at the L level; a direct L^▷ proof would re-derive frame reasoning rather than transcribe
  a truth-clause argument. The `PlusFormula` argument is passed **explicitly** at each of the five
  sites rather than left to unification, since `ofPlus ?φ =?= <concrete OpenFormula>` is a
  higher-order problem.
- **`openValid_reflectTime` covers every `.Base` reflection half, including `paste`/`untl_paste`.**
  This is where this plan's architecture pays off over Star's: because the helper is stated as
  `OpenValid (schema).reflectTime` and the transport takes the *original* purity hypotheses, no
  reflection lemma for `IsPureFutureOpen`/`IsPurePastOpen` is owed. Star, which lands on the
  mirror schema instead, needs `hφ.reflectTime` at both pasting arms.
- The eight class-restricted schemata could **not** use it (`openValid_reflectTime` is stated at
  the unconstrained class only). Four route to their past twin at the reflected parameter
  (`prior_UZ`↔`prior_SZ`, `prior_U_gap`↔`prior_S_gap`) — and these typecheck by `rfl`, with no
  `simp` normalisation needed, because every `reflect_time_*` identity involved holds definitionally.
  `z1`, `density`, `dense_indicator` and `sep` have no twin and their duals are proved directly.
- `dense_indicator` and its dual are proved **directly** from density rather than transported;
  two lines each, and it avoids a second higher-order `ofPlus` unification site.
- The `sep` and `sep_reflect` arms consume `SoundnessLemmas.sep_order` / `sep_order_mirror`
  unchanged at `P := {u | OpenTruthAt M τ u φ}`. Nothing about `OpenFormula` enters those lemmas.
