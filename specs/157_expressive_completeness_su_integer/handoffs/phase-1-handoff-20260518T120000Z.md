### Why Strategy B

Strategy A hit the multi-U-type barrier: `neg_until_equiv` introduces U' = U(~A ^ ~B, ~A), creating
two distinct U-types. The `d21_sep` infrastructure was built for single-U-type formulas and could not
handle the D3 sub-branches where both U and U' appear.

Strategy B avoids this entirely by decomposing S(a ^ ~U, q v U) directly without introducing U'.
The resulting formula has only the original U(A,B) type, making all sub-cases reduce to Cases 3 and 5.
