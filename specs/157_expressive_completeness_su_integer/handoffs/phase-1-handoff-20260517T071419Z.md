## The Blocker (Root Cause)

Our `is_U_free` predicate (used in `is_syntactically_separated`) only checks for absence of `untl`. It does NOT check for absence of `all_future`. This means a "U-free" formula like `all_future(atom a)` can appear inside S-arguments of a separated formula.

In GHR94's framework:
- `all_future(phi) = neg(U(neg phi, True))` -- derived from U
- "U-free" in GHR94 means "no U and no G(=all_future)" = "pure past"
- Our "U-free" means only "no untl" -- allows all_future

This breaks the quantifier case proof: the substitution of R-atoms (r_lt -> True in past region, etc.) is only correct if past parts evaluate ONLY at times <= t. But `all_future` inside an S-argument causes evaluation at times > t where r_lt should be False, not True.
