### Key Insight: all_future/all_past in snce/untl Arguments

The most significant finding: `all_future (neg A)` is NOT U-free in the 6-constructor language because it expands to contain `untl`. This means formulas like `S(a AND G(NOT A), q)` are NOT syntactically separated, breaking the Case 2 proof strategy. The fix (implemented for Eliminations.lean) is to factor:

```
S(a AND G_s(NOT A), q) at t  <==>  S(a, q AND NOT A) AND NOT A(t) AND G_t(NOT A)
```

This same pattern will be needed for DedekindZ Case 6 proofs.
