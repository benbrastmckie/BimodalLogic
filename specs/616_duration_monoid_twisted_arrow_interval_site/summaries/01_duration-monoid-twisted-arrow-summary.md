## Decisions

- **Concrete structures, no `Mathlib.CategoryTheory`** — followed `Site.lean`'s recorded decision
  rather than re-deciding, and wrote the reconciliation into `Duration.lean`'s Implementation Notes:
  Mathlib has none of the four notions needed (twisted-arrow category, factorization category,
  factorization-linearity, the coverage), `SingleObj` is multiplicative where `BD⁺` is additive, and
  the concrete route keeps the module buildable from `Site.lean` alone. The point at which to weigh
  the Mathlib route again is named: the path category and Conduché fibrations.
- **Keep the pinned names `Tw.coverLeft`/`Tw.coverRight` and record the C23 shadow pair** rather
  than rename: the names are pinned Challenge statements (statement fidelity), `Site.lean`'s own
  note asks the categorical packaging to share the site's vocabulary, and the pair is a mirrored
  API whose agreement `toTr_coverLeft`/`toTr_coverRight` assert — the gate's own documented case for
  its recorded set.
- **`Tw` coverage indexed by the cut point** `p` with `q := l - p`, so both transport equations
  close by `rfl` with no transport — the research's resolution, confirmed by `#print`.
- **Footprint reporting**: the plan expected `≤ [propext]` throughout; measured, ten declarations
  are `[propext]` and fourteen are `[propext, Quot.sound]`, the `Quot.sound` entering through
  Mathlib's `sub_nonneg` exactly as in `Site.lean`'s `coverRight` and `Tr.comp_assoc`. Reported as
  choice-free with the measured sets (the property the commuted cancellation secured) rather than
  escalated as a blocker; no proof was changed to chase the tighter bar.
