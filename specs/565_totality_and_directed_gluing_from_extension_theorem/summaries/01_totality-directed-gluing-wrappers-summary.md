## Overview

Both remaining clauses of `app:presheaf-dictionary` are now proved in Lean, in one new module, as
**wrappers** on `thm:extension`: translate a section onto its window, extend to a possible world,
restrict back. The task's explanatory point — that the Extension Theorem was the presheaf
appendix's analytic content all along — is carried by a measured choice record rather than by
assertion: because each clause is stated as a binder-free *engine* taking the extension property as
an explicit hypothesis, the `Classical.choice` in Totality is **attributable** exactly to
`thm:extension`, while Directed Gluing's is demonstrably **doubly** sourced. `PartialHistory` and
the `Extension/` cluster were not touched.

## Decisions

- **Parameterize on the extension property, instantiate afterwards.** This is what converts the
  axiom measurement from an observation into an attribution, and it is what makes the
  minimal-hypothesis variants one line each.
- **Measure the Directed Gluing engine too.** The plan's record had four rows and did not include
  `directed_gluing_of_isRestriction`. Measuring it gives the sharpest available statement of the
  contrast: the Totality engine is choice-free, the Directed Gluing engine is not, *even with the
  extension property hypothesized away*, because `directedSup` is independently non-constructive.
- **Say that Saturation-free is not choice-free.** `totality_of_isZTime` and
  `totality_of_completion` eliminate *Saturation* but still measure `Classical.choice`, because
  `extension_of_completion` routes through Zorn. The docstring states this rather than letting the
  weaker-hypothesis claim be misread.
- **`states_eq_of_eq` imported, not restated.** A same-name local copy is a hard
  duplicate-declaration error; the plan's probe-confirmed correction to the research
  recommendation was followed.
- **`add_le_add (le_refl p) h` throughout**, never `(add_le_add_iff_left p).mpr h`. The
  measured engine rows are the evidence this held.
