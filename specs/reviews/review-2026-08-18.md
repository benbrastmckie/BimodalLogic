## Recommendations

**Answer to the question as asked: no large infrastructure item stands in front of the strong
completeness work.** The one that looks like it might — task 450 — belongs to the decidability
front and can be sequenced independently. What is worth clearing first is small, and most of it is
clerical rather than mathematical.

Suggested order:

1. **Fix ROADMAP.md's Sorry Inventory and "BXCanonical DEAD CODE" sections** (Critical). One
   editing pass. Until this is done, the repository's own roadmap tells anyone approaching this
   work that its foundation is abandoned.

2. **Re-record the 7 stale `#guard_msgs` expectations** (High, but bounded). Diagnosis confirms
   stale expectations, not a regression — the engine is behaving as intended and in one respect
   more correctly than the recording. One focused session (1–2 hours), mostly prose. Worth doing
   before the spine so 421/422 land against a working gate, but it is not a blocker in the sense
   the raw red implies.

3. **Re-anchor the six strong-completeness task descriptions by symbol name** (Medium). Tasks 169,
   362, 421, 422, 423, 424 have no research artifacts, so the drifted description is the entire
   dispatch input. Cheapest possible intervention with a direct effect on the work you want to do.

4. **Re-issue task 424 against total-history semantics** (High). Its design is built on a deleted
   parameter, and it is the compactness feasibility gate. Either re-scope it or explicitly park it
   — but do not dispatch it as written.

5. **Then start the spine at 421**, which its own description calls *"two deliverables ... both
   small"* — a comment correction plus a carrier probe mirroring an existing, still-accurate
   pattern. Run **423** in parallel: it is vocabulary-only, self-contained, unblocks two downstream
   branches, and touches none of the same files.

6. **Defer** 450, 451, 193, 437 and the stalled `dataset-enhancement` trio. None of them stand
   between you and the strong-completeness terminus.

**A note on where the real risk sits.** After the clerical items above are cleared, the binding
constraint on this front is not infrastructure — it is task 422's own flagged unknown: *"it has NOT
been verified that the chronicle's block order can always be densified without disturbing MCS-chain
coherence."* That is the item most likely to cost a research cycle, and it sits at position two of
a four-deep sequential chain whose terminus (169) closes the repository's only remaining sorry.
Consider dispatching 422 as research before planning the rest of the spine.
