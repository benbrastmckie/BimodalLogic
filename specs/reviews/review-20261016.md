`ladder-joint-index-refutation.lean`, 1580 lines, sorry-free, registered in

meaning the mechanism that was supposed to separate the pair has no witness to run on); a sixth

quantifies over sub-objects a formula's *letters* can name, but a formula can carve out a
sub-object no set of atoms carves out, by forbidding a *pattern*. **Every index shape the
governing guidance anticipated is now spent.** What survives is not a positive lemma but a
strictly stronger necessary condition on any future candidate's modulus.
