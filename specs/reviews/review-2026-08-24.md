### C-2. A landed, sorry-free, computable decidability layer is orphaned and absent from every roadmap

**Files**: `FormalSystem/Metalogic/Decidability/BiLasso/` (19 files, 6,593 lines);
`scripts/module-invariants-manifest.txt`; `specs/ROADMAP.md`

**Description**: Task 417 (archived completed, 2026-08-18) landed a bi-lasso decision layer whose
main results are:

- `SatAtState P w φ := ∃ τ (hτ : τ.IsTotal) t, τ.states t (hτ t) = w ∧ TruthAt P.toModel τ t φ`
- `check_correct : check P w φ = true ↔ SatAtState P w φ`
- `instDecidableSatAtState` — *"the `Decidable` instance, with no `Classical.dec"`*
- a genuine truth lemma `truth_along_annot`, a small-model theorem `exists_annot_of_truth`, an
  explicit closed bound `cycleBound`, and hand-proved non-vacuity witnesses

It is sorry-free. It is also **unreachable**: nothing imports `BiLasso.lean` except itself, and all
20 modules sit in the C6 known-unreachable manifest. `specs/ROADMAP.md` — whose title is still
*"BX Completeness and Publication"* — does not mention it.

**Impact**: Task 468's audit finding F6 concludes *"FMP is syntactic, not semantic"* on the basis of
`Decidability/FMP/`. That conclusion is **too strong**: it did not account for `Decidability/BiLasso/`,
which is semantic and computable. The consequence is that the project's own tracking prices
decidability at *"several person-years"* via tableau while a substantially shorter route sits landed
and unwired.

**Recommended fix**: Scope and create the bridging task. The missing theorem is a bounded
finite-presentation model property:

> `¬ValidDiscrete φ → ∃ (P : IntPresentation) (w), P.card ≤ f(φ) ∧ SatAtState P w φ.neg`

Two facts make this materially easier than it looks, both verified this review:

1. `ValidDiscrete` (`Semantics/Validity.lean:222`) quantifies over `D` with
   `SuccOrder`/`PredOrder`/`IsSuccArchimedean`/`IsPredArchimedean` — i.e. over carriers that are ℤ
   up to order-isomorphism.

   **CORRECTION (2026-08-24, same day): the claim originally made here — "the carrier half of the
   bridge is not open work" — was WRONG, and `FormalSystem/Semantics/IntNormalForm.lean` says so
   explicitly.** An *order* isomorphism is insufficient: durations ADD, and `TaskRel`'s
   *Compositionality* is stated at `x + y`, so "a transfer that only preserves order is not enough
   to carry a frame across; the additive iso is the one that is actually needed."
   `orderIsoIntOfLinearSuccPredArch` fits the binder bundle verbatim but delivers only an order
   iso. The needed lemma is `LinearOrderedAddCommGroup.int_orderAddMonoidIso_of_isLeast_pos`
   (`D ≃+o ℤ`), which does NOT fit the bundle: `Archimedean D` does not synthesize from
   `[IsSuccArchimedean D] [IsPredArchimedean D]` (order-successor conditions, not the additive
   Archimedean property), and it additionally needs an `IsLeast {y : D | 0 < y}` witness.

   `IntNormalForm.lean` states the route precisely: supply `Archimedean D` and the least-positive
   witness (the successor structure produces the latter). `Semantics/DurationClassification.lean`
   already carries the companion `archimedean_of_lub` for the Dedekind branch; **the discrete
   branch needs the successor-based analogue, which is not in the tree.** That is one named,
   well-scoped lemma of genuinely open work — small, but not zero, and it is a prerequisite of
   anything that reasons about the discrete class, completeness included.
2. What remains is therefore a finite-`WorldState` model property over ℤ, plus extraction of an
   adjacency-matrix presentation — a filtration argument, not a tableau programme.

**One caveat that must be written into the task**, and is the reason this is a proposal rather than
a claim: `FormalSystem/Semantics/Extension/PeriodicExtension.lean` states explicitly that *"No bridge
from the first to the second is written anywhere"*, because extracting `Fin n`-data from a
`Prop`-level `Finite` is `Classical.choice` *"in its most literal role"* and would produce a
non-computable presentation. That objection is aimed at a different theorem (extending a *given*
history). For deciding validity, the existential over bounded presentations may be classical while
the *decision procedure* enumerates data — but the task must confirm this rather than assume it, and
must state which side of the constructive line each half falls on.

## Addendum 2: the "bridge" decomposed, and why most of it is not a bridge

Prompted by the question of whether a bridge theorem can be refactored away rather than proved.
What Addendum 1 and the body called "the BiLasso bridge" is **three distinct gaps**, and they have
different answers.

### Gap 1 — carrier normalization (arbitrary discrete Archimedean `D` → ℤ)

**Not a bridge. A missing semantic fact that should exist regardless.** See the CORRECTION under
C-2: the needed lemma is the discrete analogue of `archimedean_of_lub`, supplying `Archimedean D`
plus a least-positive-element witness so that
`LinearOrderedAddCommGroup.int_orderAddMonoidIso_of_isLeast_pos` applies. `IntNormalForm.lean`
names the route and records that it is not in the tree. Small, bounded, and a prerequisite for any
reasoning about the discrete class — not decidability-specific.

### Gap 2 — the finite model property (arbitrary `WorldState` → finite)

**Not a bridge. This IS the content of decidability**, and it cannot be refactored away. A
decidability result for a logic requires either an FMP or a proof-theoretic decision procedure;
there is no third option and no reformulation that removes the obligation.

### Gap 3 — representation reconciliation (`FiniteTaskFrame` vs `IntPresentation`)

**This is the only genuine bridge, and it IS avoidable.** `PeriodicExtension.lean` states the
duplication outright: `TaskFrame.extend_periodic` and `IntPresentation.extend_periodic` are "two
theorems, not one, and deliberately so" — the same content proved twice because one is `Prop`-level
and one is data.

The route to eliminating it rests on a fact verified this review:

- The existing filtration's world-space is **already the right shape for data**.
  `filteredCharacteristicSet_injective` (`FMP/FiniteModel.lean`) embeds `FilteredWorld phi` into
  `Finset (subformulaClosure phi)`, and `filtered_world_bound` (`FMP/FMP.lean`) gives the explicit
  bound `Nat.card (FilteredWorld phi) ≤ 2 ^ (subformulaClosure phi).card`. The quotient is by
  agreement on a **finite decidable** set, so it is computably enumerable.
- `PeriodicExtension.lean`'s `Classical.choice` objection is therefore **not** an objection to this
  construction. It concerns extracting `Fin n` from an *arbitrary* `Prop`-level `Finite`. A
  filtration quotient by agreement-on-a-finite-set is not that.

**The existing filtration must be rebuilt anyway**, for reasons independent of representation:
`FMP/README.md` records the task relation as
`refinedFilteredTaskRel := fun w d u => if d = 0 then w = u else True` — **universal at nonzero
duration**. *Spherical* and *Limit* hold on it "*because* the relation is universal", and a truth
lemma is false on it (`someFuture χ` separates the sides). Confirmed: zero occurrences of `TruthAt`
in the entire `FMP/` directory.

**Therefore**: build the rebuilt, non-permissive filtration so that it lands in `IntPresentation`
directly — world-space as a `Finset` of closure-assignments, relation as the adjacency matrix.
Then the filtration's output *is* `check`'s input definitionally, `check_correct` becomes the final
step rather than the far side of a transfer, and **no bridge theorem exists to prove**.

This does not make the work cheap. Re-discharging all four `def:frame` axioms for a non-universal
filtered relation is the multi-month piece and is unavoidable either way. What the refactor buys is
that you do not *also* carry a transfer lemma on top of it — and it retires the
`extend_periodic` duplication as a side effect.

### Correction to Addendum 1's A-7

A-7 relayed the audit's judgment that this theorem "serves both fronts". That is **too broad** and
is corrected here. It serves the FMP result and the decidability front. It does **not** unblock
`countermodel_discrete`: task 169's terminus is a **Base**-class countermodel over the
**non-Archimedean** carrier `ℚ ×ₗ ℤ`, a different frame class and a different carrier, reached by
tasks 421→422. The completeness and decidability fronts remain genuinely independent.

### Consequence for task 469

469 should be re-scoped from "scope and price the bridge" to the sharper question:
**can the non-permissive filtration be constructed to land in `IntPresentation` directly, so that
no transfer lemma is needed?** Same investigation, better target, and a positive answer removes an
artifact rather than adding one.
