## Summary

The review answers one question — *are all elements of the completeness proofs finished?* — and
assesses the documentation that describes the answer.

**The proofs are in excellent shape. The documentation is not.** Every defect found is a
documentation defect, and they run in **both** directions:

- **Top-level `README.md` under-claims.** It describes a weaker, more incomplete proof state than
  the tree has — advertising sorries that do not exist and marking two proven frame classes as
  pending or absent.
- **The subdirectory `FormalSystem/**/README.md` files over-claim** — the repository's historical
  failure mode. `FormalSystem/README.md:289-290` states decidability is "fully proven" when only
  its sound direction exists; `Metalogic/Algebraic/README.md:32` marks a **nonexistent file's**
  "Main completeness theorem" as *Sorry-free*; `Metalogic/WeakCanonical/README.md:44-47`
  advertises three declarations that exist nowhere in the tree, one of them a completeness result.

The in-file module docstrings are the reliable layer: `Metalogic.lean`, `Decidability.lean`,
`WeakCanonical.lean` and `StrongCompleteness.lean` are all careful, current, and correctly apply

## Answer: Is Completeness Finished?

**Weak completeness is finished — all four frame classes, unconditionally, sorry-free, axiom-clean.**
Finite-context consequence completeness is likewise finished for all four. But `Context :=
List Formula`, so that layer is inter-derivable with weak completeness through the deduction
theorem; the repository is explicit and correct that it is **not** strong completeness.

**Genuine strong completeness (arbitrary `Γ : Set Formula`) is proven for zero classes**, and its
status splits three ways that must not be collapsed:

| Class | Weak completeness | Consequence (finite `Γ`) | Strong (`Γ : Set Formula`) |
|-------|-------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Base** | PROVEN — `BXCanonical/Completeness.lean:196` | PROVEN — `StrongCompleteness.lean:535` | **OPEN** — gated on `CompactBase` |
| **Dense** | PROVEN — `BXCanonical/Completeness.lean:255` | PROVEN — `StrongCompleteness.lean:639` | **OPEN** — gated on `CompactDense` |
| **Discrete** | PROVEN — `BXCanonical/Completeness.lean:296` | PROVEN — `StrongCompleteness.lean:746` | **REFUTED** — `DiscreteNonCompactness.lean:280` |
| **Dedekind** (Continuous) | PROVEN — `StrongCompleteness.lean:469` | PROVEN — `StrongCompleteness.lean:450` | **NOT STATED AT ALL** |

Soundness counterparts all present and axiom-clean: `soundness` (`Soundness.lean:1080`),
`soundness_dense` (`:1254`), `soundness_discrete` (`:1400`), `soundness_dedekind` (`:1927`).

**The completeness theorems are done and clean; the completeness programme is not.** What remains
is compactness (`CompactBase` / `CompactDense`) and the decision procedure's completeness
direction — neither of which is a completeness proof in the weak sense.
