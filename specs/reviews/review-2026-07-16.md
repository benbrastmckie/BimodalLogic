### But the residual hardened

The remaining sorry was re-adjudicated on 2026-07-15 (`KampPrior.lean:517-561`;
`specs/377_.../reports/06_kampprior-520-adjudication.md`) on machine-checked evidence:

- **It is an arity cap, not a missing lemma.** The consumer `kampPrior_site_rungK_gate_match`
  (`:948`, general in `k`, axiom-clean) *carries* rather than discharges an `hreal` obligation whose
  conclusion is an **arity-4** joint type over `(x1, w, x, t)`, guarded only by a lossy **unary**
  point type at `w`.
- **The unary producer is faithfulness, not a defect.** Rabinovich caps arity everywhere the method
  touches: Def 3.1 (p.4) one variable; Lemma 3.2(2) (p.4) ≤2 free variables; Def 4.1 (p.5) unary
  E[Σ]. There is no arity-4 joint type in the paper's 16 pages. **The arity-4 consumer is the
  off-paper party.**
- **The escape hatch is machine-checked closed.** `chain_split` needs a path-shaped constraint
  graph; `AtomKind.order` (`NormalForm.lean:60`) gives an arity-4 NF an order atom on every ordered
  pair — the complete graph K₄ — so cutting at anchor `w` separates nothing. Verified both
  directions, axiom-free (`reports/05_chain-split-arity4-nonapplicability-probe.lean`).
- **The existing arity-4 producer is dead.** `kampPrior_hreal_supply`
  (`InteriorHrealSupplyK.lean:61`) is landed but **unwired**, machine-confirmed **circular**
  (`InteriorGateGeneralK.lean:1541`), and **refuted at fiber level**
  (`ExteriorPinnedProbeM1K.lean:816`).

### The fact that sizes the problem

`kamp_prior_expressive_completeness` sets `k := psi.quantifier_depth` (`KampPrior.lean:709`) — an
**arbitrary** depth. `nf_characterizable_temporal_prior` inducts over `k` and re-enters
`nf_nvar_exist_all_depths … k 1` at every successor step. So the `k ≥ 2` arm is genuinely
value-reached for every formula of quantifier depth ≥ 2, and — unlike the `n+2` arm — **it cannot be
domain-restricted away**.

Stated plainly: **as of today the Kamp theorem is formalized for formulas of quantifier depth ≤ 1.**
That is a real theorem and the k=0/k=1 machinery is axiom-clean, but it is not Kamp's theorem.
