## Answer to the review question

### What L⁺ is

L⁺ ("the stability-modal language") is L plus `⊡` — the Logos counterfactual fragment with trivial
constitutive antecedents: `□φ := ⊤ □→ φ`, `⊡φ := ¬⊥ □→ φ`. Lean carrier: `PlusFormula` /
`PlusAxiom` / `PlusDerivationTree` under `FormalSystem/PlusLanguage/`. `⊡`'s state-locality is the
anchor every decidability engine in the tree rests on. Authority for mathematical status is
`FormalSystem/Metalogic/Decidability/RaySummary/README.md`'s (C)/(S)/(W) verdict table; `specs/ROADMAP.md`
is the batch/ruling layer above it.
