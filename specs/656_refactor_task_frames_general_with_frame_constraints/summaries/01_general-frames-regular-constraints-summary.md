## Overview

`FormalSystem.Semantics.FrameOver` is now the **general** task frame — a nonempty state type and
the primitive relation on the positive cone, and nothing more. `def:frame`'s four constraints
(*Compositionality*, *Seriality*, *Limit*, *Saturation*) left the structure and became the
`Prop`-valued class `FrameOver.IsRegular`, with `TaskFrame.IsRegular` delegating to the fibre;
the class satisfying all four is the class of **regular** frames, and the qualifier is dropped
thereafter exactly as the paper drops it. Every pre-existing theorem keeps its name and its
statement, now at `[F.IsRegular]`, so no downstream module changed meaning; two results whose own
docstrings recorded that they need less were generalized (`eq_of_taskRel_zero_of_limit`,
`rev_sep_of_limit`) with the original names derived from them. On top of the split, the state
topology landed as `Semantics/StateTopology.lean` (the cone-neighbourhood topology `𝒩_F` as the
sole `TopologicalSpace` instance, `FrameOver.t1Space_iff_limit`), and
`Semantics/StateTopology/Counterexamples.lean` carries the frames the split exists to make
statable — chief among them the four-state funnel, the acceptance test.

## Decisions

- **The constraints are a class, not a bundled `Prop` field.** `class FrameOver.IsRegular` taken
  as an instance binder is what keeps every consumer site byte-identical (`F.comp`, `F.serial`,
  `F.limit`, `F.saturation` keep their names, types and spelling), which was the whole point of
  requirement (3).
- **`FrameOver.IsRegular`, never bare `IsRegular`.** Mathlib's `IsRegular` (cancellable monoid
  elements) and `RegularSpace` are unrelated and must not be shadowed, and
  `Mathlib.Topology.Separation.Basic` comes into scope in `StateTopology.lean`.
- **The general constructor keeps the bare name.** `ofReflective` is the 3-argument general
  constructor and `ofReflectiveRegular` the 7-argument regular one, per requirement (1).
- **`𝒩_F` is the only `TopologicalSpace` instance.** `𝒯_F` stays a plain `def` so a state space
  never carries two topologies; the user-ratified option (a).
- **Import weight, not instance pinning.** `StateTopology.lean` and its counterexamples stay out
  of the `Semantics.lean` aggregator, on the carried-in decision's stated preference.
- **`variable {F : TaskFrame} [F.IsRegular]` is unusable** under `--wfail`: automatic section
  variable inclusion plus `linter.unusedSectionVars` makes every non-consuming declaration a fatal
  warning. Every regularity binder in the tree is per-declaration.
- **Instance synthesis does not see through a frame definition.** Each named frame built by
  `ofReflectiveRegular` carries an explicit `instance X_isRegular`, whose *term* elaboration does
  unfold `X`. Twenty such instances were added.

## Impacts

- A frame satisfying some `def:frame` constraints and not others is now expressible, which is what
  the topology results, the sep-without-*Limit* fact and the rigidity sharpness witnesses need.
  `Semantics/Correspondence/Rigidity.lean`'s bare-relation workaround is now the architecture.
- Every state space carries exactly one `TopologicalSpace` instance (`𝒩_F`), so downstream
  topology work has a canonical choice and no diamond at the state type.
- Consumers are unchanged in meaning: validity quantifies over the regular frames exactly as
  before, and `F.comp` and its siblings read at every use site as they did.
- The `Preorder ℤ` import-weight fragility inherited from the countable-ℝ rigidity task is held off
  by keeping the topology-carrying modules out of `Semantics.lean`; the generated root imports
  them directly.
