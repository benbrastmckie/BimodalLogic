## Root Cause Analysis

The sorry requires transferring depth-(k'+1) arity-3 existentials between environments `[y, x, t]` in M and `[y₀, x₀, t₀]` in M₀. The environment is non-constant (x /= t) so `ih_exist` (ExistPart) cannot express this directly -- it only handles constant-parent envs `Fin.cons y (fun _ => z)`.

### Approaches Attempted and Why They Fail

1. **nf_extend_fwd/bwd chain**: From depth-(k'+2) 1-var at x/x₀, get depth-(k'+1) 2-var at [y,x]/[y₀,x₀]. From this, nf_extend adds z at depth-k' 3-var at [z,y,x]/[z₀,y₀,x₀]. But z₀'s relationship to t₀ is uncontrolled. Extending from [y,t] gives a DIFFERENT z. No way to pin the SAME z to all pairs.

2. **Pairwise 2-var composition**: Atom part works (proved in Phase 1). Quantifier part fails: the 4-var existential at [z,y,x,t] depends on z's relationship to ALL three of y,x,t, but nf_extend from one pair doesn't constrain z relative to the other pair.

3. **ih_exist at constant parent**: Produces formulas for `∃ y, nf_eval [y, z, z]` (constant parent z). Neither `z=x` nor `z=t` gives `[y, x, t]`. No combination of two ih_exist calls (one at x, one at t) produces the non-constant env.

4. **Formula enrichment (Eq-zone pattern)**: The Eq zone works because x=t makes `[y, x, t] = [y, t, t]`, exactly matching ih_exist's constant-parent form. For Until/Since (x /= t), this env reduction is impossible.

5. **nf_skipIdx_cross**: Already proved in KampBypass.lean. Goes from (n+1)-var to n-var agreement (projection). The reverse direction (composing n-var projections into (n+1)-var) does not hold.

### The Correct Fix

The formula `char_kp1(nf_t₀) AND (char_kp1(nf_x₀) Until top)` is TOO WEAK. It encodes only 1-var NF types at x and t, not the full 2-var NF at [x,t]. The enriched formula must encode the quantifier conditions DIRECTLY.

**Recursive formula construction** (Rabinovich Prop 3.5 pattern): For each ssn : NF (k'+1) 3, encode `sub_nf.2 ssn` as a temporal formula conjunct. The encoding is zone-dependent:

- **y = x or y = t zones**: Reduce to 2-var conditions, encodable as formulas at x or t respectively
- **y > x zone**: Temporal formula at x for "∃ y > x with matching NF" -- requires encoding [y,x,t] conditions via [y,x]-projection + recursive non-constant env handling for t-interactions
- **y < t zone**: Temporal formula at t for "∃ y < t with matching NF" -- same structure
- **t < y < x zone (between)**: Most complex -- requires encoding existence of y with matching NF in the interval, using nested Until/Since with the between-zone guard

At each zone, the 3-var NF conditions decompose into:
1. Atoms (orders + predicates): determined by zone + 1-var NF type of y
2. Depth-k' quantifier conditions: involve 4-var existentials on [z,y,x,t] -- same non-constant env problem at one depth lower

The recursion terminates because depth decreases while arity increases, bottoming at depth 0 where everything is purely atomic (handled by the k=0 infrastructure's VecEADecomp + KampBypassUntil/Since).
