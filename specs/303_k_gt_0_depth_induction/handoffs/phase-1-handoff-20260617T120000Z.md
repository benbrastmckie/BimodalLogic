- `ih_exist` characterizes `exists y, nf_eval M k (n+1) (Fin.cons y (fun _ => t)) sub_nf'` with CONSTANT parent `(fun _ => t)`
- For Until zone (t < x), the 3-var NF at [y, x, t] has non-constant tail [x, t]
