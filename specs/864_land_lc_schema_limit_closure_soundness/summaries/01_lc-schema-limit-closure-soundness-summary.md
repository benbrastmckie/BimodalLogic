## Decisions

- **Home module.** Landed in `FormalSystem/PlusLanguage/` beside `PlusLimitClosure.lean`, not in
  `FormalSystem/Metalogic/NamingRules.lean` as the dispatch tentatively suggested. The research
  confirmed the other answer and the plan fixed it: `NamingRules.lean` is about rules that
  discharge an auxiliary letter, which LC_n does not do, and staying inside `PlusLanguage/` adds
  no upward import edge (confirmed: `check-metalogic-cycles.sh` reports the recorded 8 upward
  lines unchanged).
- **`omega_chain`'s recurrence family is a state-time predicate, not a formula family.** See
  Plan Deviations.
- **The cofinality hypothesis is an explicit binder, not hard-wired to `intOrder`.** The
  ℤ-dependence of the whole route is therefore isolated at one auditable place, and the kernel
  stays reusable if the dense case is ever settled.
- **`int_marker_exists_gt` is stated at `ℤ` rather than at a frame's duration carrier.** `omega`
  reads the syntactic type of a hypothesis and does not see through `↑intOrder = ℤ` (documented
  in `Semantics/TemporalOrder.lean`'s own `intOrder` docstring), so the marker arithmetic is done
  once at `ℤ` and transported by defeq at the single application site.
- **No `LCU` declaration was invented.** LCU is identified with the n = 1 instance of `lcN` and
  recorded as such in the schema module's docstring.

## Plan Deviations

- **Phase 3, `omega_chain`** altered: the recurrence family is the probe's own state-time
  predicate `A : ℕ → F.Duration → F.WorldState → Prop`, not the plan Challenge's
  `A : ℕ → PlusFormula`. The Challenge's shape is not provable: the splice (`paste`) replaces the
  history strictly *before* the new marker, so truth of `A (k+1)` cannot be transported from the
  continuation history to the pasted one for an arbitrary formula — only a condition reading the
  time and the state there survives. The landed statement is **strictly stronger at every
  binder** (any formula family instantiates it; `hstart` additionally allows the starting point
  to lie on any history through the evaluation state rather than on the evaluation history
  itself), so no weakening was introduced, and the plan explicitly pre-authorises
  `omega_chain`'s recurrence-family bundle as the adjustable binder shape. The `M : TaskModel F`
  argument and the probe's unused segment-property parameter `P` are both absent, matching the
  Challenge's own conclusion shape.
- **Phase 2, `omega_limit`** altered: the Challenge's `[F.IsRegular]` instance binder is dropped.
  `WorldHistory.ofTotal` needs no frame constraint, regularity enters one layer up at `stg_step`
  where `paste` uses *Compositionality*, and the `unusedArguments` env linter (C16) reported the
  binder as a new finding beyond `scripts/nolints.json`'s baseline. Removing a hypothesis
  strengthens the statement.
- **Phase 6 ledger scope** altered: 12 rows and 12 C14 probe lines, not the 7 the plan's Scope
  Hypothesis estimated. The plan directs this explicitly ("adjust the row count to match what is
  there rather than to match this number"); the extra five are the probe's own decomposition
  lemmas (`marker_mono`, `marker_agree_add`, `int_marker_add_le`, `int_marker_exists_gt`,
  `stg_step`), which the plan required be kept as named lemmas rather than inlined. The four
  definitions (`bigAnd`, `lcAnte`, `lcCons`, `lcN`) carry no row, as planned.
- **Scope beyond the plan's enumerated files**: `scripts/measure-refactor-partitions.py`,
  `ORGANISATION.md`, `typst/generated/status.typ`, `FormalSystem/README.md` and `README.md` were
  also edited. Each is mandated by a gate for a new file in a language directory
  (`LANGUAGE_FILE_LAYERS`, the layer-1 list, the typst count sync, the generated inventory
  blocks); none is a scope widening of the deliverable.

  nowhere in the landed content. The obstruction is precise and recorded in both new modules'
  docstrings: the ω-chain's markers must be cofinal in the duration order, which fails over a
  dense order, and the library's existing Zorn route does not generalize past n = 1 because a
