## Decisions

- Carriers and derived operators are `def`, not `abbrev` (the challenge block's `abbrev`s exist
  only to survive the snapshot tool); `ofPlus`, `RegFree` and `ofFormula` live inside their
  formula namespaces, as the challenge pins.
- `QuantRecurrence.lean` imports `QuantTruth`, `Semantics.HistoryMorphism` and
  `Semantics.Extension.Extension`, not `QuantInvariance`: it uses nothing from it.
- `standard_not_invariant` instantiates the two letters at `Atom.mkFresh "" 0` and
  `Atom.mkFresh "" 1`, distinct by `decide`.
- The Blackburn-de Rijke-Venema citation is limited to what the held scan of §7.3 supports
  (point nominals, the satisfaction operator); the binder `↓` is not attributed to it.
- Manuscript passages are cited by `\label` plus a quotable phrase verified verbatim against the
  manuscript source ("nothing prevents a world state from occurring at many times in a single
  history", "as in chess games which transpose move order", "taking world states to be strictly
  ordered prevents the same world state from occurring more than once in any history"); no line
  number and no task number appears outside `specs/`.
- Axiom profiles were measured with `#print axioms` on a scratch file rather than through more
  LSP workers, after the build guard reported memory pressure once.
- Commits carry the session line from `git-commit-scoped.sh` plus the attribution trailer, added
  by a message-only `git commit --amend --only` on the agent's own HEAD commit.
