## Key decisions taken in this phase

1. **The plan's D4 role-word predicate under-covers, and was widened.** Measured 204
   mandatory-tier lines across 43 files against the plan-time hypothesis of 115/22. More
   importantly, two whole classes of stale line carry **no role word** and were therefore
   invisible to the predicate:
   - **Constant-form constructor expressions** in comments: `untl φ (imp bot bot)` for
     `someFuture`, `untl φ bot` for `next`, `(untl φ.neg ⊤).neg` for `allFuture`. The code-only
     rewriter never touched comments, so every such expression went stale. 79 candidates scanned,
     59 corrected.
   - **Paren-form renderings**: `untl(γ, β∧xi)`, `snce(alpha, beta')`. 343 candidates scanned
     across 14 files, 53 corrected.
   Each correction was checked against the argument order of the declaration it describes, not
   applied by pattern.

2. **Three renderings coexist and all three are now named where they appear.** The constructor is
   guard-first (`untl(g, e)`); the prefix `U(e, g)` rendering (`prettyPrint`, `asUntil?`'s returned
   pair) is event-first; the paper's and manual's infix `φ U ψ` is guard-first. Comments that
   previously said "Burgess convention" without saying *which* rendering were rewritten to name
   the rendering explicitly rather than deleted — `PriorDefsDense.lean:41`,
   `TemporalDerived.lean:701`, `Tableau.lean:311`/`:324`, `Formula.lean:508`/`:512`.

3. **`RRelation.lean` needed zero edits.** The plan flagged `RRelation.lean:1527` as one of two
   hard cases. Re-derived: its cross-notation mapping ("In Xu's notation U(event, guard), so Xu's
   `U(γ, β)` = our `untl(β, γ)`") was already stated guard-first and matches the migrated code
   (`Formula.untl β₁ γ` at `untl_conj_guard`, `Formula.untl β γ` at `burgessR`). All 79 of its
   paren-form comment lines were already guard-first.

4. **`Lemma53FaithfulPast.lean:191` was wrong in the *opposite* direction** — it read
   `kMinus P = (snce ⊤ P.neg).neg` where the definition is `(snce φ.neg Formula.top).neg`. Fixed
   to `(snce P.neg ⊤).neg`, matching `KPlusFaithful.lean:169`. Note `kPlus`/`kMinus` genuinely do
   take the operand in the *guard* position, so `(untl P.neg ⊤).neg` is correct for them and is
   **not** a stale `allFuture`-shaped expression.
