## Key Decisions

- **`Prop`-valued, not `Bool` with a bridge.** The plan bullet offered either. `Prop` is what the
  `Anchored` fields and the two vacuity lemmas consume directly, and — decisively — this phase's
  own verification criterion is spelled `#eval decide ((…).BoxStabFree)`, which only typechecks
  when `BoxStabFree` is a `Prop` with a `Decidable` instance. Hence the hand-rolled
  `decidableBoxStabFree`, which keeps every check computed with no `Classical` on that path.
- **Divergence from the inheritance bullet's reuse hint, flagged not hidden.** The bullet said to
  route through `subformulas_trans` / the per-arm membership lemmas. Neither is usable:
  `subformulas_trans` is a statement about membership and says nothing about `BoxStabFree`, and
  the eight per-arm lemmas CONSTRUCT a membership where this proof must DESTRUCTURE one — the
  opposite direction. The landed proof is an induction on `φ` mirroring `subformulas_trans`'s own
  arm-for-arm shape, destructuring with `simp only [subformulas, List.mem_cons, List.mem_append]`
  exactly as that proof does, so no project lemma is re-derived — which is what the hint was
  protecting. Statement and decomposition are exactly as pinned, nothing is unproved, and no
  alternative approach was substituted, so the phase is not marked `[BLOCKED]`. Annotated inline
  in the plan and logged in `issues.jsonl`.
- **The two modal arms carry the content.** In `boxStabFree_of_mem_subformulas` the `box` and
  `stab` cases need no membership reasoning at all: `BoxStabFree (.box _)` is `False`, so `hφ`
  discharges them via `absurd hφ not_false`. That is also why
  `not_stab_mem_plusSubformulaClosure` is proved outright rather than by any symmetry with the
  `box` case — the two go through different arms.
- **Trialled standalone before editing.** The whole section was elaborated against the
  already-built `Subformulas.olean` via `lake env lean` in the scratchpad and came out clean on the
  first attempt (only the scratch `#eval` atoms needed fixing — `Atom` is a structure with
  `base`/`freshIndex`, not a `Nat`), so the real module was edited exactly once. Same discipline
  Phase 3 recorded as a win.
