## Key Decisions

- **The valuation is an existential over the closure-membership proof**, not a `dite`. An atom
  holds at `(z, q)` iff it is an atom of `φ`'s closure AND the presentation labels `q` with its
  letter; off the closure it holds nowhere. Membership in `plusSubformulaClosure φ` is a `Prop`,
  so any two proofs are definitionally equal and `clLetter φ h'` IS `clLetter φ h` — which is
  why `modelOf_valuation` is `⟨fun hv => hv.2, fun hl => ⟨h, hl⟩⟩` with no transport and no
  `Subsingleton` appeal. A `dite` would have needed `DecidablePred` plumbing and a
  `dif_pos` rewrite at every use for no gain.
- **Off-closure atoms are false, and that is a free choice `Anchored` does not constrain.**
  `Anchored.atom` quantifies over `h : PlusFormula.atom p ∈ plusSubformulaClosure φ`, so it says
  nothing about an atom `φ` never mentions. Making those false is the choice that keeps the
  valuation a per-state datum of the presentation with no extra parameter; the docstring says so
  explicitly rather than leaving the reader to infer it.
- **The relation is inline, not a named helper.** The plan writes
  `FrameOver.ofSlicedStep (fun _ q q' => step q q' = true) fwd bwd`, and a named `stepRel`
  abbreviation would have been a declaration the plan's `## Lean Challenge Statements` does not
  carry. `IsSlicedStepPath` beta-reduces at this relation, which is why `frameOf_mem_HF_iff`'s
  right-hand side can be spelled `∀ t, step (g t) (g (t + 1)) = true` and still be closed by
  `FrameOver.ofSlicedStep_mem_HF_iff _ _ _ f` outright.
- **`open` is section-local.** `FormalSystem.Syntax` (for `Atom`) and `FormalSystem.Semantics`
  (for `FrameOver`, `TaskModel`, `WorldHistory`, `intOrder`) are opened inside
  `section Constructed` rather than added to the module's top-level `open` block, so Phases 2-5's
  elaboration environment is bit-for-bit unchanged.
- **The `(frameOf hC).WorldState` projections needed no coercion help.** `w.2` in the valuation,
  `(z, q)` in `modelOf_valuation`, and `p q : ℤ × Q` in `frameOf_step` all unified with the
  `ofSlicedStep` carrier definitionally — no `show`, no `change`, no `TaskFrame.WorldState`
  rewrite. This was the one mechanical risk in the phase and it did not materialize.
- **Trialled standalone before editing, as Phases 3-5 did.** The whole section plus the
  `inferInstance` check was elaborated against the already-built oleans via `lake env lean` in
  the scratchpad and came out clean on the FIRST attempt with no diagnostics at all; the Phase 7
  consumption contract was then checked in a second scratch file. The real module was edited
