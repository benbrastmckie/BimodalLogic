## Overview

The `Rigidity.lean` scope note — which asserted, with nothing proved, that the rigidity boundary
for *time-indexed* frames is Dedekind completeness rather than the Archimedean property — is now
two compiled modules. `FormalSystem/Semantics/TimeIndexed.lean` defines the time-indexed frame
structure at the frame-correspondence program's own field names and proves that over a densely
ordered, Dedekind-complete time order a finite-stated frame satisfying *Limit* has only constant
histories. `FormalSystem/Semantics/TimeIndexedSharpness.lean` witnesses both hypotheses: the
two-state ℚ frame switching across `√2` (Dedekind completeness cannot be dropped) and a ℤ frame
switching across `0` (density cannot be dropped), together with the duration-vs-time comparison
table. Both hypotheses of the positive theorem are therefore load-bearing and separately refuted.

## Decisions

- **Dedekind completeness enters as a Prop-valued `hlub` binder**, not a
  `ConditionallyCompleteLinearOrder` instance, following `DurationClassification.lean`'s stated
  convention. No topology is imported and no `OrderTopology` instance is created; the
  "connectedness" argument is run as an explicit least-upper-bound walk.
- **The positive theorem is stated at exactly the hypotheses its proof consumes.** Its docstring
  records what it does *not* use: *Compositionality*, *Seriality*, *Saturation*, the converse
  convention, unboundedness, topology.
- **Field names diverge from `FrameOver`'s on purpose** and the module docstring says why: this is
  shared infrastructure whose other intended consumer is the manuscript's Theorem A.
- **The analysis import is confined to the sharpness module** and, as the build proved, must also
  be kept out of the `Semantics` aggregator — see Plan Deviations.

## Reported Correction

The task description's gloss — the positive theorem shows the frame "has only constant histories,
i.e. is static" — **overshoots, and this is reported rather than silently accommodated**. Those
are two different claims. `constantHistories_of_lub` concludes `ConstantHistories`; it constrains
only pairs of states that some history realizes and cannot by itself refute a permitted transition
no history witnesses. `static_of_lub_of_realized` reaches `Static`, but only from two further
named hypotheses — a reflexivity law and a realization law — stated as hypotheses precisely
because neither follows from the others. The `Rigidity.lean` scope note itself said only "force
constant histories" and was accurate as written; its replacement keeps that phrasing.

## Impacts

- The `Rigidity.lean` scope note is discharged: the claim it recorded is now two theorems and two
  witnesses rather than an unproved remark.
- `TimeIndexed` is shared infrastructure the manuscript's frame-correspondence Theorem A can
  consume unchanged, at `TaskFrame`-weight dependencies.
- A latent fragility is now documented rather than merely avoided: any module that can see
  `Int.instConditionallyCompleteLinearOrder` may silently lose computability of an `ℤ` order
  construction. The constraint is recorded at both the aggregator bullet and the witness module.
