## Context & Scope

The manuscript's subsection `sub:RestrictedModalities` introduces three restricted modals over the constructed possible worlds `H_F`:

- Stability: `⟨τ⟩_x := {σ ∈ H_F | σ(x) = τ(x)}` -- quotable: "the set of possible worlds that intersect τ at x".
- Open Futures: `|τ⟩_x := {σ ∈ H_F | σ(y) = τ(y) for all y ≤ x}`.
- Open Pasts: `⟨τ|_x := {σ ∈ H_F | σ(y) = τ(y) for all y ≥ x}`.

with the clauses `M,τ,x ⊨ ▷φ iff M,σ,x ⊨ φ for all σ ∈ |τ⟩_x` and `M,τ,x ⊨ ◁φ iff ... for all σ ∈ ⟨τ|_x` (macros `\Openfuture` = boxed `▷`, `\Openpast` = boxed `◁`, `\Stability` = boxed dot, `\Nomic`). The manuscript then asserts, in prose, exactly the facts deliverable (1) formalizes: "`|τ⟩_x ⊆ ⟨τ⟩_x` and `⟨τ|_x ⊆ ⟨τ⟩_x` where `|τ⟩_x ∩ ⟨τ|_x = {τ}`", and "`|τ⟩_y ⊆ |τ⟩_x` and `⟨τ|_x ⊆ ⟨τ|_y` for `x ≤ y`, so that moving forward in time narrows the open futures and widens the open pasts". It closes with "I will omit further consideration of the restricted modals `⊡`, `▷`, `◁`, and `⊚`" -- the sentence the new module docstring should quote when recording the nomic operator, the world registers and any axiomatization as out of scope.

Only stability is formalized today (`PlusTruthAt`, `Semantics/PlusLanguage/PlusTruth.lean`). Two 559 research rounds conflated `⊡` with Ockhamist historical necessity; the corrected reading (report 04 section 2) is that Ockhamist necessity is `▷`, because a tree moment determines its past while a task-frame state does not. The 559 probe `04_semantics-native-general-duration.lean` section `HN` proves this in a Mathlib-only mirror; this task sites it against the live definitions.

### The design decision (argued, not assumed)

Two options were named by the dispatch; a third (put `▷`/`◁` into the generic clause classes) is ruled out by the one-directory siting and by the contract's own grain note.

**Option A -- extension language.** `OpenFormula` with nine constructors (`atom bot imp box untl snce stab ofut opast`), `OpenTruthAt` with nine clauses (seven copied from `PlusTruthAt`, plus `ofut φ ↦ ∀ σ, AgreeUpTo τ σ t → OpenTruthAt M σ t φ` and `opast φ ↦ ∀ σ, AgreeFrom τ σ t → ...`), instances `TruthEnv`/`StabClauses`/`PointTruth` (all fields `Iff.rfl`), `ofPlus` with `openTruthAt_ofPlus` (seven `Iff.rfl`-shaped cases, the `plusTruthAt_ofFormula` pattern), `reflectTime` swapping `untl`/`snce` and `ofut`/`opast` with its involution, and the reversal transport `openTruthAt_rev` (nine cases). Cost: one inductive, three inductions of 7-9 trivial cases each, three instances; every derived-operator lemma and every validity notion is inherited. Estimated 450-600 lines including docstrings across `OpenFormula.lean`, `OpenTruth.lean`, `OpenValidity.lean`. Gains: deliverable (3) is literally `∀ α, OpenValid (hnOpen α)` and `¬ sinkFrame.OpenValidOn (hnStab p)`; deliverable (5) is a list of validities and `¬ OpenValid` refutations; `▷` nests under tenses for free (`P▷̂α` is a formula); every L⁺ refutation transfers through `openValid_ofPlus_iff`; the open-past mirror is one lemma `openValid_reflectTime` applied to each open-future validity.

**Option B -- semantic operators.** `OpenFuture (P : WorldHistory F → F.Duration → Prop) τ t := ∀ σ, AgreeUpTo τ σ t → P σ t`, likewise `OpenPast`, `Stab`; results stated for `P := fun σ s => PlusTruthAt M σ s φ`. Cost: ~200-250 lines for the classes and operators. Losses: (3) becomes a meta-level schema over `M`, `φ` -- not a validity of any formula and not a `¬ Valid` refutation; the Ockhamist consequent `P▷̂α` needs a semantic `somePast` operator on predicates, i.e. the tense clauses rebuilt as set operators (the truth recursion without its syntax); S5 for `▷` and the strength ordering are statable but only pointwise; deliverable (4) still requires a reversal transport for `PlusTruthAt` (seven cases) to say anything about the tense operators under `◁`, and that induction does not exist either, so the saving over A is only the inductive, `ofPlus`, and `reflectTime`, roughly 150 lines.

**Recommendation: Option A.** It is the only option meeting the dispatch's "(3) stated as a validity and a refutation", and its marginal cost over B is bounded because the two extension contracts were built for exactly this. Two consequences to record in the directory README: the inductive lives under `Semantics/` (a deliberate departure from the `Syntax/X` + `Semantics/X` mirror, justified by the one-directory constraint and by the absence of any proof system for L^▷ -- axiomatization is out of scope), and `AgreeUpTo`/`AgreeFrom` are reused from `PlusPasting.lean` rather than redefined (single definition, C17-clean), with the class module importing `PlusPasting`.

## Decisions

- Recommend Option A (extension language `OpenFormula` in a `Semantics/OpenLanguage/` directory) over semantic operators; rationale in "The design decision".
- Reuse `AgreeUpTo`/`AgreeFrom`/`paste` from `PlusPasting.lean`; do not redefine the relations.
- The refutation frame is `FrameOver.ofStep` on the three-state sink relation (the dispatch's finite-state integer-time frame, Saturation from `saturation_of_finite` inside `ofStep`), declared as an `abbrev`; the `NF` refutation is recorded as a fallback and as the witness for `▷φ → ⊡φ` failing.
- Time reversal is done semantically with `FrameOver.rev` (probe 02) and one nine-case transport, citing `lem:time-reflection`; the alternative of hand-proving each open-past theorem (the `PlusSoundness` pattern) is recorded as the fallback if `openValid_reflectTime`'s `rev_rev` bookkeeping proves awkward.
- No `user_decision` is raised: the design choice was delegated to research by the dispatch and the artifacts determine it.
