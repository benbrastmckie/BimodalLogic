## Overview

`plan-format.md` marked the per-phase `Files to modify` field REQUIRED, but
`validate-artifact.sh` had no check for it, so a plan omitting the field everywhere validated
clean while `file_scope` stayed `null` — invisible to the orchestrator's admission overlap gate.
This implementation closed the gap with a two-tier presence check in `validate-artifact.sh`'s
existing per-phase loop (per-phase omission warns, `--strict` enforces; whole-plan omission
errors unconditionally), repaired the one live whole-plan offender
(`internalize_tableau_branches_and_prove_routine_rule_admissibility`'s plan) as the regression
fixture and backfilled its `file_scope`, and documented the enforcement level and its promotion
criterion in `plan-format.md`. The harvester's `[]`-is-normal contract was left untouched, exactly
as planned.
