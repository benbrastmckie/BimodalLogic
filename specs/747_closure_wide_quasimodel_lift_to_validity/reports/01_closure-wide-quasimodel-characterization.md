## Findings

### Codebase Patterns

**F1. `box` is a per-model datum, not a per-time one (compiled).** `evidence/01_box-time-invariant.lean`:

> `theorem box_time_invariant (M : TaskModel F) (τ : WorldHistory F) (t s : F.Duration) (ψ : PlusFormula) : PlusTruthAt M τ t (.box ψ) ↔ PlusTruthAt M τ s (.box ψ)`
> `theorem box_global (M : TaskModel F) (τ σ : WorldHistory F) (t s : F.Duration) (ψ : PlusFormula) : PlusTruthAt M τ t (.box ψ) ↔ PlusTruthAt M σ s (.box ψ)`

Proof: `box_iff` twice, then `plusTruthAt_timeShift` on `σ.timeShift (s - t)` and `add_sub_cancel`. Axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` (observed). This holds on EVERY `TaskFrame`, not only regular ℤ-frames: the history set of a task frame is shift-closed by construction. Consequence for (W): `Anchored.box`'s `∀ z` quantifier is redundant, the per-time slice quantification the record names does not exist in this semantics, and the `box`-letters of any closure-wide object are `|{box ψ ∈ cl φ}|` global bits. (The README's "modelOf's box anchor is time-invariant across the whole slice" is thus not a degeneracy of `modelOf` -- it is true of every model.)

**F2. `stab` is a per-world-state datum (in tree).** `stab_state_only` (`PlusTruth.lean`): `τ.state t = σ.state s → (⊡φ at (τ,t) ↔ ⊡φ at (σ,s))`. Consequence: the cell of a world state `w`, `cell(w) := {tp(σ, 0) : σ.state 0 = w}` where `tp(σ,z) := {ψ ∈ cl φ : PlusTruthAt M σ z ψ}`, is time-independent, and the `stab`-letters at `w` are read off `cell(w)`: `⊡ψ ∈ tp(σ,z) ↔ ∀ t' ∈ cell(σ.state z), ψ ∈ t'`. The set of cells `𝒞_M := {cell(w) : w}` is a subset of `𝒫(𝒫(cl φ))`, finite with a bound depending on `φ` alone.

**F3. The anchor layer of any model satisfies two fixpoint equations, by the truth clauses alone.** With `B_M := ⋃ 𝒞_M` (all realized types):
- (box) `∀ t ∈ B_M, (box ψ ∈ t ↔ ∀ t' ∈ B_M, ψ ∈ t')` -- from `box_iff` + F1;
- (stab) `∀ C ∈ 𝒞_M, ∀ t ∈ C, (stab ψ ∈ t ↔ ∀ t' ∈ C, ψ ∈ t')` -- from `stab_iff` + F2;
- (Hintikka) `bot ∉ t`, `imp ψ χ ∈ t ↔ (ψ ∈ t → χ ∈ t)`.
These are the closure-wide counterparts of `Anchored.box`/`Anchored.stab`/`LocalRun.bot`/`LocalRun.imp`, stated on types rather than on one seam's colouring. They are decidable on a concrete finite `𝒞` (finite quantifiers over finite sets of finite sets), and the completeness direction -- every regular ℤ-model's `𝒞_M` satisfies them -- is a direct unfolding, not an induction. This is the part of (W) that is cheap, bounded and non-vacuous.

**F4. (S)'s frame shape is incomplete for `box`/`stab`-bearing L⁺, by a one-line corollary (compiled).** `evidence/02_modelOf-finite-width-incomplete.lean`:

> `theorem modelOf_not_sat_Φ (hC : Cons φ lab step) (τ : WorldHistory (frameOf hC).toTaskFrame) (t : ℤ) : ¬ PlusTruthAt (modelOf φ lab step hC) τ t NoFiniteWidth.Φ`
> `theorem modelOf_incomplete : ¬ PlusValidZTime NoFiniteWidth.Φ.neg ∧ ∀ {φ lab step} (hC : Cons φ lab step) τ t, PlusTruthAt (modelOf φ lab step hC) τ t NoFiniteWidth.Φ.neg`

The first is `NoFiniteWidth.no_finite_width_sat` at `R := fun _ q q' => step q q' = true`, `fwd := hC.serialFwd`, `bwd := hC.serialBwd`, `M := modelOf …`, with `[Finite Q]` from `[Fintype Q]`; the second pairs it with `not_plusValidZTime_neg_Φ`. Axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` (observed). Reading: ranged over ALL `Cons` presentations, `modelOf` never refutes `Φ.neg`, a ℤ-time non-validity inside the CTL-like fragment. No clause added to `Cons` changes this, because the obstruction is the carrier shape `ℤ × Q` with `Q` finite (finite width), not the labelling. So (W)'s soundness half cannot be "(S) at every seam"; it needs a frame constructor whose per-time slice is infinite.

**F5. Trace-level `Presented` collapses `⊡` at the seam under `nowUnique` (compiled).** `evidence/03_stab-collapse-presented-nowUnique.lean`:

> `theorem stab_iff_of_presented_nowUnique (M) (τ) (t) (φ) (hP : Presented M τ t φ lab step) (hU : ∀ q q', lab q (clNow φ) = true → lab q' (clNow φ) = true → q = q') {ψ} (h : ψ ∈ plusSubformulaClosure φ) : PlusTruthAt M τ t ψ ↔ PlusTruthAt M τ t (.stab ψ)`

Proof: every history `σ` in the seam fibre is `spliced τ t bf` for some ray pair (`exists_spliced_eq`, extracted from `plusStab_iff_omega`'s ⇐ half), so by `Presented` its closure trace is some anchored run's `runTrace`; `IsRun` puts `now` at `ρ t`, `hU` identifies the `now` states of any two such runs, and the letter at `t` is then the same label for `τ` and for `σ`. Axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` (observed). Reading: `Cons.nowUnique` was DERIVED as necessary for `presented_modelOf`'s ⇐ direction (its docstring records the two-component counterexample), so every (S)-realized seam satisfies `ψ ↔ ⊡ψ` for all `ψ ∈ cl φ`. Hence the Determined schema `Fp → ⊡Fp` -- refuted on model A by the jump history and on `natFrame` by `refute_determined` -- is unrefutable at any such seam. This is independent of F4 (it would persist on an infinite-width frame), and it fixes the realization hypothesis (W) may use: Hit-level (`HitPresented`: equality of family-triple SETS, under which two histories through one state can carry different types) or a product summary per cell, never trace-set equality with a unique anchor state.

**F6. The run layer is forced to be path-closed, and this is where HWZ's shape and this semantics part.** Histories are ALL bi-infinite step paths (`mem_HF_iff_adjacent`); the `⊡`-fibre is the PRODUCT `BwdSeq × FwdSeq` (`seamOmegaEquiv`), so the set of histories through a state is closed under splicing the backward half of one with the forward half of another, and the whole history set is closed under limits of chains of partial histories -- stated as the VALID formula `blc p` (`blc_plusValid`, Zorn + `PartialHistory.extension`). Consequences:
- Any (W) finite object whose run layer is not limit-closed refutes `blc`, a validity -- so limit-closure is a NECESSARY consistency condition of the object, not an option (and `Cons`'s `ulive`/`slive`, which forbid every deferring run, are one way to satisfy it, at the price of F4 and of trace-level incompleteness `Probe735.not_presented`).
- HWZ 2000 Def. 12's quasimodel `⟨f, R⟩` chooses `R` freely (Remark 13: `R ⊆ Ω_f` arbitrary above saturation), and Thm. 14 (⇐) takes the model's DOMAIN to be `R × κ` -- the runs ARE the elements, with no closure under splicing imposed. That freedom is exactly what this semantics lacks, which is why `quasistate_does_not_determine_hit` holds (HWZ-verbatim quasistates cannot see `Hit`) and why (S) needed `Cons` rather than Büchi-style acceptance of a chosen run set. The record's phrase "HWZ Thm 15 / GKWZ Thm 1.28's work for THIS semantics" therefore does not transfer as a transcription: the quasimodel must be a FRAME-SCHEME (a finite description of an infinite-width, finitely branching regular frame) plus a per-cell product summary, not a state function with a chosen run set.

**F7. The frame constructor (W) needs is named but not built, and its design space is bounded by a wired probe.** `NoFiniteWidth.lean`'s positive-half frame `F` is `FrameOver.ofReflectiveRegular Node (ofStepRel Step)` with Saturation discharged BY HAND from finite fibres; it is countable, finitely branching in both senses, time-homogeneous, with carrier `pre k / x k / post k j` (ℕ-indexed threads). `ofSlicedStep` cannot build it (`[Finite W]` on the slice). The 746 probe lands `fib_finite_of_finBranch`/`saturation_of_finBranch` (two-sided finite branching, no carrier hypothesis) and REFUTES both countdown-with-reset shapes (`Rbad`: infinite backward preimage, infinite `d = -1` fibre; `Rok`: infinite forward branching at the commitment state). `Node` is finitely branching because each thread's countdown is DETERMINISTIC per thread and the nondeterminism is pushed into which thread a history is on -- a shape the probe does not refute and does not build. The parallel constructor's regularity part is measured at ~120-160 lines [CARRIED-UNVERIFIED: `RaySummary/README.md`, "(S): what landed and what did not", last paragraph]; the thread-unfolding design that would turn a finite object into such a frame has no measurement and no construction anywhere in the tree.

**F8. The bound.** The anchor layer is bounded syntactically (F2/F3). The run layer, at the Hit level, inherits (C)'s obstruction unchanged: `Probe745.hitPresented_modelA` is `∃ L`, and the single named obligation `kTypeOf_listSeg_replicate_eventually_periodic` is stated and not proved. So even a fully landed (W) composition theorem would deliver `¬ PlusValidZTime φ ↔ ∃ object, …` and NOT `Decidable (PlusValidZTime φ)` until the bound lands; the task's "ON STATING THE GOAL ITSELF" clause applies with full force, and the plan must not state a `Decidable` instance in any form.
