## Overview

Requirement (W) asked for one finite object witnessing `¬ PlusValidZTime φ` for some regular
ℤ-model, history and time, and asked that its shape be characterized before anything was built.
This round lands the characterization's output: the object is two-layered, its **anchor layer**
(closure types, per-state cells, the `box`/`stab` fixpoint equations, the finite object
`AnchorCons` with its completeness theorem for EVERY model and the bound `2 ^ (2 ^ |cl φ|)`) is
landed sorry-free in a new module with a decidable `Finset` twin and a non-vacuous `#guard`
witness, and its **run layer** (the `untl`/`snce` nesting, the lift from one seam to validity) is
recorded as BLOCKED on three named obligations, with the shape of (S)'s construction refuted as
that lift's soundness half by a wired, gate-compiled probe. The record's "for every time"
quantifier was dropped by a theorem (`box_time_invariant`), not an argument. Nothing landed
states `Decidable (PlusValidZTime φ)` in any form, and nothing is a negative decidability result.

## Decisions

- Closure types are `Finset`-valued from the start (`tpOf` as a classical `Finset.filter`), so
  the cardinality bound is a four-step `calc` through `Set.ncard_le_ncard`, `Set.ncard_powerset`,
  `Set.ncard_coe_finset`, `Finset.card_powerset` with the finiteness autoparams passed explicitly.
- The two lemmas that unfold `tpOf` (`mem_tpOf_iff`, `tpOf_subset`) are scoped `open Classical
  in` like the definition; every later lemma routes through `mem_tpOf_iff`.
- `anchorCons_anchorCells`'s `box` field uses one helper (`tpOf_mem_cellAt_zero`: the type of any
  point lies in its state's cell at time `0`, by `σ.timeShift z`); the `stab` field needs none.
- `decidableAnchorConsFin` is `decidable_of_iff` on the five-field conjunction and was found by
  instance search directly; both `#guard`s evaluate under compiled `decide` with no
  `maxRecDepth` change.
- The probe keeps the two research scratch files' declarations verbatim in two `section`s with
  their own `variable` blocks (WIDTH needs `[Fintype Q] [DecidableEq Q] [Nonempty Q]`, COLLAPSE
  needs `{F : FrameOver intOrder} [F.IsRegular]`).
- The four theorem-index declarations carry `Paper: — (reason)` lines so invariant C15 anchors
  them at the declaration, as every other indexed declaration in `RaySummary/` does.
