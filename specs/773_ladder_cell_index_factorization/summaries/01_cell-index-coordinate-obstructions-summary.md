## Decisions

- **The parity gate is stated at a symbolic modulus, not a literal.** `omega` handles `%` only at
  a literal modulus, so the obligation `2B % (P+1) ≠ (2B+1) % (P+1)` needed `Nat.ModEq.dvd` to get
  `(P+1 : ℤ) ∣ 1` and then `Int.le_of_dvd`. Fixing the modulus at `2` to dodge it would have
  weakened a Challenge statement and was refused.
- **`gate_separates` and `gate_collides_below_two` were kept** when they arrived with the ported
  refutation section rather than stripped: they make the header's "what the earlier one-part gate
  did and did not certify" bullet machine-checked instead of prose, and they weaken nothing.
- **The third gate's (iii-a) attempt was recorded with its measured reason, not its predicted
  one.** The plan predicted no refutation because an achievable window would be positioned by the
  residue coordinates. The measurement is stronger and simpler: there is no window. `Count false
  false` holds the countdown at `0` through an idle interval of ANY length while `Drift` moves the
  next deadline one per step either way, so every second-region length is reachable from every
  pending state — independently of `T`, `P` and `B`. The task's own wording asked for "the precise
  reason the window is determined", so this is that branch rather than a substitution.
- **The (iii-b) attempt turned an argument into a bound**, and the bound carries a refinement to
  the parameter-origin rule: the gap clip's `φ`-derivation must cover `snce`-nesting depth as well
  as `X`-nesting depth.
