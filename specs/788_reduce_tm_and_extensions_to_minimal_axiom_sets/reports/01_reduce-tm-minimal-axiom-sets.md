## Risks & Mitigations
- **Transitive hidden use of a demoted constructor** inside an engine (would make the Z1 route circular or break a proof): mitigated by the grep sweeps above (none found) and, decisively, by the Phase 2 full rebuild — a hidden use fails to compile.
- **Build time**: the only whole-tree rebuild is forced by editing `Axioms.lean`; batch all constructor deletions into one edit and run it once, detached and guarded with the maximum timeout. Do not iterate on `Axioms.lean` across multiple partial builds.
- **Exhaustive-match blast radius** (~130 arms over ~25 files incl. tooling): purely mechanical; let the compiler enumerate them (each missing/extra arm is an error at a named line). `FormulaEnumerator`/`ProofExtractorMain` entries that *named* an axiom should be re-pointed to the derived theorem so dataset coverage is preserved rather than dropped.
- **Machine-appendix contract** (`MachineAppendixMain` asserts exactly 29 entries; `typst-sync-check.sh` CHECK3 compares live count to `machine-appendix.jsonl`): update the assertion to 20 and regenerate; the derived schemata may deserve their own appendix rows as "derived theorems" so the Typst reference does not silently lose them.
- **Benchmark/dataset baselines** (`scripts/c20-declaration-baseline.txt`, `certificate-witness-inventory.txt`, `decidability-inventory-baseline.txt`, benchmark anchors): names of *soundness* lemmas survive; axiom-entry names change. Diff the baselines after Phase 3 rather than hand-editing in advance.
- **Automation regressions** for gated schemata (DN, Z1): `@[tmLemma]` cannot discharge a `Dense ≤ fc` hypothesis by itself; follow the existing gated-lemma mechanism and keep `WeakeningSearchTest` green.
- **Paper/tree presentation drift**: the paper lists 15 BX keys as axioms; the tree will have 7. Record it; a later paper revision can adopt the reduced set (as happened for BX_r). The footnote erratum "UE follows from UC" is already logged; the derivation here confirms it is UG.
