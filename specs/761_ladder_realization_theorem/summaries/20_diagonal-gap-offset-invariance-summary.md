## Decisions

- **The declarations live in `LadderRealize.lean`, not `LadderAgree.lean`.** The natural home for
  a model-level invariance is `LadderAgree.lean`'s `section Hist`, beside half (i)
  (`truthAt_iff_of_objAt_eq_of_stabPinned`), whose proof shape this induction mirrors case for
  case. It is landed in `LadderRealize.lean` instead, following the prior round's own ruling:
  `LadderAgree` is upstream of `LadderPump → LadderChain → LadderRealize`, so editing it rebuilds
  the whole stack, and the consumer (`cellAt_const_of_kappa_eq_of_covered`) is here. `LadderAgree.lean`
  is untouched.
- **The induction quantifies over the walk `g` inside the induction, not outside it.** The `⊡`
  case needs the induction hypothesis at a DIFFERENT walk (the one the inner history carries), so
  `k`, `k'`, `g`, `σ` and `σ'` are all introduced after `induction ψ`. A statement with `g` fixed
  before the induction does not close at `⊡`.
- **The `⊡` case is proved, not hypothesised.** Half (i) carries its `⊡` clause on a pinned-letter
  premise `hL` because its two walks differ; here the two histories carry ONE walk and differ only
  in the offset, which is what makes the clause provable and is why this coordinate is tractable
  independently of the register question.
