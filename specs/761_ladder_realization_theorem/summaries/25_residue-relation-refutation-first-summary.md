## The Verdict

`ProbeRowResidue.no_rowLinked_path_of_Srel (P a b) (hab : 1 ≤ a + b)`: there are
`Srel P a (b + 1)`-related states of `R4`, a ladder path through the first, and no ladder path
through the second row-linked to it at `(a, b + 1)` — at EVERY modulus `P + 1`. Axioms
`[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.

The pair is `(r0, 2(a+b)+2, ·)` against `(r0, 2(a+b)+2+(P+1), ·)`. Position `t + 3` of the longer
region carries `r0` with a large countdown; inside the shorter region only `t + 1`, `t + 2` meet
`Zrel a b`'s countdown clause and they carry `r1`, `r2`; an answer beyond the region passes its
countdown-`0` position, which nothing in `(t, t + 3)` on the longer path matches.

**Failing coordinate: the residue** (not the register offset — registers and gaps are matched and
unread; not the past row — no backward jump is used). Exactly: the conjunct fixes the residue at
the linked pair, while the row link needs every position of the longer region answered at its own
residue above `Zrel`'s threshold, whose margin (`2` per forward unit) is independent of the
object's cycle length. On the `2`-cycle `C3` two residues fit in that margin and the attack is
answered at the level of states; on a `3`-cycle they do not.
