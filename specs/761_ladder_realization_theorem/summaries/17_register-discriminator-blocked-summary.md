## Decisions

- **The decision rule was applied as written and reached no candidate.** The ladder was walked in
  the pinned order and each refusal points at a compiled object: (R-b) refused by
  `iterSpliced_register_clipped_interior_refuted` on side (A); (R-a) refused by
  `stab_bit_ne_of_register_ne` at `m = 1` on side (B); (R-c) refused by the same theorem at every
  `m > 0`. No candidate was adopted for convenience and neither side's measurement is carried as a
  hypothesis of anything.
- **Side (A)'s refutation reuses the existing fixture.** `ClimbWitness.climb` with `fwdT`, `bwdT`
  and `labT`, as the phase body directed, rather than a second counterexample. The new content is
  one generic lemma plus one witness instantiation.
- **Side (B) needed a new fixture, and that is a finding.** None of the four round-15 probe
  fixtures the phase body pointed at reads the REGISTER coordinate — all four read the countdown —
  so the register requirement could not be derived from them and was measured on a new
  forced-pending-continuation path (`regPath`) with a new labelling (`labR`). The probe formula is
  the cheapest one that reads the register at all: the register becomes observable only once a
  history leaves the idle state and the next region's countdown is handed its value.
- **No relation, no `Tc_d`, no `D`.** Defining the relation's threshold companions would pre-commit
  the `Tc_0` base that one of the two successor routes may re-base, and the phase body makes that a
  `/revise` ruling. `Tc_0 = 0` and the threshold's vacuity at `d = D` are recorded in prose from
  `margin_room_at_depth`'s own docstring rather than re-landed.
- **No new `(K2)` ruling was taken.** `CounterQuotient.lean` is untouched (`git diff` empty), so
  `paramsOkK1`–`paramsOkK4`, `ParamsOk`, `Tcof`, `Tnof`, `Pof`, `Bof` and `paramsOk_params` are
  byte-identical and `Tc_0` is not re-based.
