  `DischPathK` applied to `Set.range ξ`. `DischPathK` quantifies a guarded path inside a candidate

  consumes and what the run theorem can prove from a real history's type sequence. `DischPathK` is

## Why the Agreement Is Blocked

Three obstructions, each a consequence of what is now compiled. The full record is the
`**BLOCKER**` entry under Phase 11 in the plan.

1. **The walk must be pumped, not the counters.** `cntAt_eq_of_obj_eq`: two ladder paths with the
   same object walk carry the same countdown at every position.
2. **The periodicity the pumping needs is not the periodicity that is landed.** The only landed
   periodicity statement is the eventual periodicity of the backward-reachability fixpoint
   `prodPreStep (cohIdle …)` on `Finset (ProdState Q φ)`, with its outputs `satThreshold` and
   `satPeriod` — a statement about the CERTIFICATE relation, whose bridge to `tpOf` along a ladder
   path is exactly obligations (S) and (C'), which read this agreement. A THIRD circularity. A
   model-level pumping lemma has to be authored instead, and no phase of this plan costs one.
3. **The `⊡` bit forces an induction on `⊡`-nesting depth even at the history level.** By
   `stab_mem_tpOf_iff_cell`, `⊡χ ∈ tpOf M φ σ z` iff `χ` belongs to every type in the cell at
   `σ.state z` — the object the agreement compares. Route (R-b) relocates the stratification into
   this phase's proof rather than removing it; at the model level the stratified objects are
   `Finset PlusFormula` values of `tpOf`, so (R-a)'s recorded cost (a stratified certificate
   family) does not transfer.
