## The obstruction

`ladderSound`'s conclusion is `(q, T, kappa Tc Tn P B (c, n)) ∈ Dmax …`. `Dmax` is the union of
the `certAdmK`-certified subsets, so the proof must exhibit ONE certified `R` containing that
member — and `Dmax`'s own certification is NOT available (Phase 9's recorded exclusion: `certStabK`
and `certBoxK` are biconditionals, so only the four monotone conditions survive a union, and the
module says in source that nothing claims `certAdmK … (Dmax …)`).

Four candidate sets were worked through:

1. **The projection of ONE admissible ladder history.** Conditions (1)-(4) are reachable.
   Condition (6) `certBoxK` fails: its `←` direction forces `□ψ ∈ x.2.1.val` as soon as every
   member of `R` carries `ψ`, whereas `box_mem_tpOf_iff` makes the real bit quantify over EVERY
   history of the model. A single chain cannot refute a `□`.
2. **The full realizable set** (the plan's `Real`, Finding 3). (1), (2), (4) and (6) are reachable
   — (6) exactly because `box_mem_tpOf_iff`'s quantifier is global, every member of `Real` is some
   `tpOf M φ σ' z'`, and conversely. (5) `certStabK` is NOT: `stab_mem_tpOf_iff` makes the `⊡` bit
   a datum of the ONE world state `⟨(q, c, n), h⟩`, while `certStabK`'s fibre is taken over the
   whole `(q, κ)`-class. BOTH directions then need the cells of class-equivalent states to agree.
3. **The cell at one representative state per `(q, κ)`-class.** (5) holds at the representative,
   but (2) `certBiInfK` fails: the successor forced at a member lies at the NEXT ladder state,
   whose class has its own representative, and the successor's type need not lie in that
   representative's cell — the agreement again.
4. **Confining `R` to a single state's cell.** Refuted by (2) for the same reason as 3.

So `certStabK` wants the fibre to be exactly one state's cell and `certBiInfK` forbids it, and the
gap between them is precisely the agreement of class-equivalent cells.

```lean
/-- **Any `certStabK`-certified set collapses the `⊡` bits across a `(q, κ)`-class.** -/
theorem stab_bit_agree_of_certStabK {Q : Type} (φ : PlusFormula) (Tc Tn P B : ℕ)
    (R : Set (Q × ClType φ × CK Tc Tn P B))
    (hR : certStabK φ Tc Tn P B R)
    (x y : Q × ClType φ × CK Tc Tn P B) (hx : x ∈ R) (hy : y ∈ R)
    (hq : y.1 = x.1) (hk : y.2.2 = x.2.2)
    (ψ : PlusFormula) (hψ : ψ ∈ plusSubformulaClosure φ) (hs : isStab ψ = true) :
    (ψ ∈ x.2.1.val ↔ ψ ∈ y.2.1.val) := by
  have hX := hR x hx ψ hψ hs
  have hY := hR y hy ψ hψ hs
  rw [hX, hY]
  constructor
  · intro h z hz hz1 hz2
    exact h z hz (by rw [hz1, hq]) (by rw [hz2, hk])
  · intro h z hz hz1 hz2
    exact h z hz (by rw [hz1, ← hq]) (by rw [hz2, ← hk])

variable {F : FrameOver intOrder}

/-- The `⊡` bit of a realized type IS a cell-level datum: `stab_mem_tpOf_iff` read through
`cellAt`. -/
theorem stab_mem_tpOf_iff_cell (M : TaskModel F.toTaskFrame) (φ : PlusFormula)
    (σ : WorldHistory F.toTaskFrame) (z : ℤ) {χ : PlusFormula}
    (h : PlusFormula.stab χ ∈ plusSubformulaClosure φ) :
    PlusFormula.stab χ ∈ tpOf M φ σ z ↔ ∀ T ∈ cellAt M φ (σ.state z) z, χ ∈ T := by
  rw [stab_mem_tpOf_iff M φ σ z h]
  constructor
  · rintro H T ⟨σ', hs', rfl⟩
    exact H σ' hs'.symm
  · intro H σ' hσ'
    exact H _ ⟨σ', hσ'.symm, rfl⟩
```

## Why this is a plan-structure blocker, not a proof-search failure

- Phase 13's Goal `cellAt_const_of_kappa_eq` IS that agreement, and Phase 13 derives it "by
  induction on `stab`-nesting depth from `ladderSound` and `ladderLift`".
- The plan's own Research Integration, Finding 3, records the same dependency in the other
  direction: "`Real` is itself certified GIVEN constancy of `cellAt` on `K`-classes."
- So Phases 11, 12 and 13 are mutually circular as decomposed: 11 needs 13, 13 needs 11 and 12,
  and 12 declares `Depends on: 7, 11`.
- Phase 11's own `Scope Hypothesis` anticipates exactly this and instructs: "If the `stab`
  induction needs constancy of `cellAt` on `K`-classes — which is Phase 13's corollary and would be
  circular here — stop and record the circularity in the handoff." That instruction is what this
  handoff executes.
- The hoped-for escape in that same sentence — "the licensed shape puts the `stab` clause at LOWER
  depth precisely to avoid it" — was followed and does not escape. At depth `d` the argument needs,
  for each class-equivalent state and each history through it, a history through the given state
  agreeing on a formula of depth `d - 1`. That is a lift at lower depth, i.e. (C'), Phase 12. The
  depth stratification does not remove the lift; it only lowers the depth at which the lift is
  called. So the induction is genuinely SIMULTANEOUS in (S) and (C'), and cannot be split across
  phases whose `Depends on` edges run one way.
