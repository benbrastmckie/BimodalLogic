## Decisions

- **The targets are parametric in `(p : Atom)`, not fixed to a concrete atom.** The probes fix
  `Atom.mkBase "p"`. Parameterizing is free here because `natModel`'s valuation is
  `fun n _ => n = 0` — atom-agnostic — so the probes' own refutation arguments transfer verbatim.
  The one cost is that `decide` can no longer evaluate a closure (see Plan Deviations).
- **The declarations are named after what they say, not after the probes' `Xp`/`dXp` spellings.**
  Invariant C26 requires camelCase `def`s, and this phase's own instruction was to name for the
  formula placed. The probes' abbreviation table is preserved verbatim in the module docstring, so
  the correspondence is checkable rather than lost.
- **The factoring was moved from the syntactic side to the semantic side.** See Plan Deviations;
  this is the phase's one substantive finding.
- **The closure-shape fact was strengthened past what the plan asked for.** The plan asked for
  "every `untl` member has the guard the argument assumes". What the closure computation actually
  yields is the full member list, so that is what is stated, with the guard fact derived. Stating
  the weaker form would have discarded information already in hand.

## Plan Deviations

- **Phase 9, "Land the closure-membership chain for both targets"** altered: landed as **two**
  chains (13 + 17 steps), not one shared scaffolding. The phase's **Scope Hypothesis is falsified**
  and is recorded as such under the phase heading. The hypothesis was that the two targets' shared
  `Xp`/`X¬p`/`⟐Xp`/`⟐X¬p` prefix lets one membership block serve both. The shared *text* is real —
  the two probes' blocks agree on eleven of thirteen lines — but it cannot become a shared
  *theorem*: `plusClosureOf` is indexed by the context, so `plusClosureOf [hopTarget p]` and
  `plusClosureOf [pumpTarget p]` are two different `Finset PlusFormula` values, and a transport
  lemma would additionally need one target to be a subformula of the other. Neither is: they differ
  at the innermost consequent (`⊥` against `(Fp → Fp) → ⊥`). Factoring is not expensive here, it is
  unavailable.
- **Phase 9, "Prove `not_plusValidZTime_pumpTarget` and `not_plusValidZTime_hopTarget`"** altered:
  factored through two shared antecedent lemmas. This is the sharing the Scope Hypothesis was
  reaching for, relocated to where it exists. Both targets carry the identical antecedents `□⟐Xp`
  and `□⟐X¬p`, and both hold on the permissive frame for one reason, so the two lemmas are proved
  once and each non-validity theorem is three lines.
- **Phase 9, "Prove the closure-shape fact … by `decide` on the concrete closure"** altered: proved
  by `simp` on the computed subformula list instead. `decide` cannot run: with the atom left as a
  parameter the closure is not a closed term, so no `Decidable` instance evaluates. The `simp`
  route is strictly stronger than the `decide`-at-a-concrete-atom alternative it replaces, because
  it holds for every atom.
- **Phase 9, "Define `pumpTarget` and `hopTarget`"** altered in naming only; see Decisions.
- No plan step was skipped, and no plan step was deferred.

## Impacts

- `hopTarget` and `pumpTarget` are now library declarations rather than probe-local copies, and
  parametric in the atom, so Phases 10 and 11 can state their limit theorems for an arbitrary atom
  instead of a fixed one.
- The two closure-membership chains are the guarded read-access the limit theorems need: every
  clause of `PlusLocalCoherentShare`, `PlusBoxFaithful`, `PlusTarget` and `StabFaithful` is gated
  on closure membership, and these 30 declarations are what discharge those gates.
- `untl_mem_hopClosure` / `untl_mem_pumpClosure` isolate, as a theorem rather than as prose, the
  one structural difference between the two targets: `pumpClosure` has a third `untl` member whose
  guard is `⊤` rather than `⊥`. That is exactly why the hop-free limit and the compression limit
  are independent results rather than one implying the other.
- Nothing downstream changed behaviourally. The added import is the only edit to an existing Lean
  module besides the generated root.
