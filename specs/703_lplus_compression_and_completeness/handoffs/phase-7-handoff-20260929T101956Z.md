## Why the phase is blocked

Invariant A ("pad `back` and `fwd` by repetition to `plusCompressionBound`") is not provable, for
two independent reasons.

1. **Arithmetic.** Repetition preserves the decoded label function only at multiples of the
   cycle length, because `Periodic.cyc` reads `i % length`. So the invariant needs
   `L ∣ plusCompressionBound` for every extracted `L`, which the good-cycle theorem does not
   supply. At a three-member closure, `exists_base_plusCycleT` permits every `L` in 1…8 while
   `plusCompressionBound` is 56; back lengths 5, 7 and 8 have no common multiple below 280.
2. **Its stated purpose does not describe this tree.** `PlusWitnessFamily/Decide.lean`'s
   `perBack` is `repBack.length * (lassos.map (·.back.length)).prod` — a product, never a least
   common multiple. A common length `L` would give `NB = |repBack| · L^n`, not
   `plusCompressionBound`.

Nothing downstream needs Invariant A for correctness, which is why "drop it" is the recommended
resolution rather than "widen the bound".
