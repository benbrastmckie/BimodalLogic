## The settled design question, and why it is settled

6c looked like a choice between two routes. It is not:

- **Route (α), the nested (Emerson–Lei) fixpoint, is correct**: the inner reachability is taken
  **inside the set being contracted** (`EUFix.lfp X …`, not `EUFix.lfp G.verts …`).
- **Route (β) is UNSOUND.** (β) was: `fwdWalkable` membership plus a separate clause that every
  walkable position with a pending eventuality lies in `untlReach g e`. `untlReach` takes its
  reachability inside all of `verts`, so a discharging path it certifies **may leave the set from
  which the walk can be continued forever**. The round-robin construction then has nothing to
  concatenate: a segment that delivers `e` but strands the walk at a dead end does not extend to a
  fair walk. Recorded here and at the plan's Phase 15 heading so no dispatch spends a run on the
  cheaper-looking clause pair.

`fwdWalkable` / `bwdWalkable` (`Computed.lean`) stay as landed: they are plain `EG ⊤`, they are
correct as that, and `fwdLiveT ⊆ fwdWalkable` will be a one-line consequence. They are **not** a
substitute for `fwdLiveT` and no downstream clause should read them as one.

## The largest remaining piece, named

(α) makes the fixpoint the right **set**. It does **not** by itself produce the single walk that
discharges **every** eventuality. That still needs the **round-robin concatenation**: finitely many
eventualities, each deliverable from every vertex of the fixpoint without leaving it, so splice the
delivering segments in rotation and interleave them into one infinite walk. This argument has **no
counterpart anywhere in the tree** — the `Formula` side never needed it, because there the demand is
universal over threads and `threadFulfilling_iff_window` is proved directly. Write it
**generically**, at `Nu` / `EUFix`'s level in `Fixpoint.lean`, not at the certificate. Budget it as
the single largest item in STEP 6, and do **not** budget it as transcription.
