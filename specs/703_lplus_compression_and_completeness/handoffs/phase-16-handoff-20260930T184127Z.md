## The deviation of substance, and how to reverse it

**`Φ_back` / `Φ_fwd` are re-indexed to the COMBINED periods** — `-G.NB → G.winLo` and
`G.NM + G.NF → G.winHi`, where 16.1 had `-G.nb → -2 * G.nb` and `G.nm + G.nf → G.nm + 2 * G.nf`. The
handoff at the end of dispatch 34 required the indexing mismatch be settled explicitly; this settles
it. Three reasons, in full at the plan's Phase 16 heading; the decisive one is that `prevTime_edge`
folds by `G.NB`, so an operator fixed for one `G.nb`-period constrains nothing about the fold
`TailStable` exists to certify.

**Reversing it would not be cheap and should not be attempted piecemeal**: `L₀` / `R₀` would stop
being sets at window times, `mem_liveAt_iff_live` would no longer apply to them, and every theorem
from `iterBack_L₀` onward would lose its statement. If the re-index is ever to be revisited, revisit
the *window* (`winLo` / `winHi` in `Window.lean`) instead — every lemma here is stated from `G.NB` /
`G.NF` and would follow the window automatically.

## The three corrections to the plan this dispatch recorded

1. **`tailStable_iff_window` is about two-directional `Live`, not `FwdLive`.** The bullet says "the
   true forward-live sets"; it cannot be, because `fwdLiveT` membership does not imply `FwdLive` (no
   bi-infinite run), so the `←` direction would be false. `G.liveT` is exact for `Live` in both
   directions, so `L₀` is a set of two-directionally live positions and the transfer soundness was
   re-proved at that strength (`live_subset_stepBack` / `live_subset_stepFwd`, witnessed by
   `live_iff`'s single fully fulfilling run). A strengthening, not a weakening.
2. **No shift of a `LabRun` is a `LabRun`.** `agrees` and `steps` are conditions at *every* time
   while the slice sequence is periodic only away from `mid`, so the obvious argument for the `←`
   direction fails outright. The landed route is the three-region run; `runOfPos` is the abstraction
   that makes it affordable.
3. **The right tail is a separate construction, not a symmetry argument.**
   `PlusSlicedCertificate` is not symmetric under time reversal, and `runOfPos` asks for `succP`
   steps, so the right-tail chain runs along `predP` and is converted by `mem_succP_iff_mem_predP`.
