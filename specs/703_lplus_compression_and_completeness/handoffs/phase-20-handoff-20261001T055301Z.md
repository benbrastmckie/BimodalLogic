## READ THIS BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE: the plan's "backward half is genuinely short" claim is FALSE

Phase 20's fourth task bullet says the backward half of `TailStable` is immediate because "with edges
`i → i` only, `Φ_back` is the identity on the position sets it acts on". **It is not the identity.**
`Stable.lean:130`:

```
stepBack t X = (posAt (t - 1)).filter (fun p => (succP (t - 1) p ∩ X).Nonempty)
```

This is a `succP`-**preimage**, and `succP` constrains the arriving label only through `StepClause`,
which permits the label to change along the step. So with self-loops `stepBack t X` is "the positions
at `t - 1` whose index matches some member of `X` and whose label is `StepClause`-compatible with
it" — a different set, at a different time, not `X`. The self-loop structure pins the **state**
component and says nothing about the **label** component, and the label component is where the whole
difficulty lives.

Consequence, taken rather than concealed: the backward conjunct is an equation between a
`succP`-preimage and a computed live set, of the same order of difficulty as the forward one, and
both conjuncts moved together into 20.2. The plan's Contingency puts `TailStable` in 20.1; that split
point was chosen on the strength of this false claim, and the plan's Phase 20 heading now records the
correction. **Do not budget the backward conjunct at zero.**
