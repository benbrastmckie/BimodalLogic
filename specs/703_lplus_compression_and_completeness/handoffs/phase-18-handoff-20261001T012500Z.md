## The one thing a successor must check before writing the `stab` case

`Check.lean`'s `StabFaithful` and `BoxLiveFaithful` are stated at **window times**
(`t ∈ G.winTimes`) and against the **computed** `G.liveAt t`. That is the only decidable form; a
`∀ t : ℤ` form would make `decidableCertifies` unprovable, and weakening it is explicitly
forbidden. But the truth lemma needs the `stab` clause at an **arbitrary** `t`, and transporting it
has two halves of unequal cost:

- the **slab** half folds freely: `exists_win_eq_slice` gives a window representative with the same
  slice, and `Timed.lean`'s `slab_congr` transports `G.slab`;
- the **liveness** half needs `{p | G.Live t p}` to agree with `{p | G.Live s p}` at that
  representative, and the landed linchpins give it only at the period multiples
  `-G.NB - k·G.NB` (`tailStable_iff_window`) and `G.NM + G.NF + k·G.NF`
  (`tailStable_iff_window_fwd`) — **not** at an arbitrary tail time of a different residue.

A naive "shift the run by `G.NB`" argument does **not** close this, for the same reason that forced
`runOfPos`'s three-region construction: the slice sequence is `NB`-periodic only on the negatives,
so a run through a far-left time extends into a region where that periodicity fails.

**Two routes, in this order.**

1. **Frame-side normalization — check this first, it is the cheap outcome.** `Frame.lean`'s
   `plusTruthAt_shiftBack` and `timeShift_offset_zero` may let the truth lemma be proved at a
   normalized time only, in which case the clause is never read off the window and there is no gap
   at all.
2. **A liveness-periodicity lemma at an arbitrary residue**: `G.Live t p ↔ G.Live (t - G.NB) p` for
   `t ≤ -G.NB`, and its mirror, proved from `TailStable` by re-running `runOfPos` from a shifted
   reference time. Real work, and it belongs to Phase 16's machinery rather than to a soundness
   proof.

If **neither** closes it, STOP and record a blocker. Do **not** weaken `StabFaithful` to a
non-decidable `∀ t` form — that undoes Phase 17.

to stability instead would be the fix, and it is also the cheapest route to open question 2.
