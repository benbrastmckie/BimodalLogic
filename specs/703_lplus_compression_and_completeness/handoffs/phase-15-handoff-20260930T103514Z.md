## Why 15.3 was not started

Its STEP 1 is the re-carrier and its STEP 2 fixes `winLo` / `winHi`. The landed `exists_window_eq`
window is **single**-period `[-nb, nm + nf)`; the `Formula` side's is **doubled** on both sides
(`cohWindowLo = -2·NB`, `cohWindowHi = NM + 2·NF`). A single-period window cannot give a sound wrap,
because identical local graphs do not imply identical liveness — an eventuality may only be
dischargeable by reaching the non-periodic window, and the distance to it differs between two
same-residue times. Phase 16's four-state fixture is exactly that phenomenon. Writing `winLo`/`winHi`
before the fixture confirms the doubling is the improvisation the research's anti-goals forbid, so it
was not done. Two concrete cautions for whoever builds the fixture:

1. **The fixture as the plan words it may not be directly realizable in this carrier.**
   `PlusSlicedCertificate` gives every slice the same width `n` and a *per-slice* edge relation, and
   the back period is 1, so the back slice's single `edge` must simultaneously serve `t = -2`
   (`a → a`, `a → b`, `b → b`) and `t = -1` (`a → c`, `b → c`). Those five edges therefore all live
   in one `Fin 4 → Fin 4 → Bool`, which means `a → c` is also an edge at `t = -2`. Check whether the
   asymmetry survives that, and if it does not, redesign the fixture rather than the theorem.
2. **"Evaluate the stability label of `⊡(XX ¬q)` with `#eval` or `#guard`" is not literally
   available.** `⊡`-truth is a universal over histories of an infinite carrier, so it is not
   `Decidable` and not `#eval`-able. The plan's own stronger requirement — "prove as named lemmas
   that it is **true** at `(-1, a)` and **false** at `(-2, a)`" — is the one to satisfy; the `#eval`
   is at best a check on a *computed* liveness set, which does not exist yet.
