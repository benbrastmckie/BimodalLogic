and a `snce` in its closure.**

## The mechanism

`Position.lean`'s `snceClauseAt` constrains the **later** label from the earlier one:

```
snce g e ∈ Y ↔ e ∈ X ∨ (g ∈ X ∧ snce g e ∈ X)
```

So a label carrying a `snce` obligation constrains its **predecessors** and leaves its
**successors** free; `LabCoherent` constrains no eventuality at all; and `AgreesOnState` pins only
atoms, `□`s and `⊡`s. Hence a position whose label carries a `snce` obligation that no admissible
predecessor can discharge is a legitimate member of `posAt` with **empty `predP`** — not
backward-live, therefore not live — while its `succP` is non-empty, because dropping the obligation
rightward is always permitted. `Stable.lean`'s `stepBack` is a `succP`-**preimage**, so that
position lands in `iterBack`, and the unfiltered backward conjunct demands it be live. It is not.

This is the exact mirror of `FixtureStable.lean`'s obstruction to the raw forward demand
(`Fixture.Φ_fwd_R₀_ne`), and the reason the forward side needed a filter at all.

## One correction to the record, worth carrying forward

`Stable.lean`'s `fwdLiveAt` note justifies the asymmetry this way: a backward filter would have no
soundness lemma to stand on, because "`FoldB` relates two negative times only, so no backward
counterpart of `mem_fwdLiveT_of_fwdLive_fold` exists **at the right tail**". That sentence is
correct and is about the **right** tail, whose times are positive. It does **not** cover the left
tail: the left tail's times `-G.NB - k * G.NB` are **negative**, `FoldB` does relate them
(`FoldB a b ↔ a = b ∨ (a < 0 ∧ b < 0 ∧ a % NB = b % NB)`), and `Fold.lean` already lands every
transport a mirror construction needs — `foldB_slice`, `foldB_posAt`, `foldB_predP`,
`foldB_prevTime`. The only missing piece is a `bwdVertFold` counterpart of `Bridge.lean`'s
`fwdVertFold`; `bwdVert` (the diagonal) exists, `bwdVertFold` does not. **This is an observation
offered to the ruling, not a proof that the mirror lemma closes.**
