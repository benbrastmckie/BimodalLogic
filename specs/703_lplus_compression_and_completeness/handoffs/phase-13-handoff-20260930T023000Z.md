## Design decisions a successor must not relitigate

- **`BiSerial` is the `∀ t` form; `BiSerialWindow` is the decided one.** The plan's wording puts it
  the other way round, but `G.frame (h : G.BiSerial)` reads the condition at an arbitrary time, so
  the `∀ t` form has to be the one the Challenge signature names. Both exist and are provably
  equivalent.
- **`PlusGraphPath`'s readout default is its own `back.head`, via `back_ne`** — not a `n_pos` field.
  That is what makes `datum_mem` hold at every time with no special case for the default, and hence
  `lab_sub` hold with no side condition. `n_pos` is a *theorem* here, not a field.
- **`Inhabited (PlusSlice n C)` is unconditional.** The inert slice is definable at `n = 0`, so no
  positivity hypothesis is threaded through the slice readout.
- **`exists_window_eq` is a residue computation, not an induction.** Because `BiSerialAt` is stated
  at one slice, extending it over all times needs only that the slice at an arbitrary time equals
  the slice at a window time.
- **The generic readout lemmas are reused, not rewritten.** Confirmed by reading their binders: all
  six are over `{α : Type*} [Inhabited α]` with no monomorphic load-bearing position.
