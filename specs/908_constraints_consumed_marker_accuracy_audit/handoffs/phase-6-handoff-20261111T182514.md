## Key Decisions

- **Route 1 held for all three `None` candidates, on the first probe.** The probe was run in
  place on `Path.lean` rather than in a scratch copy: all three binders were deleted together and
  the module rebuilt. That is strictly stronger evidence than a scratch copy, because it also
  exercises every call site of the three declarations inside the module, and the full library
  build then covered the dependents outside it. `GUARD_EXIT=0`, 1191 jobs, zero `error:` lines,
  no fallback to route 2 for any of the three. The reason the binder was dead weight is visible
  in the term: every ingredient the recursion reaches (`factorLeft`, `cutState`, `respects_task`,
  `states_eq_of_time_eq`, `Beh.mem_dom`) is constraint-free, because the section `e.sec` carries
  its own task-respect obligation.
- **The window cluster needed no new enabler twin.** `FrameOver.taskRel_natCast_iff_iter_of_constraints`
  (`IntNormalForm.lean:210`) and `FrameOver.reflection_int_of_limit`
  (`IntNormalFormEffective.lean:80`) are the binder-free forms of the two lemmas
  `Beh.ofAdjacentWindow`'s `respects_task` obligation routes through, and both already existed.
  The plan's enabler accounting for this phase was therefore an over-estimate in the task's
  favour.
- **`beh_int_iff_adjacent_window`'s forward direction reaches no constraint at all.** Only its
  backward direction (the appeal to the window constructor) does. That is recorded in the twin's
  docstring rather than being flattened into the marker, which enumerates the union over the
  whole declaration.
- **The ~1168 rationale paragraph was split rather than patched.** It said "The marker lists the
  bundle the binder supplies; the proof reaches it through those two lemmas" -- the reading
  `REFERENCE_NORMAL_FORM.md` rejects. The twin's docstring now says what the elaborated term
  reaches; the corollary's says "the marker enumerates what the elaborated term reaches, not
  what the binder supplies".
- **Two line-length repairs, both consequences of the substitution.** Replacing
  `Beh.ofGerm F.toTaskFrame u` with `Beh.ofGermOfSerialLimit F.toTaskFrame hser hlim u` and
  `PathHom.comp_id f` with `PathHom.comp_id_of_constraints hcomp hser hlim f` pushed two lines
  past the 100-character limit; both were rewrapped, not suppressed.
