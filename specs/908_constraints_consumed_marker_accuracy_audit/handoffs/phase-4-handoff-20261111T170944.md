## Key Decisions

- **No `TaskFrame`-level binder-free nullity wrapper exists.** `TaskFrame.nullity_identity`
  (`TaskFrame.lean:2682`) takes `[F.IsRegular]`; the binder-free form
  `FrameOver.nullity_identity_of_serial_limit` lives one level down at `TaskFrame.lean:1323`.
  Per the plan's own instruction for exactly this case, the twin routes through `F.toFibre`
  explicitly rather than adding a new `TaskFrame`-level lemma. This works because
  `TaskFrame.TaskRel` is `@[reducible] def ... := F.toFibre.TaskRel`, so a hypothesis of type
  `TaskFrame.Serial F.TaskRel` discharges the fibre-level argument directly -- no bridging
  lemma, no `change`, no `simp only`.
- The twin takes `(F : TaskFrame)` explicitly and `hser`/`hlim` before `w`, exactly the
  planned argument order; `ofGerm` passes them positionally, so its own signature is untouched.
- The twin's docstring says "a bundling instance", never `IsRegular`, mirroring phase 3's twins
  -- C34a's `delegates()` predicate refuses a delegate that names a bundling class anywhere in
  its span.
- The module docstring's **Frame constraints.** paragraph was rewritten: it previously asserted
  that the *Germs* clause reaches the constraints "only through the `[F.IsRegular]` bundle",
  which the twin makes false. It now names `ofGermOfSerialLimit` as where the construction
  happens and records the bundle on `ofGerm`/`germ_ofGerm`/`ofGerm_germ`/`germEquiv` as a
  convenience projecting exactly two fields. The **Module contents** bullet for `Beh.ofGerm`
  gained a clause naming the twin.
- No inversion needed. The plan prepared a fallback (keep `ofGerm`'s body as the real definition
  and prove the twin equal to it) in case the added unfolding layer broke a downstream
  `rfl`/`simp`. `germ_ofGerm` (`rfl`), `ofGerm_germ` and `germEquiv` all still close by their
  existing proofs, unchanged, and the 1211-job `Presheaf` build -- which covers all four
  enumerated direct dependents plus the aggregator -- was green on the first attempt.

  `check-module-invariants.sh` C26 enforces lowerCamelCase for `def`/`abbrev` names, exempting

  to `Beh.ofGermOfSerialLimit`; signature, hypothesis order, marker and delegation direction are
  exactly as planned, and the re-run reports
  `PASS C26 zero snake_case def/abbrev names in the live tree`. Annotated inline on the plan's
  phase 4 task list.
  **This will recur in phases 5, 6 and 7**, every one of which adds `def`-valued twins the plan
  names with a snake_case suffix: `PathHom.id_of_serial_limit` (phase 5, plan line 326), and the
  `toPath`/`ofStep`/`ofPath` twins (phases 6-7). Those phases must use lowerCamelCase for `def`
  twins and may keep snake_case only for `theorem` twins.

  `def ofGerm_of_serial_limit (F : TaskFrame) (hser : ...) (hlim : ...) (w : F.WorldState) : Presheaf.Beh F 0`.
