## Overview

Nine declarations landed in `FormalSystem/Metalogic/Expressiveness/OpenFutureElimination.lean`,
each a term-mode one-liner over the already-landed `openValidIn_ztime_iff_tr` /
`openValidIn_rtime_iff_tr`, carrying decidability and completeness of open-language (L^▷) validity
back to their L⁺ counterparts. Every one takes its L⁺ fact as an explicit leading hypothesis, no
antecedent is discharged, no proof system for L^▷ is built, and nothing is claimed at `.Base` or
`.Dense`. The reduction is the deliverable: each open-language result now follows by application
the moment its L⁺ antecedent arrives.

## Decisions

- **`FrameClass` is written fully qualified** (`FormalSystem.ProofSystem.FrameClass`) rather than
  adding an `open FormalSystem.ProofSystem` line, matching the two landed transfer rows and
  leaving the module's header untouched.
- **The per-formula `Decidable` hypotheses mention `PlusValidIn FrameClass.ZTime (tr hsep φ)`**,
  not the `PlusValidZTime` abbreviation, so `decidable_of_iff`'s hand-supplied first explicit
  argument is in exactly the goal's form. `_` does not work there: `PlusValidIn` does not unfold
  under instance search.
- **Every `Decidable`-valued declaration compiles without `noncomputable`.** The uniform shape
  that would need the keyword is inhabited by `Classical.dec` with no hypothesis at all, and is
  recorded as rejected in the module docstring so it cannot return as a simplification.
- **The completeness rows stay parametric in `Der : PlusFormula → Prop`.** Naming `PlusDerivable`
  would require importing `FormalSystem.PlusLanguage.Derivation` into the host module;
  instantiating `Der` is a one-liner at the point of use.
- **`openDecider` carries a `Paper:` line** although it has no theorem-index row. C15's second
  assertion runs index -> declaration only, so the line is inert, and the plan asked for nine.

  (PlusValidIn FrameClass.ZTime _)` lands; completeness follows by applying

  `Der` at `PlusDerivable fc []` in one line.

  state one consistent thing about L^▷ decidability and completeness: a reduction with
