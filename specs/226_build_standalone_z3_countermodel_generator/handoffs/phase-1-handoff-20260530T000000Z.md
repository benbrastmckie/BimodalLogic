## Key Decisions Made

   - `box` uses `"child"` field (not `"inner"`)
   - `untl`/`snce` use `"event"`/`"guard"` fields (not `"left"`/`"right"`)
   - Until semantics: strict Burgess-style: ∃ s > t, truth(phi, s) ∧ ∀ r ∈ (t,s), truth(psi, r)
