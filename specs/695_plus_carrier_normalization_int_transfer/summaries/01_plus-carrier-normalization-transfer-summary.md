## Decisions

- **A direct seven-case `plusTruthAt_map`, not a `PlusTruthCorr` structure** — the plan's D1,
  carried out unchanged. The induction is paid either way, because `PlusTruthAt` is a native
  recursion on `PlusFormula` and cannot delegate to `Truth.truthAt_of_truthCorr` at any constructor
  but `atom`; and `Semantics/TruthClauses.lean`'s extension contract forbids the generic home such a
  structure would want. The `atom` case reuses `alignedCorr`'s own valuation-agreement field.
- **The plan's D5 "do it" carried out**: the L-side twin `validZTime_iff_validInt` was pinned and
  ledgered alongside the L⁺ one, removing the drift where the mirror was ledgered and the original
  was not.
- **Phase 3 executed before Phase 2** — both sit in wave 2 on disjoint files, and
  `context/contracts/phase-closure.md`'s cheapest-closure-first clause prefers the `local`-tier
  phase over the `full`-tier one. Probed first that no script validates the ledger's `pinned:C2`
  column text, so ledgering before pinning left no transient red.
- **The `stab` case needs no dependent transport**: `(FrameOver.map F e).WorldState` is
  definitionally `F.WorldState`, so the stability clause's state-agreement side condition is an
  ordinary equation in one type, carried by `WorldHistory.comap` plus `aligned_comap`.
