# Literature

Reference papers for the BX completeness proof and algebraic representation theory. Organized by topic; all obtained papers have both PDF and Markdown conversions in this directory.

## Recent Additions

Six papers on algebraic representation theory for Since/Until temporal logics, addressing the Jónsson-Tarski representation for binary modal operators:

| Paper | What it adds | Where to start |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **Venema 1993 (Anti-Axioms)** | The key paper connecting orthodox axiomatizability to BAO representation. Shows that orthodox systems (MP+TG+SUB only, no IRR rule) ensure closure under ultraproducts, which is why Jónsson-Tarski representation works cleanly for S/U algebras. | Section 2 (algebraic preliminaries) for BAO definitions; Theorem 3.5 for the main algebraic result. |
| **Venema 1991 (Dissertation)** | Foundational algebraic treatment of multi-dimensional modal logics with binary operators. Contains representation results for polymodal BAOs including binary temporal operators. | Part I for general BAO theory; Part III for temporal applications. |
| **Goldblatt--Hodkinson--Venema 2003** | Shows limitations of BAO representation: 2^ℵ₀ BAO varieties not generated by complex algebras of any first-order definable class. Important for understanding what representation can and cannot achieve. | Sections 1--3 for the setup; Theorem 4.1 for the main negative result. |
| **Venema 1997 (Atom Structures)** | Studies when a BAO variety is determined by its atom structures (atom-canonicity). Relevant because S/U representation depends on canonical extensions being well-behaved. | Sections 2--3 for Sahlqvist equations and atom structures. |
| **de Rijke--Venema 1995** | Sahlqvist theorem for Boolean algebras with operators. Extends correspondence theory to the algebraic setting, applicable to polymodal (including binary S/U) operators. | Section 3 for the main Sahlqvist result for BAOs. |
| **Venema 2001 (Survey)** | Survey of temporal logic including S/U semantics, axiomatics, decidability, and connections to branching time. Confirms the `U(φ,ψ)` convention: φ=event at witness, ψ=guard at intermediates. | Section 4 (Since and Until) for the S/U extension; Section 3 (Axiomatics) for completeness of base tense logic. |

## Recommended Reading Order

### Track A: Completeness Theory (BX axiom system)

1. **Burgess 1982** -- the original Since/Until axiomatization and completeness proof
2. **Xu 1988** -- generalizes Burgess to non-linear time; defines the axiom system formalized in this project
3. **Verbrugge 2004** -- the constructive step-by-step insertion technique for model building
4. **Venema 1993 (Since and Until)** -- extends to strict and discrete time; "Completeness via Completeness"
5. **Reynolds 1992** -- IRR-free completeness over the reals; uses Doets's theorem
6. **Reynolds 1994** -- axiomatization of U and S over integer time
7. **Thomason 1984** -- standard reference for combining tense and modality
8. **Caleiro--Viganò--Volpe 2013** -- mosaic-based completeness for S5 + tense

### Track B: Algebraic Representation (Jónsson-Tarski for S/U)

1. **Venema 2001 (Survey)** -- overview of temporal logic; establishes context
2. **Venema 1993 (Anti-Axioms)** -- orthodox axioms ↔ BAO closure under ultraproducts
3. **de Rijke--Venema 1995** -- Sahlqvist for BAOs
4. **Venema 1997 (Atom Structures)** -- atom-canonicity and representation limits
5. **Goldblatt--Hodkinson--Venema 2003** -- negative results for BAO representation
6. **Venema 1991 (Dissertation)** -- full treatment of multi-dimensional modal algebras

### Algebraic Representation Theory

Papers on Jónsson-Tarski representation for Boolean algebras with operators (BAOs), directly applicable to Since/Until as binary modal operators. The key insight: S and U are binary normal operators (additive in each argument), so the standard BAO representation theorem applies. Orthodox axiomatizability (no IRR rule) ensures the corresponding variety is closed under ultraproducts, giving clean algebraic behavior.

| File | Citation | Relevance | Quality |

"Derivation Rules as Anti-Axioms in Modal Logic." *JSL* 58(3), 1003--1034. | **HIGHEST** -- the key paper connecting orthodox axiomatizability to BAO representation. Shows that orthodox systems ensure closure under ultraproducts; non-orthodox (IRR-using) systems break this. Explains why Venema 1993b works algebraically. | Good (author preprint) |

### Expressive Completeness Theory

The FO → temporal direction ("separation property"): every monadic first-order sentence over a linear order is equivalent to a temporal formula using {U,S}. Required for Reynolds Theorem 14 (gap elimination).

## Citation Corrections

Prior research reports contained two errors:
- "Burgess 1984" for the Since/Until paper should be **Burgess 1982** (NDJFL). The 1984 reference is the separate handbook chapter "Basic Tense Logic."
- "Reynolds 2003" should be **Reynolds 2001** (JSL 66(3)).
